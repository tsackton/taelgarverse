# Sulqat
:speaker:{ .middle } *(sul-KAHT)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A river in the [Mawakel Peninsula](<../mawar-confederacy/mawakel-peninsula.md>), the [Northwest Coast](<../northwest-coast.md>)  
</div>


The Sulqat is a cold, rushing river, mostly unnavigable, that flows northeast from the [Mawar Mountains](<../mawar-confederacy/mawar-mountains.md>) to the [Slate Sea](<../slate-sea.md>), past the city of [Suhaya](<../mawar-confederacy/suhaya.md>). The salmon runs on the Sulqat are a major source of food and income for the people of the [Mawar Confederacy](<../mawar-confederacy/mawar-confederacy.md>).
