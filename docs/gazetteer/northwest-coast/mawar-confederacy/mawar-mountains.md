# The Mawar Mountains
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A mountain range in the [Mawakel Peninsula](<mawakel-peninsula.md>), the [Northwest Coast](<../northwest-coast.md>)  
</div>


The Mawar Mountains rise between the [Mawakel Peninsula](<mawakel-peninsula.md>) and the [Tawir Forest](<../tawir-forest.md>), cutting the peninsula off from the continental interior. The [Sulqat](<../rivers/sulqat.md>) flows from the range toward the [Slate Sea](<../slate-sea.md>).
