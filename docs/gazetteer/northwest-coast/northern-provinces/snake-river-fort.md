A small Chardonian fort at a [Snake River](<../../major-rivers/chasa-nahadi-watershed/snake-river.md>) crossing east of [Voltara](<voltara/voltara.md>), marking the edge of settled territory before the open plains. Travelers receive warnings here about whatever dangers make be lurking in the east, across the plains.
