# Airion's Secret Lair
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>

A hidden stronghold built by [Airion the Mistspeaker](<../../people/other-humans/airion.md>) at the summit of [Mount Graybane](<mount-graybane.md>), in the [Tawir Forest](<tawir-forest.md>). It is protected by a magical storm that makes ascent of the mountain extremely difficult, and is rumored to hold secret, and potentially dangerous, research or artifacts concerning the elemental planes.
