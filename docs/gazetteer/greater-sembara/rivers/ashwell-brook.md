Ashwell Brook is one of the more prominent small tributaries of the [Wistel](<wistel-enst-watershed/wistel.md>) between [Gowerbourne](<../sembara/heartlands/gowerbourne.md>) and [Wisford](<../sembara/heartlands/wisford.md>).
