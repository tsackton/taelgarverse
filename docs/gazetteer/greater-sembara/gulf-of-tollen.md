# The Gulf of Tollen

The Gulf of Tollen is a gulf within the [Western Gulf](<western-gulf.md>), at the mouth of the [Volta](<rivers/volta-watershed/volta.md>). Its islands contain naval fortifications maintained by [Tollen](<tollen/tollen.md>).
