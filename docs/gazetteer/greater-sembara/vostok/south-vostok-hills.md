# The South Vostok Hills
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A line of hills in [Vostok](<vostok.md>), [Greater Sembara](<../greater-sembara.md>)  
</div>


The South Vostok Hills lie along the [Green Sea](<../../green-sea.md>) coast east of [Tollen](<../tollen/tollen.md>), stretching north toward the [Draukar Hills](<draukar-hills.md>). Their difficult terrain separates the forests of [Vostok](<vostok.md>) from the rest of [Greater Sembara](<../greater-sembara.md>).
