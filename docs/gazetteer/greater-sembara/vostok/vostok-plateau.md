# The Vostok Plateau
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A plateau in [Vostok](<vostok.md>), [Greater Sembara](<../greater-sembara.md>)  
</div>


The Vostok Plateau is a high, cold, dry plateau in [Vostok](<vostok.md>), east of the [Pekul Mountains](<pekul-mountains.md>) and west of the [Svinjo Mountains](<../../northern-green-sea/svinjo-mountains.md>). [Lake Pekul](<lake-pekul.md>) lies on the plateau.
