# The Draukar Hills
:speaker:{ .middle } *(DROW-kar)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A line of hills in [Vostok](<vostok.md>), [Greater Sembara](<../greater-sembara.md>)  
</div>


The Draukar Hills, northwest of Tollen and east of Breva along the [Volta](<../rivers/volta-watershed/volta.md>), are a forested, difficult-to-cross highlands of jagged cliffs, waterfalls, and steep ravines.
