# The Aveil Road
:speaker:{ .middle } *(ah-veh-EEL road)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A road in [Sembara](<../sembara/sembara.md>), [Greater Sembara](<../greater-sembara.md>)

The Aveil Road runs north from the tin-mining town of Sélestat, on the northern side of the [Aveil Ridge](<../sembara/barony-of-aveil/aveil-ridge.md>), to the [Wistel](<../rivers/wistel-enst-watershed/wistel.md>).
