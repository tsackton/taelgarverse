# The Old Veltor Road
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A road in the [Barony of Aveil](<../sembara/barony-of-aveil/barony-of-aveil.md>), [Sembara](<../sembara/sembara.md>), [Greater Sembara](<../greater-sembara.md>)  
</div>


The Old Veltor Road is a maintained road along the [Auberonne](<../rivers/wistel-enst-watershed/auberonne.md>), running from [Rinburg](<../sembara/barony-of-aveil/rinburg.md>) to [Veltor](<../sembara/barony-of-aveil/veltor.md>). The route is ancient, but the road underwent substantial repairs after the [hobgoblin wars](<../../../events/1600s/third-hobgoblin-war-sembara.md>).
