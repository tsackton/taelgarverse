# Aben Garrison

The Aben Garrison is an [Army of Mostreve](<../../../groups/sembaran-army/army-of-mostreve.md>) post near the headwaters of the [Aben](<../rivers/tyrwinghan-watershed/aben.md>) in the [Tyrwinghan Hills](<tyrwinghan-hills.md>). It houses the Red Camp of the Color Guard and serves as a gathering place for [Rangers](<../../../groups/rangers.md>).
