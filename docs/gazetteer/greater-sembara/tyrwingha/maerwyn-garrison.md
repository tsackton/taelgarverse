# The Maerwyn Garrison
:speaker:{ .middle } *(MAIR-win GAIR-ih-sun)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A garrison in the [Maerwyn Mountains](<../maerwyn-mountains.md>), the [Mostreve Hills](<../mostreve-hills.md>), [Greater Sembara](<../greater-sembara.md>)  
</div>


Maerwyn Garrison is an [Army of Mostreve](<../../../groups/sembaran-army/army-of-mostreve.md>) post in the southern [Maerwyn Mountains](<../maerwyn-mountains.md>), guarding the western side of the gap into the [Tyrwinghan Hills](<tyrwinghan-hills.md>). Its stone barracks overlook the approach, sheltered by wooded slopes. It houses roughly a hundred soldiers drawn from Tyrwingha’s three Guard Companies.
