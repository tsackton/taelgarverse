Cyfarthfa Garrison is a post of the [Army of Mostreve](<../../../groups/sembaran-army/army-of-mostreve.md>) in [Tyrwingha](<tyrwingha.md>), north of the [Rhewin](<../rivers/tyrwinghan-watershed/rhewin.md>) and near the [Tyrwinghan Road](<../roads/tyrwinghan-road.md>). It houses around a hundred soldiers.
