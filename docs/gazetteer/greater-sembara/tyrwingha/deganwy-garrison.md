Deganwy Garrison is a post of the [Army of Mostreve](<../../../groups/sembaran-army/army-of-mostreve.md>) in the southern [Tyrwinghan Hills](<tyrwinghan-hills.md>), near the headwaters of the [Llanfen](<../rivers/tyrwinghan-watershed/llanfen.md>). It houses around a hundred soldiers.
