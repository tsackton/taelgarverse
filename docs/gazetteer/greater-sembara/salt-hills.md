# The Salt Hills
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A line of hills in the [Sentinel Range](<../sentinel-range.md>)  
</div>


The Salt Hills are a spur of the [Sentinel Range](<../sentinel-range.md>) separating the [Duchy of Maseau](<duchy-of-maseau/duchy-of-maseau.md>) from the [Aurbez and Andonne region](<../upper-istaros/aurbez-and-the-andonne.md>). Several salt mines in the hills are controlled by the Duke of Maseau, and deposits of low-quality iron are also found there.
