# Enstbain
:speaker:{ .middle } *(ENST-bayn)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A hill in the [Duchy of Wisford](<duchy-of-wisford.md>), [Sembara](<../sembara.md>), [Greater Sembara](<../../greater-sembara.md>)  
</div>


Enstbain is a rounded hill east of [Fellburn](<fellburn.md>), overlooking the [Enst](<../../rivers/wistel-enst-watershed/enst.md>). It rises steeply from the river, with rocky slopes on its river-facing side and gentler, grassy slopes to the north. Sheep graze the northern slopes, among scattered oaks and patches of hawthorn scrub.
