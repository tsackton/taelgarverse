# Gottarbain
:speaker:{ .middle } *(GOT-ar-bayn)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A hill in the [Duchy of Wisford](<duchy-of-wisford.md>), [Sembara](<../sembara.md>), [Greater Sembara](<../../greater-sembara.md>)  
</div>


Gottarbain is a hill northwest of [Fellburn](<fellburn.md>), with low foothills extending toward the town’s walls. Patches of oak woodland break up its grassy slopes, and exposed stone marks its broad summit.
