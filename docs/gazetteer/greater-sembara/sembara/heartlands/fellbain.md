# Fellbain
:speaker:{ .middle } *(FELL-bayn)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A hill in the [Duchy of Wisford](<duchy-of-wisford.md>), [Sembara](<../sembara.md>), [Greater Sembara](<../../greater-sembara.md>)  
</div>


Fellbain is a hill northeast of [Fellburn](<fellburn.md>), crowned by the Oracle of Hope. A well-worn pilgrim path climbs its steep southern slopes to an open, grassy summit with wide views of the surrounding countryside.
