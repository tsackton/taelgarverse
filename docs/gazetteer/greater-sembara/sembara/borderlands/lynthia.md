Lynthia is a growing market town on the southeastern shore of [Lake Rin](<../barony-of-aveil/lake-rin.md>), near the [Enst](<../../rivers/wistel-enst-watershed/enst.md>), in the [Barony of Estrive](<barony-of-estrive.md>). A twice-daily ferry connects it with [Chantefleur](<../barony-of-aveil/chantefleur.md>) on the opposite shore.
