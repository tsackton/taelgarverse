# Enclaut
:speaker:{ .middle } *(ahn-KLOH)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A village in [Lake Rin](<lake-rin.md>), the [Barony of Aveil](<barony-of-aveil.md>), [Sembara](<../sembara.md>), [Greater Sembara](<../../greater-sembara.md>)  
</div>


Enclaut is a village on the shore of [Lake Rin](<lake-rin.md>), near [Rinburg](<rinburg.md>). It serves as a lakeside resort, with numerous elegant houses belonging to wealthy residents of Rinburg.
