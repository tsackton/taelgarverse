# Peydon

A manorial village along the [Auberonne](<../../rivers/wistel-enst-watershed/auberonne.md>) in [Barony of Aveil](<barony-of-aveil.md>), it is about 15 miles north of [Rinburg](<rinburg.md>) over the fields, and 25 miles by road. 

In early DR 1720, Peydon came under the control of the hag from Duskmire called the [Midnight Lady](<../../../../people/fey/midnight-lady.md>) after Emeric Daverre bargained away authority over the village in return for protection from the [Undead Attacks in Sembara](<../../../../events/1700s/1720/01/undead-attacks-in-sembara.md>). The [Heroes of Cleenseau](<../../../../people/pcs/cleenseau/heroes-of-cleenseau.md>) killed her in March, and the village began recovering from her bargains and curses.
