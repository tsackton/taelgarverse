# The Cleenseau Bridge
:speaker:{ .middle } *(Klen-sew BRIJ)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A bridge in [Cleenseau](<cleenseau.md>), the [Manor of Cleenseau](<../manor-of-cleenseau.md>), the [Barony of Aveil](<../../barony-of-aveil.md>), [Sembara](<../../../sembara.md>), [Greater Sembara](<../../../../greater-sembara.md>)  
</div>


Cleenseau Bridge is a massive ancient stone bridge spanning the [Enst](<../../../../rivers/wistel-enst-watershed/enst.md>) at [Cleenseau](<cleenseau.md>). Its ten arches cross 1,200 feet of river, and it is wide enough for eight oxen abreast. Usually attributed to the Drankorians about a thousand years ago, it may be older: some scholars credit its construction to the giants.

The bridge provides the only safe river crossing for many miles and anchors Cleenseau’s importance as a market town and trade crossroads. The [Manor of Cleenseau](<../manor-of-cleenseau.md>) collects a toll for each axle of wagons crossing it.
