# Wickerley
:speaker:{ .middle } *(WICK-er-lee)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A village in the [Duchy of Telham](<duchy-of-telham.md>), [Sembara](<../sembara.md>), [Greater Sembara](<../../greater-sembara.md>)  
</div>


Wickerley is a small, poor village in the [Duchy of Telham](<duchy-of-telham.md>), about two days’ walk north of the [Great South Road](<../../roads/great-south-road.md>). Its barley has a modest reputation for quality, and two brewers supply the surrounding villages.
