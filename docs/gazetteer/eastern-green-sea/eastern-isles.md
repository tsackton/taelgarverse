# The Eastern Isles
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } An archipelago in the [Green Sea](<../green-sea.md>)  
    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } Located in the [Eastern Green Sea](<eastern-green-sea.md>)  
</div>


Halfling sailors in [Tollen](<../greater-sembara/tollen/tollen.md>) and elsewhere sometimes speak of the faraway islands that stretch across the wide eastern edge of the [Green Sea](<../green-sea.md>), separating it from the [Outer Ocean](<../outer-ocean.md>). Called the Eastern Isles, or sometimes the Wandering Isles, they are a strange and diverse collection of lands, said to move slowly at the whims of the ocean, sometimes forming new channels and confounding navigation.
