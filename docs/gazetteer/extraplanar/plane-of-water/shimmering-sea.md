# The Shimmering Sea
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>

The Shimmering Sea is a region of the [Elemental Plane of Water](<../../../cosmology/energy-realms/elemental-plane-of-water.md>), described by planar geographers as a light-filled expanse of warm waters, with extensive floating coral reefs and vast mats of tangled seagrass.
