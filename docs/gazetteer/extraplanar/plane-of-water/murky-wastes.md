# The Murky Wastes
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A region in the [Elemental Plane of Water](<../../../cosmology/energy-realms/elemental-plane-of-water.md>), the [Energy Realms](<../../../cosmology/energy-realms/energy-realms.md>)  
</div>


The Murky Wastes are a region of the [Elemental Plane of Water](<../../../cosmology/energy-realms/elemental-plane-of-water.md>), traditionally catalogued by planar geographers. They are described as waters filled with pockets of silt and floating sediment, mixed with anoxic regions where no life can thrive.
