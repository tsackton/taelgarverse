# The Pillar of Karth
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A spire in the [Elemental Plane of Earth](<../../../cosmology/energy-realms/elemental-plane-of-earth.md>), the [Energy Realms](<../../../cosmology/energy-realms/energy-realms.md>)  
</div>


The Pillar of Karth is a landmark of the [Elemental Plane of Earth](<../../../cosmology/energy-realms/elemental-plane-of-earth.md>). It is a towering spire of impossibly hard stone that vibrates slightly with the resonances of a hidden song of the earth.

The Pillar is rumored to stand at the very center of the plane. Some believe it is a remnant of the plane's creation and contains the raw essence of earth.
