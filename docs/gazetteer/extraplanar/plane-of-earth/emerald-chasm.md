# The Emerald Chasm
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A chasm in the [Elemental Plane of Earth](<../../../cosmology/energy-realms/elemental-plane-of-earth.md>), the [Energy Realms](<../../../cosmology/energy-realms/energy-realms.md>)  
</div>


The Emerald Chasm is a landmark of sorts on the [Elemental Plane of Earth](<../../../cosmology/energy-realms/elemental-plane-of-earth.md>). It is a vast abyss lined with massive emerald crystals, some as large as towers, which glow faintly with green light.

The chasm is associated with the [Dao](<../../../creatures/extraplanar/elementals.md>). The greatest and most powerful dao supposedly have carved homes into the emerald-lined walls of the abyss.
