Melusa is an isolated [Zimka](<../../../../groups/cultures/zimka.md>) village of 400-500 people in Zefya's Realm, a mist-bound place whose cliffs and floating islands resemble the [Great Chasm](<../../../greater-sembara/zimkova/great-chasm.md>). Its inhabitants speak Old Zimkovan and live under the fey lord [Forquarion](<../../../../people/extraplanar-powers/forquarion.md>), whom they call Malquin the Vaqar.

Most of the village consists of stone houses that cluster on a broad cliffside ledge around a waterfall-fed pool teeming with salmon. Rope bridges connect the village to two floating islands: one supports grazing cattle, the other lush fruit trees and crops. A third island holds Forquarion's palace of ice.

## Life and Beliefs

Most of the time, a pale sun hangs almost motionless behind gray clouds. Rarely, it fades, and day turns to night. When this happens, the villagers believe a sacrifice must be sent into [the labyrinth](<zeyfa-s-labyrinth.md>) before daylight can return. Without a normal cycle of day and night, a gong from the palace marks the hours for sleeping and waking.

The villagers say their ancestors were brought here by [Zeyfa](<../../../../gods-and-religions/gods/kestavo/zeyfa.md>) to escape a great tragedy, and regard Malquin as Zeyfa's lieutenant. Rumors of other [Kestavo](<../../../../gods-and-religions/religions/northern-folk-religions/kestavo.md>) survive only as distant, half-remembered stories in [Melusa](<melusa.md>). The elders preach endurance, treating their isolation as punishment for ancestral sins, while [Zahara](<../../../../people/zimkovans/zahara.md>), [Iskander](<../../../../people/zimkovans/iskander.md>), and a small circle of dissidents seek a way out.


## The Chasm Explorers

In [January 1730](<../../../../campaigns/one-shots/into-the-chasm/into-the-chasm-episode-03.md>), [Kalima](<../../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>) led the [Chasm Explorers](<../../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/chasm-explorers.md>) to Melusa after their escape from the labyrinth. Strange cracks had opened in the cliff, but palace guards and a giant ice wolf barred the only apparently safe route back to the Great Chasm.

After bargaining with Forquarion for passage, the explorers later [returned to evacuate roughly thirty-five willing villagers](<../../../../campaigns/one-shots/into-the-chasm/into-the-chasm-episode-06.md>) before the portal pulled away from the cliff. Most villagers stayed behind; their subsequent fate remains unknown.
