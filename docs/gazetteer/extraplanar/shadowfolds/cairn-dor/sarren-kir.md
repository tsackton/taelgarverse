# Sarren Kir

Sarren Kir, the Fort of the Sleepless, is the hill fort and royal residence of [Yeshara](<../../../../people/extraplanar-powers/yeshara.md>) in [Cairn Dor](<cairn-dor.md>). It stands on a rocky hill above the [Wolf Queen's Stillings](<wolf-queen-s-stillings.md>) and serves as the principal stronghold of the [Shemra Azem](<../../../../groups/shemra-azem.md>).

The approach to Sarren Kir passes through dense fog and a trilith marked with wolf motifs. Beyond the trilith the fog gives way abruptly to clear skies and the guarded approaches to the fort. [Yeshara](<../../../../people/extraplanar-powers/yeshara.md>) rules from within the stronghold, while [Shemra Azem](<../../../../groups/shemra-azem.md>) patrols control the surrounding roads and countryside.

The fort also supports Cairn Dor's system of captive dreamers. In DR 1748, human prisoners awaiting confinement as dreamers were held there.
