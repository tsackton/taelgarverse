# Zeyfa's Realm
:speaker:{ .middle } *(ZAY-fahz RELM)*  

Zeyfa’s Realm is an isolated extraplanar realm of mist-bound cliffs, floating islands, and ice, containing the village of [Melusa](<melusa.md>) and [Zeyfa's Labyrinth](<zeyfa-s-labyrinth.md>). The fey lord [Forquarion](<../../../../people/extraplanar-powers/forquarion.md>) rules Melusa from a floating palace of ice.
