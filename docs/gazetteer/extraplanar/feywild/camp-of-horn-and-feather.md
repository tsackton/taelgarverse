# The Camp of Horn and Feather
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A settlement in [Twilight's Edge](<twilight-s-edge.md>), the [Feywild](<../../../cosmology/feywild.md>), [Multiverse](<../../../cosmology/multiverse.md>)  
</div>


The Camp of Horn and Feather is a lightly fortified fey encampment on a ridge at the western edge of [Twilight's Edge](<twilight-s-edge.md>), on the border beside the [Grey Mists](<grey-mists.md>) and near Duskmire. Tents and a bonfire stand behind a wall, where the five fey siblings Thyron, Kyron, Myron, Syron, and Lyron camp with their animal companions.

The siblings keep watch over the border and hunt the surrounding lands. Watchfulness mingles with wild hospitality here: visitors may be challenged for their credentials, then drawn into feasting, tall tales, dances, and extravagant wagers around the bonfire.
