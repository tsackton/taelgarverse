![The Door in the Floor](/taelgarverse/assets/the-door-in-the-floor.webp){align="right"; width="400"}The Dandelion Root Portal is a fey crossing between the [Material Plane](<../../../../cosmology/material-plane.md>) and the [Feywild](<../../../../cosmology/feywild.md>). 

In the [Twilight Kingdom](<twilight-kingdom.md>), a portal in a ruined manor leads to the ruins of [Dandelion House](<../../../greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/dandelion-house.md>) in [Varrow Forest](<../../../greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/varrow-forest.md>).

On the Material Plane, the crossing begins behind a hidden door in a root-covered subcellar beneath [Dandelion House](<../../../greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/dandelion-house.md>). Beyond the door, a narrow tunnel runs through the hollow center of a massive dry root, becoming bone-like before emerging through the ruined manor in the Twilight Kingdom.
