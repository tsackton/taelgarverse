# The 27th House
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A fey house in the [Twilight Kingdom](<twilight-kingdom.md>), the [Feywild](<../../../../cosmology/feywild.md>), [Multiverse](<../../../../cosmology/multiverse.md>)  
</div>


A strange house, north of [Lord Endless Ending's Manor](<lord-endless-ending-s-manor.md>) in the [Twilight Kingdom](<twilight-kingdom.md>). It is reached by a westward footpath branching from the north road, which runs along a narrowing promontory above cliffs that drop into deep darkness, ending not at a visible building but at a single freestanding door.

Lord Endless Ending says the center of the house contains the 27th room, whose walls are made of cold iron older than twilight, which contains a glass key.
