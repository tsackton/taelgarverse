# The Evensong Spire
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A fortress in [Twilight's Grace](<twilight-s-grace.md>), the [Feywild](<../../../../cosmology/feywild.md>), [Multiverse](<../../../../cosmology/multiverse.md>)  
</div>


Evensong Spire is a fortress of immense towers intertwined with the materialized colors of the Sunset Gate at the western edge of [Twilight's Grace](<twilight-s-grace.md>). Sir Caldris, the Watcher of the Evening Song, keeps watch here.

It is one of the grandest entrances to Twililght's Grace in the Feywild, and its enormous doors of embodied flame are said to open only to those [Archfey Ethlenn](<../../../../people/extraplanar-powers/archfey/archfey-ethlenn.md>) welcomes to her realm.
