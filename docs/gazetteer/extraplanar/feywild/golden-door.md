The Golden Door is one of the four fey portals between the [Material Plane](<../../../cosmology/material-plane.md>) and the [Feywild](<../../../cosmology/feywild.md>) cataloged in [The Lore of the Feywild](<../../../things/books/the-lore-of-the-feywild.md>). It is describes it as a door of shining afternoon sunlight that appears only to those whom fortune favors. 

Stories place the Golden Door somewhere along the ancient road following the north bank of the [Yeraad](<../../greater-chardon/rivers/yeraad.md>), but it does not appear in exactly the same place twice. Tales from the villages and towns along that road tell of people lucky at cards following a golden shimmer in the dark and vanishing for a night, or a year, before returning from a court of endless games in an eternal spring afternoon.
