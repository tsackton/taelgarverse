# The Tangled Vale

The Tangled Vale is one of the four fey portals described in [The Lore of the Feywild](<../../../things/books/the-lore-of-the-feywild.md>). It appears as a sheltered pocket of moss and small flowers within a dense brier of tangled thorns, and entering it is said to feel like stepping into a bright painting.

On the [Material Plane](<../../../cosmology/material-plane.md>), the Tangled Vale is located in the [Tawir Forest](<../../northwest-coast/tawir-forest.md>), though its exact place supposedly changes. The crossing connects to Wildrun, a fey realm roughly aligned with the Tawir Forest and the [Mawakel Peninsula](<../../northwest-coast/mawar-confederacy/mawakel-peninsula.md>).
