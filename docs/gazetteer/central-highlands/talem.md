# Talem

Talem is the largest village of the [Bek'eni](<../../groups/cultures/deno-qai-tribes/elderwood-tribes/bek-eni.md>) in the [Elderwood](<elderwood.md>). For years before his death in DR 1748, the green dragon [Grimbaskal](<../../people/other-nonhumans/mezzar.md>) controlled the village while posing as an elven adviser named Mezzar, displacing its Godcaller with a follower of his own.
