# Talem
:speaker:{ .middle } *(TAH-lem)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A village in the [Elderwood](<elderwood.md>), [Ainumarya](<ainumarya.md>), the [Central Highlands](<central-highlands.md>)  
</div>


Talem is the largest village of the [Bek'eni](<../../groups/cultures/deno-qai-tribes/elderwood-tribes/bek-eni.md>) in the [Elderwood](<elderwood.md>). 

For many years, the [Bek'eni](<../../groups/cultures/deno-qai-tribes/elderwood-tribes/bek-eni.md>) lived, unbeknownst to them, under the sway of the green dragon [Grimbaskal](<../../people/other-nonhumans/mezzar.md>), who frequently traveled among them posing as the elven advisor Mezzar. Mezzar had significant sway in Talem, and both the Godcaller and the Chief were loyal to him. This abrutly ended with Mezzar's unmasking and death in DR 1748.
