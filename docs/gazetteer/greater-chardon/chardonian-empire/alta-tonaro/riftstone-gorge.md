# The Riftstone Gorge
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A gorge in the [Chataan Mountains](<../../chataan-mountains.md>), [Greater Chardon](<../../greater-chardon.md>)  
</div>


Riftstone Gorge is a deep, broken canyon system cut into the arid northern slopes of the [Chataan Mountains](<../../chataan-mountains.md>), beneath the towering bulk of [Thundercrown](<thundercrown.md>). It consists of a wide central canyon, flanked by steep cliffs, with narrow slot canyons branching in all directories. Most of the year, this is a land of dust, with only the occasional sparse scrub and hardy trees clinging to shaded canyon bottoms. However, after a rain, the canyon floor comes alive with green and sudden wildflowers. 

The Riftstone Gorge is mineral-rich with chalyte and silver, and strategically important for the [Chardonian Empire](<../chardonian-empire.md>). In the early DR 1700s, it was the site of a major Chardonian defeat, the [Riftstone Gorge Disaster](<../../../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/letters-and-notes/reports-on-chalyte-in-alta-tonaro.md#report-of-the-riftstone-gorge-disaster>), which ultimately led to the end of the chalyte boom in the area, and the gorge became a byword for the dangers of the deep chalyte reserves in the Chataans. Since the defeat of [Dhrukmir](<../../../../people/giants/dhrukmir.md>), the stone giant tyrant who claimed the northern [Chataan Mountains](<../../chataan-mountains.md>) for himself, the newly formed [Imperial Chalyte Mining Company](<../../../../groups/chardonian-organizations/imperial-chalyte-mining-company.md>) has begun to redevelop the rich chalyte veins in this area
