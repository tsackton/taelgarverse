# Fort Varian
:speaker:{ .middle } *(VAH-ree-ahn)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A [Chardonian](<../chardonian-empire.md>) fort in [Portalia](<portalia.md>), the [Chardonian Empire](<../chardonian-empire.md>), [Greater Chardon](<../../greater-chardon.md>)  
</div>


Fort Varian guards the northern mouth of the [Old Chardon Canal](<old-chardon-canal.md>), where the canal meets the [Gulf of Chardon](<../../gulf-of-chardon.md>). It is built on a small island in the marshlands, but has no accessible harbor for merchant ships or travelers.  Services and a safe port may be found nearby at [Nevium](<nevium.md>). 

This fort, a striking castle rising from a prominence among the salt marshes of the northern entrance to the [Old Chardon Canal](<old-chardon-canal.md>), is the most significant of the Chardonian Navy’s fortifications in the area, and serves as the departure point for the canal’s daily southbound convoy.
