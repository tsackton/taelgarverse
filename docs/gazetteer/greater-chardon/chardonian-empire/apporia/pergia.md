# Pergia
:speaker:{ .middle } *(PEHR-jah)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A city in [Portalia](<portalia.md>), the [Chardonian Empire](<../chardonian-empire.md>), [Greater Chardon](<../../greater-chardon.md>)

Pergia is a small coastal city in [Portalia](<portalia.md>), near the southern entrance to the [Old Chardon Canal](<old-chardon-canal.md>) on [Emerald Bay](<emerald-bay.md>). It lies on the broad tidal estuary immediately west of the Old Chardon Canal’s southern entrance.
