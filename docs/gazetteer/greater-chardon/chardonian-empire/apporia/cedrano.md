# Cedrano
:speaker:{ .middle } *(KEH-dran-oh)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A province in the [Chardonian Empire](<../chardonian-empire.md>), [Greater Chardon](<../../greater-chardon.md>)  
</div>


Cedrano is a coastal province in the [Chardonian Empire](<../chardonian-empire.md>), one of the three Apporian Provinces, located on the northeast coast of the [Apporian Peninsula](<apporia.md>), across the [Gulf of Chardon](<../../gulf-of-chardon.md>) from [Chardon](<../chardon/chardon.md>). 

This is a land of rocky cliffs and tiny fishing villages nestled in small harbors. The interior is almost entirely uninhabited, dominated by rough mountainous terrain, with the exception of the [Kedron](<kedron.md>) river valley, which is dotted with some small villages and gives the province its name. The rocky, rough terrain of Cedrano supports minimal agriculture, largely small-scale sustenance farming; its economy is supported by silver mines in the interior, especially along the Kedron, and the rich bounty of its coastal waters.

The only significant city in Cedrano is [Castrella](<castrella.md>), at the mouth of the [Kedron](<kedron.md>) river, though numerous smaller villages dot the cliffs.

Cedrano was largely overrun during the [War of the Dark Rift](<../../../../events/1500s/war-of-the-dark-rift.md>), and [Castrella](<castrella.md>) was besieged but never sacked. It was incorporated into the growing [Chardonian Empire](<../chardonian-empire.md>) shortly after the end of the war, in DR 1599, but granted significant autonomy.
