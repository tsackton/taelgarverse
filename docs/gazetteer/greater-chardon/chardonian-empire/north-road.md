# The North Road
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A road in the [Chardonian Empire](<chardonian-empire.md>), [Greater Chardon](<../greater-chardon.md>)  
</div>


The North Road is a major trade road and the principle highway of the [Coastlands](<coastlands.md>). It enters Chardon through the [northmost gate of the city](<chardon/trade-gate-chardon.md>), and passes through the [Bastion Quarter](<chardon/bastion-quarter.md>) toward the [River Bridge](<chardon/river-bridge-chardon.md>).
