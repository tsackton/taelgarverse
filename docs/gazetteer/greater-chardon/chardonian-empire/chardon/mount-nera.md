# Mount Nera
:speaker:{ .middle } *(mount NEH-rah)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A mountain in [Chardon](<chardon.md>), the [Chardonian Empire](<../chardonian-empire.md>), [Greater Chardon](<../../greater-chardon.md>)  
</div>


Mount Nera is a large, presumably extinct volcano, with a conic summit of basalt and obsidian, rumored to be deeply magical. It sits on the north bank of the [Chasa River](<../../../major-rivers/chasa-nahadi-watershed/chasa.md>), looming over the city of [Chardon](<chardon.md>), and is one of the most prominent landmarks in the city. 

A prominent spur of the mountain runs south from the summit to nearly the course of the [Chasa](<../../../major-rivers/chasa-nahadi-watershed/chasa.md>), and is a major feature of the [North Bank](<north-bank.md>). This spur was extensively fortified during the [Blood Years](<../../../../events/1500s/blood-years.md>), to defend Chardon from hobgoblin attacks, though most of these fortifications are no longer regularly manned.
