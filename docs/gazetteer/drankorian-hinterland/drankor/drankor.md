# Drankor
:speaker:{ .middle } *(DRAN-kor)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A ruined city in the [Desolation of Cha'mutte](<../desolation-of-cha-mutte.md>), the [Drankorian Hinterland](<../drankorian-hinterland.md>)  
</div>




Drankor is a ruined city near the mouth of the [Istaros](<../../major-rivers/istaros-watershed/istaros.md>), once the heart of the [Drankorian Empire](<../../../history/historical-realms/drankorian-empire.md>) and a great center of magic and learning. Devastated by the [First Plague](<../../../events/1000s/1059/first-plague.md>) in DR 1059, its jungle-covered ruins lie within the [Desolation of Cha'mutte](<../desolation-of-cha-mutte.md>), haunted by undead and dangerous remnants of imperial magic.
