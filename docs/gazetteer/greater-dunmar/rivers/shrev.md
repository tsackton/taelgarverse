# Shrev
:speaker:{ .middle } *(SHREV)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A river in the [Nayan Floodplains](<../nayan-floodplains.md>), [Greater Dunmar](<../greater-dunmar.md>)  
</div>


One of the rivers of western [Dunmar](<../realms/dunmar/dunmar.md>), flowing from the [Yuvanti Mountains](<../yuvanti-mountains.md>) to the [Nevos Sea](<../../nevos-and-apporia/nevos-sea.md>). Normally a placid river, during the summer monsoons it regularly overflows its banks, nourishing the dry savanna of the southern Nayan Floodplains. 

For much of the reign of the [Nayan Samraats](<../../../groups/dunmari-dynasties/nayan-dynasty.md>), the Shrev has served as a practical southern boundary of Dunmari influence, and often as a fortified defense against strange monsters and evil magics emerging from the jungles to the south.
