# Yengorza
:speaker:{ .middle } *(yen-GOR-za)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A mountain range in [Ursk](<ursk.md>), the [Northern Green Sea](<../northern-green-sea.md>)  
</div>


Yengorza is a rugged mountain range along the northern border of [Ursk](<ursk.md>), south of the [Ket](<../rivers/ket.md>). Its highlands contain glaciers and give rise to rivers including the [Dalinka](<../rivers/dalinka.md>) and [Beyil](<../rivers/beyil.md>), which flow toward the [Green Sea](<../../green-sea.md>).
