# Midri
:speaker:{ .middle } *(MID-ree)*  

The tanshi of soil, dirt, connections among trees, deep roots and the nutrients stored and shared for the long winters. Particularly appreciated offerings of tubers, mushrooms, and fresh, clean earth. Is said to watch over the long winters and help the community survive until spring.

During the [Great War](<../../../../events/1500s/great-war.md>), Midri and [K'onisati](<k-onisati.md>) fell silent defending a crossing of the [Yemo'te](<../../../../gazetteer/northern-sentinels/yemo-te.md>) from hobgoblin forces, and were lost, becoming part of the [Meswati](<meswati.md>), the Silent Ones in [Deno'qai](<../../../../groups/cultures/deno-qai-tribes/deno-qai.md>) lore. 


In DR 1748, [Delwath](<../../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath.md>), [Aristaea](<../../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/guests/aristaea.md>), and [Iascaire](<../../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/guests/iascaire.md>) [freed Midri](<../../../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/session-notes/session-55-dufr.md>) and the other lost [Meswati](<meswati.md>). Since then, they have returned to the [Ko'zula](<../../../../groups/cultures/deno-qai-tribes/northern-tribes/ko-zula.md>).
