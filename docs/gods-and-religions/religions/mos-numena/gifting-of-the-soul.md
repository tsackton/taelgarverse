# Gifting of the Soul

The Gifting of the Soul is a concept from [Mos Numena](<mos-numena.md>) theology that describes the divine miracle by which a living being receives a soul at birth. It is the counterpart to the [Gathering of the Soul](<gathering-of-the-soul.md>), the miracle by which the soul of the dead is drawn into the [Land of the Dead](<../../../cosmology/land-of-the-dead.md>).
