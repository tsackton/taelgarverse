# Into the Chasm: People, Places, and Organizations

This page collects the cast, places, and groups recorded across the six episodes of [Into the Chasm](<into-the-chasm.md>). Descriptions follow the adventure through its ending.

!!! tip "Campaign home"
	Return to [the landing page](<into-the-chasm.md>), or begin with [Episode 1](<into-the-chasm-episode-01.md>).

## Player Characters

| Character                    | Place in the party                                                                                                                       |

| [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) | A halfling lore-gatherer whose music, magic, and Old Zimkovan help the party understand Kalima and Melusa.                               |

### Other figures

- **[Pakrinek](<../../../people/sembarans/pakrinek.md>)** — A kenku warrior sent to protect Eidrikas's expedition. In [Episode 4](<into-the-chasm-episode-04.md>), Eidrikas reports that the orcs killed Pakrinek.
- **The dead man in the labyrinth** — A sacrifice from Melusa whose answers to Finnan's magic offer the party its first account of the village. [Episode 2](<into-the-chasm-episode-02.md>).
- **The damaged custodian** — A stone-and-metal construct that questions the party's authorization and cannot resume repairs while a threat remains. [Episode 5](<into-the-chasm-episode-05.md>).
- **The older Melusan woman** — An elder who blames the strangers for the disturbance and persuades many listeners to remain. [Episode 6](<into-the-chasm-episode-06.md>).

## Groups and Traditions

- **[Vejo Vaikai](<../../../groups/vejo-vaikai.md>)** — Eidrikas's mystic community, which guards knowledge of the ancient mechanisms that stabilize the chasm's planar vortices. The mechanisms' builders remain unknown.
- **[Kestavo](<../../../gods-and-religions/religions/northern-folk-religions/kestavo.md>)** — The spirits tended by Justas and recalled in Finnan's lore. Their magic unsettles Melusans for whom the Kestavo have become little more than old stories.
- **[Zeyfa](<../../../gods-and-religions/gods/kestavo/zeyfa.md>)** — The feared West Wind associated with the labyrinth and its sacrifices by the villagers of Melusa.
- **[Melua](<../../../gods-and-religions/gods/kestavo/melua.md>)** — An old waterfall kestavo recalled through Finnan's ancestral memories, suggesting a connection with Melusa's distant past. [Episode 2](<into-the-chasm-episode-02.md>).
- **The orcs** — The remnants of the [Ravager of Winter](<../../../people/orcs/ravager-of-winter.md>)'s horde that attacks the machinery, kills Pakrinek, and carries away the keystone. A lightning-shrouded orc and a Thark-marked God Caller are among the enemies the party faces in the lower chamber.
