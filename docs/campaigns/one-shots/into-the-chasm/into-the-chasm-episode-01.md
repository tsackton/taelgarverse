# Into the Chasm - Episode 1

!!! info "Into the Chasm: in which a storm flings five strangers into Zeyfa's maze"
	*Featuring: [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>), [Jrain](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/jrain-fanlish.md>), [Eolo](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/eolo.md>), [Ekko](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/ekko.md>), [Justas](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/justas-rhostrin.md>)*
	*In Taelgar: Jan 25, 1730 DR*
	*On Earth: Monday, November 17, 2025*
	*[Great Chasm](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/great-chasm.md>) and [Zeyfa's Labyrinth](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/zeyfa-s-labyrinth.md>)*

Drawn by omens around the [Great Chasm](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/great-chasm.md>), five adventurers and a terrified Zimkovan woman are torn from the world by an impossible ice storm and awaken on a lone platform in [Zeyfa’s Labyrinth](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/zeyfa-s-labyrinth.md>), where ice wolves, wind-shaped shades, and chasm wisps test their resolve before they choose to leap into a maze that may be the only way out.

## Narrative

For generations, the [Great Chasm](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/great-chasm.md>) has split [Zimkova](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/zimkova.md>), an endless cleft of strange lights, impossible structures, gravity-defying winds, and unseasonable growths. As our adventure begins, months of ominous signs have issued from the [Great Chasm](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/great-chasm.md>), bringing uncertainty and fear to the people of [Zimkova](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/zimkova.md>). When the sky above the chasm turns black for five days, the Vejo Vaikai send an expedition of mystics protected by Kemeko warriors to investigate. They do not return; instead, a [strange woman](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>) speaking Old Zimkovan emerges from the [Great Chasm](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/great-chasm.md>). These events draw five adventurers -- [Jrain](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/jrain-fanlish.md>), [Justas](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/justas-rhostrin.md>), [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>), [Ekko](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/ekko.md>), and [Eolo](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/eolo.md>) -- to the edge of the [Great Chasm](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/great-chasm.md>), where a sudden and unnatural whiteout storm drags them and the Zimkovan woman down [into the Chasm](<into-the-chasm.md>). As they fall through the blowing whiteness and plummet into the unknown, each struggles to hold on to their gear and whatever else they can save.

They awaken on an ice platform, apparently floating in a black void, the false sky of a shrieking white storm overhead. Dim blue light glows from inside the platform, revealing two bridges leading off into darkness and a large frozen statue of a wolf before each bridge. [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) plays a simple melody on his clay pipe, bringing light and clear looks to the platform. [Eolo](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/eolo.md>) snaps to standing with both crossbows drawn, [Ekko](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/ekko.md>) lowers a wing to restrain [Eolo](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/eolo.md>) from panicked action, [Justas](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/justas-rhostrin.md>) rises defensively with axe and shield, and [Jrain](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/jrain-fanlish.md>) regains her poise standing beside the [young Zimkovan woman](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>) she tried to protect during the fall. The woman is dressed in unfamiliar leathers and furs, terrified, and only speaks Old Zimkovan; when [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) sings to calm her, the wind nearly steals his voice, but the spell settles her. As he starts to question the woman, [Eolo](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/eolo.md>) approaches one of the frozen ice statues of a giant wolf.

The wolf begins to move as [Eolo](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/eolo.md>) approaches, gathering itself to pounce. [Eolo](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/eolo.md>) tries a telekinetic shove and fires crossbow bolts, but the guardian gets the better of the exchange, knocking him down, biting into his throat, and leaving an icy shard pulsing in his neck. [Ekko](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/ekko.md>) takes the shape of a polar bear, cracking the other wolf's shell of ice, while [Jrain](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/jrain-fanlish.md>) puts arrows into the first guardian. [Justas](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/justas-rhostrin.md>) steps into the fight with axe, shield, and a conjured _Spiritual Weapon_. Finnan's music and calming magic keeps panic from breaking into unchecked fear. As the battle proceeds, wind-shades sweep in from the darkness: one terrible shriek drops the Zimkovan woman unconscious on the ice. The first wolf shatters, and chasm wisps spill out of it, drawn to light. They snuff Finnan's magical glow, and leave the party fighting by the platform's dim blue light, trying to shove [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) toward the edge. As [Justas](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/justas-rhostrin.md>) and [Eolo](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/eolo.md>) weather the wolves' bites and the party finally defeats the platform guardians, [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) brings the Zimokvan woman back with a healing word.

After the last wisp is gone, quiet falls on the ice platform. With the last of his calming magic, [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) turns back to the woman, asking for information in Old Zimkovan. Looking at the ice bridges, the woman realizes with despair that she is back in [Zeyfa's Labyrinth](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/zeyfa-s-labyrinth.md>), from which no one escapes. Fighting her despair, [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) coaxes old stories from her with music. She speaks of her people, who came generations ago to hide from a cataclysm that changed the earth; they found [Zeyfa](<../../../gods-and-religions/gods/kestavo/zeyfa.md>) already waiting and demanding appeasement. The only way out, she says, shaking with terror, is to proceed deeper into the maze, towards the beast. Panicked, she collapses. Trying to exude calm, [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) summons an illusion of a small hearth, and [Justas](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/justas-rhostrin.md>) searches his charms for one of Romil's tokens, which he makes glow with the light of the [Kestavo](<../../../gods-and-religions/religions/northern-folk-religions/kestavo.md>). Scared but steadied, the woman clutches the glowing token while singing an unfamiliar lullaby.

As the woman steadies herself by the illusory, but calming, hearth, [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) turns from her to the others, introduces himself as [Finnan Oakstride](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>), and retells her tale: before Drankor, when much of the land was [swamp](<../../../history/historical-realms/urtzabala.md>), as The Downfall reshaped the world her people fled into this place, and became trapped in its maze. The strangers then speak of their own journeys that brought them here, before the conversation turns to what the group knows of the chasm, Kemeko, the [Kestavo](<../../../gods-and-religions/religions/northern-folk-religions/kestavo.md>), and [Zeyfa](<../../../gods-and-religions/gods/kestavo/zeyfa.md>). When [Jrain](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/jrain-fanlish.md>) the woman asks what appeases the West Wind, [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) gently draws the her back, calming her confusion over what he is and then receiving the answer: when the sun sets and the new ones are old enough, the West Wind demands that one of her people be sent into its labyrinth as a sacrifice.

The conversation stretches into a short rest. [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) looks for magic, but apart from Justas's shield nothing on the platform or in the false sky gives a clean signature. Meanwhile the cloud presses lower, the wind howls harder, and the woman curls in on herself, saying the beast does not like waiting. The group tests the platform for means of escape, but are rebuffed. An object dropped straight down is blown violently back; even [Eolo](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/eolo.md>) and [Ekko](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/ekko.md>) leaping into the void only to learn that the updraft makes escape by flight impossible. With little choice, the party decides to brave the bridges, the maze, and the beast. When [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) asks, the woman introduces herself as [Kalima](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>), and tells the party that all paths lead to the beast. She only escaped by luck, after finding an earthen tunnel and digging through it. [Justas](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/justas-rhostrin.md>) and [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) argue that she is safer with them than left on the platform alone; she agrees to come if she can stay between them, and the group sets out across the bridge into the snowy maze.

## Combat

**Ice Platform Battle.** A fight erupts when the frozen wolves awaken and escalates through wind-shades and chasm wisps before the party stabilizes [Kalima](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>) and secures the platform.
