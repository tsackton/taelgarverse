# Into the Chasm - Episode 3

!!! info "The Beast and the Palace: in which the explorers defeat a beast, and reach the strange village of Melusa"
	*Featuring: [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>), [Jrain](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/jrain-fanlish.md>), [Eolo](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/eolo.md>), [Ekko](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/ekko.md>), [Justas](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/justas-rhostrin.md>)*

	*On Earth: Thursday, January 8, 2026*
	*[Zeyfa's Labyrinth](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/zeyfa-s-labyrinth.md>) and Melusa*

The party kills the shaggy white beast, allowing them to escape [Zeyfa's Labyrinth](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/zeyfa-s-labyrinth.md>). Climbing to the surface, they reach a cliffside village called Melusa, and shelter with Kalima's family there.

## Timeline

- Jan 25, 1730 DR, afternoon: The party kills the shaggy white beast, escapes [Zeyfa's Labyrinth](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/zeyfa-s-labyrinth.md>), and follows [Kalima](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>) into Melusa.
- Jan 25, 1730 DR, evening: Kalima's family, Nura and Khaled, shelter the party and explain Melusa's strange life of endurance and sacrifice.
- Jan 25, 1730 DR, night: [Ekko](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/ekko.md>) and [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) scout the village with shapeshifting and magic, learning the Ice Palace is on guard and finding strange extraplanar gashes in the wall of the chasm.

## Narrative

Our session begins as the party slides into a chamber, its walls made of black stone, the place [Kalima](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>) has dreaded, the home of Zefya's beast. The creature, a shaggy white monstrosity, rears large and turns to fight, calling up living snow echoes of the party to defend itself. The living snow punishes the party, drawing attacks and forcing the [Chasm Explorers](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/chasm-explorers.md>) to probe for weaknesses while protecting [Kalima](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>), keeping her out of sight. Ekko's summons a pack of spirit animals to aid him, [Justas](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/justas-rhostrin.md>) summons spiritual protectors, and [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) summons a fey spirit, all of which leap into battle. With [Rala](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/rala.md>) and Jrain's attacks, and Eolo's psionic strikes, this is enough to turn the tides, once the party realizes that the snow echoes are vulnerable to each other's attacks. When the final echoes fall, the exposed beast dies under Eolo's psionic volley and the chamber shifts around the group.

As the beast dies, the unnatural chamber softens into earth and granite and exposes a way out of [Zeyfa's Labyrinth](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/zeyfa-s-labyrinth.md>). The party pauses long enough to for [Justas](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/justas-rhostrin.md>) to take the creature's head as a trophy, and to read the changed space for meaning before trusting Kalima's route forward.

The earthen tunnel carries them out onto a cliffside, with view of Melusa above, the village [Kalima](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>) has been trying to reach, as well as a floating ice palace. [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) sends his trickster fey spirit toward the Ice Palace in search of Forquarion the Frostbinder, the rumored lord of the ice palace that rules and protects Melusa.

[Kalima](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>) hurries the party up switchbacks as the path climbs through fog and floating stone along the edge of the [Great Chasm](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/great-chasm.md>). The climb leads to Melusa, a cliffside village draped in fog. Walking quietly through the village trying to remain unnoticed, the party sees five strange extraplanar gashes in a cliff, guarded by a massive ice wolf statue, before [Kalima](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>) leads them quickly to Nura, her sister, and Khaled, her brother-in-law. 

At Nura and Khaled's house, Kalima's family receives the party with caution and urgency while the baby Samir sleeps nearby. [Kalima](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>), taking out Justas's light-enchanted stone, tells her family this is proof that the Kestavo are more than stories.

Safe for the moment out of sight, the party talks to Nura, Khaled, and [Kalima](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>). The conversation winds through the history and struggles of Melusa, which endures because what other choice is there. The fog cannot be crossed, and people do not leave the village, so all that is left is to endure what the place requires. The land itself seems stuck out of time: the sun rarely sets in Melusa, but when it does, it only rises again after a sacrifice is sent into the labyrinth. Even the souls of the dead seem stuck, somehow. The party also learns about the strangeness of Melusa: the villager's ancestors were brought here, rescued from a calamity, by Zefya, and now Zefya rules, and the Kestavo are nothing more than half-remembered tales. Justas' appearance, and Kestavan magic, shakes Nura, Khaled, and [Kalima](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>), forcing them to wonder about the ancient stories of the gods. 

The conservation winds down as a gong rings out across the village, signaling it is time to rest.

After the village settles under the gong, [Ekko](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/ekko.md>) slips out as a gray cat to scout the strange gashes. The mirror-like gashes seem to lead to different places, and are watched by human guards in addition to the ice wolf statue.

[Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) extends the search with Clairvoyance and sees a circular disk in the Ice Palace covered in dense [Sylvan writing](<../../../background/sylvan-writing.md>), illuminated by a pale light. Below it, soldiers prepare to hunt the fey trickster spirit [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>) had sent to investigate the palace. Our session ends as the party hides and tries to rest for the evening.

## Combat

**Zefya's Beast and the Living Snow Echoes.** The party faces the shaggy white beast, Zeftya's monster feared by [Kalima](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>), who is using living snow echoes of the party to protect itself.  The [Chasm Explorers](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/chasm-explorers.md>) keep [Kalima](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>) hidden, break the Living Snow echoes, and kill the beast.
