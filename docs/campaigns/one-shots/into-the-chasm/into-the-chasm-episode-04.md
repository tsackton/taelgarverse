# Into the Chasm - Episode 4

!!! info "Escape from Melusa: in which the party bargains with Malquin for a way home"
	*Featuring: [Finnan](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>), [Jrain](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/jrain-fanlish.md>), [Eolo](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/eolo.md>), [Ekko](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/ekko.md>), [Justas](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/justas-rhostrin.md>)*
	*In Taelgar: Jan 26, 1730 DR to Jan 29, 1730 DR*
	*On Earth: Thursday, January 8, 2026*
	*[Melusa](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/zefya-s-realm/melusa.md>), Ice Palace, and [Great Chasm](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/great-chasm.md>)*

After a tense night in [Melusa](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/zefya-s-realm/melusa.md>), the party bargains with Malquin for passage home; stepping through a strange gash in reality they find [Eidrikas](<../../../people/sembarans/eidrikas.md>) in the true [Great Chasm](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/great-chasm.md>).

## Timeline

- Jan 26, 1730 DR, night: During the so-called night, [Kalima](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>) slips from [Nura](<../../../people/zimkovans/nura.md>) and Khaled's house, as Ice Palace soldiers move outside. The party stays hidden and rests.
- Jan 27, 1730 DR: The party learns more about [Melusa](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/zefya-s-realm/melusa.md>) and the rebels who want to escape, led by [Zahara](<../../../people/zimkovans/zahara.md>). Deciding to bargain directly with Malquin, the party enters the Ice Palace. Malquin veers between fear and authority, but lets the party leave if they return with proof the outside world is real. The [Chasm Explorers](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/chasm-explorers.md>) escape through a strange gash in reality that shows a mirror of Melusa's ledge, without the village.
- Jan 27, 1730 DR - Jan 29, 1730 DR: Stepping through the gash, the party finds themselves in the [Great Chasm](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/great-chasm.md>), a few days later, where they meet [Eidrikas](<../../../people/sembarans/eidrikas.md>), who asks for help to restore the [Great Chasm](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/great-chasm.md>).

## Narrative

Our session begins at night, as the party hides and rests. During the night, [Jrain](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/jrain-fanlish.md>) hears the door open during her watch, and sees [Kalima](<../../../people/sembarans/kalima.md>) slip into the whiteout fog outside, following a guide, while Malquin's soldiers move through the village, and [Samir](<../../../people/zimkovans/samir.md>) lies awake watching the escape.

Morning brings no simple explanation for Kalima's flight. [Samir](<../../../people/zimkovans/samir.md>), still only a baby, answers with memories of falling toward the labyrinth and seeing [Zahara](<../../../people/zimkovans/zahara.md>) above him, then identifies himself as [Taquin](<../../../people/zimkovans/taquin.md>). [Nura](<../../../people/zimkovans/nura.md>) describes Melusa's wrong-soul curse and the brief lives expected for such children. The party discusses what to do next, and decides that, rather than rely on the village elders, they should talk to Zahara's troublemakers, likely a better path to answers.

Leaving [Nura](<../../../people/zimkovans/nura.md>) and Khaled's house, the [Chasm Explorers](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/chasm-explorers.md>) walk through [Melusa](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/zefya-s-realm/melusa.md>) to find [Iskander](<../../../people/zimkovans/iskander.md>), a contact of Zahara's. Once he is convinced they are serious, he tells the party more about the five gashes in the cliff. They appeared a few months ago, but the one apparently safe exit is held by Malquin's forces and guarded by a forty-foot ice wolf. A few have tried to escape, and failed, but most of the villagers are too frightened or resigned to act. He also explains more about Zahara's rebels. There are only a handful of them, he says, and they often withdraw to a hidden refuge on one of the floating islands when the village feels unsafe.

With Iskander's information exhausted, the party decides to act instead of spend more time investigating the situation, and develops a plan to confront Malquin directly. They ask [Iskander](<../../../people/zimkovans/iskander.md>) to warn Zahara's circle, so they are not caught off guard if this creates an opening for escape, and then leave Iskander's for the Ice Palace. Along the way, the party stops to look at the gashes; [Eolo](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/eolo.md>) tests the metal gash and comes away with a sharp, shimmering fragment of metal, and a mangled arm. Reaching the edge of the village, the party announces themselves, drawing enough attention for the bridge to the Ice Palace to form.

Inside the Ice Palace, the party comes face to face with Malquin. The audience is less a formal reception than an encounter with a ruler struggling to hold himself together. Malquin appears and reappears around the chamber, names himself [Malquin the Vaqar](<../../../people/extraplanar-powers/forquarion.md>), and alternates between grand authority, terror of annihilation, and suspicion that the party might be another false thing from the whispers that speak to him. The severed head from the labyrinth unsettles him, but it also helps make the outside feel possible. In the end he bargains: they may pass through the apparently safe mirror-world gash, with his necklace as a token, but he wants proof that what waits beyond is real and not some trick.

Malquin's permission proves real enough for the ice wolf to let them approach, and the party steps through the mirror-world gash. The portal opens onto a sheer cliff in the true [Great Chasm](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/great-chasm.md>). Below them, the party sees strange carved floating platforms in the Chasm, flickering with unstable light. [Eolo](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/eolo.md>) and [Ekko](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/ekko.md>) help the others reach the stones, where they see a person below. Clairvoyance reveals this is [Eidrikas](<../../../people/sembarans/eidrikas.md>), alive but in agony as he uses his body to complete a damaged runic circuit in the stone platform. He tells [Ekko](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/ekko.md>) that [orcs](<../../../creatures/species/orcs.md>) overran the site, killed [Pakrinek](<../../../people/sembarans/pakrinek.md>), and carried a vital stone key down through the platform network, presumably trying to escape. His count of days also reveals that several extra days passed outside while the party was trapped in [Melusa](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/zefya-s-realm/melusa.md>), leaving only a narrow window to recover the stolen piece before the mechanism cracks entirely with unknown consequences.
