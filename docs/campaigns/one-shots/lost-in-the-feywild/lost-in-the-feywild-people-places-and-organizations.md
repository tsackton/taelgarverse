# Lost in the Feywild: People, Places, and Organizations

This page collects the named cast, recurring places, organizations, and books recorded across the seven episodes of [Lost in the Feywild](<lost-in-the-feywild.md>).

!!! tip "Campaign home"
	Return to [the campaign landing page](<lost-in-the-feywild.md>), or begin with [Episode 1](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-01.md>).

## Player Characters

| Character     | Place in the party                                                                                                               |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Kaito Min](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/kaito-min.md>) | An elven performer and opportunist who enters the adventure hungry for treasure and leaves the prison able to imagine happiness. |
| [Edric](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/edric.md>)     | A generous wanderer devoted to [The Night Queen](<../../../gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/mos-numena-pantheon/the-night-queen.md>), whose faith provides the moral compass for the party's decisions.            |
| [Tarek](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/tarek.md>)     | A quick-handed Tollish traveler and salvager whose gathered items often become the clues the party needs.                        |
| [Ayveen](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/ayveen.md>)    | A hopeful Tollish adventurer and musician, whose patience and stories ground the party and help guide the prisoner home.         |
| [Txarro](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro.md>)    | A lizardfolk druid and protector whose animal forms and healing guide the party through the Feywild seeking a way home.          |

### Companion

- [Mossfoot](<../../../people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/mossfoot.md>) — Edric's stalwart but not especially brave pony, left at [Dandelion House](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/dandelion-house.md>) when the party entered the hidden root tunnel. First appears in [Episode 1](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-01.md>).

## Places

| Place | Role in the story | First appearance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Tollen](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/tollen/tollen.md>) | The shared home of the Tollen Misfits and the point from which their road west begins. | [Episode 1](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-01.md>) |
| [Varrow Forest](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/varrow-forest.md>) | The old and increasingly trackless forest the party crosses with Alden to reach Dandelion House. | [Episode 1](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-01.md>) |
| [Dandelion House](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/dandelion-house.md>) | A majestic, decrepit Drankorian manor whose inherited deed and hidden root tunnel draw the party into the adventure. | [Episode 1](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-01.md>) |
| [Feywild](<../../../cosmology/feywild.md>) | The extraplanar realm beyond the root tunnel, where emotion, story, place, and time do not obey mortal expectations. | [Episode 2](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-02.md>) |
| [Twilight Kingdom](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/twilight-kingdom.md>) | A silent realm of rolling hills, unfamiliar stars, distant houses, and a sun fixed just below the horizon. | [Episode 2](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-02.md>) |
| [Lord Endless Ending's Manor](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/lord-endless-ending-s-manor.md>) | A lantern-lit Feywild manor trapped in the repeated final moments of a party. | [Episode 2](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-02.md>) |
| [Twilight Promontory](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/twilight-promontory.md>) | The rocky overlook where a lone door first leads into the 27th House and where the collapsing prison finally leaves the travelers beneath the stars. | [Episode 3](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-03.md>) |
| [27th House](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house.md>) | A vast Drankorian mechanism of shifting numbered rooms built around a cold-iron central prison and used to move its captive through space and time. | [Episode 3](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-03.md>) |

## Books

| Book | Place in the story | First appearance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| *[The Shape of Possibility](<../../../things/books/the-shape-of-possibility.md>)* | Menestheus's metaphysical and mathematical treatise about modeling possible futures and the story-like simultaneity of time. | [Episode 3](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-03.md>) |
| *[Brilliant Numbers](<../../../things/books/brilliant-numbers.md>)* | Menestheus's mathematical treatise explaining the six-digit numbers used by the Arithrimos Lamperum to encode extraplanar weak points. | [Episode 3](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-03.md>) |
| *[The Shape of Discovery](<../../../things/books/the-shape-of-discovery.md>)* | A brief philosophical treatise arguing that curiosity shapes possibility through the same irrational unpredictability as greed and games of chance. | [Episode 5](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-05.md>) |
| *[Allegories for the Initiated](<../../../things/books/allegories-for-the-initiated.md>)* | A collection of fairy tales and riddles whose meanings reveal secrets already known to initiated members of the Arithrimos Lamperum. | [Episode 6](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-06.md>) |
| *[On the Science and Possibilities of Soul Bindery](<../../../things/books/on-the-science-and-possibilities-of-soul-bindery.md>)* | An Arithrimos Lamperum treatise advocating the use of captive souls to power liches, golems, and other devices; Txarro burns the library's copy. | [Episode 6](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-06.md>) |
| *[Journey of the Souls](<../../../things/books/journeys-of-souls.md>)* | An ancient Hkaran religious work about the passage of souls after death, represented in the prison library. | [Episode 6](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-06.md>) |
| *[Journey of the Souls: A Refutation](<../../../things/books/journey-of-the-souls-a-refutation.md>)* | A five-volume refutation shelved immediately after *Journey of the Souls*. | [Episode 6](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-06.md>) |
| *[The Riven Veil](<../../../things/books/the-riven-veil.md>)* | A theological and cosmological treatise by Marcion of Iridel, shelved among a larger collection of his works. | [Episode 6](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-06.md>) |
| *[Zharuk Akhal](<../../../things/books/zharuk-akhal.md>)* | A famous dwarven cosmological treatise on the Far Realms; an empty shelf records that it is out by special dispensation. | [Episode 6](<lost-in-the-feywild-episode-06.md>) |
