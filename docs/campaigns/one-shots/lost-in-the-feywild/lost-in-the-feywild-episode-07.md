Feeling their way forward, they find a small patch of stone surrounded by roots, dead-coral shapes, bone-like growths, and eerie forms resembling faces and hands. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/tarek/">Tarek</a>, still carrying the clock from the workroom in the house, studies it by touch and reads the year as 1351, one year before <a href="/taelgarverse/people/sembarans/lord-hulda/">Hulda</a>&#x27;s deed was apparently written. Kaito&#x27;s *Detect Thoughts* finds a language-using telepathic creature within thirty feet, but its mind comes with a distant chorus of other thoughts. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro/">Txarro</a> calls out to it in Common.</p></div>

### The Child and the Time Loop

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="lost-in-the-feywild-session-7" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>The voice of a lonely child answers. It is explains how it lived and learned and became a prisoner and a trapper of souls. It also tells the story of <a href="/taelgarverse/people/sembarans/lord-hulda/">Hulda</a>, who came once and left the prison into the past. The prisoner admits that it misses its teacher and home but does not know how to choose or let the dreams go.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="intermediate"><p>The voice that answers speaks as a lonely child, saying that it had once lived beside a bottomless well, where its beloved teacher taught it language, stories, and how to grow houses. Mortals exploited its love of dreams, built the cold prison around it, and used it to trap souls. It also reveals that <a href="/taelgarverse/people/sembarans/lord-hulda/">Hulda</a> came here before, but left through the root into the past, where he wrote the deed for his younger self, and died. The prisoner admits that it misses its teacher and home but does not know how to choose or let the dreams go.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>The telepathic voice answers as a child. It once lived contentedly beside a bottomless well, where a patient teacher taught it language, stories, and how to grow houses. When mortal dreamers began to visit, it found their dreams beautiful and kept them. Later visitors bearing cold metal and weapons praised this ability, promised it more dreams, and built a cold playhouse around it so that it could hide the souls they brought. Now, its fungal children sometimes replace the mortals who enter and crawl through a root leading to whatever place and time the prison occupies.</p>
<p>The prisoner explains that <a href="/taelgarverse/people/sembarans/lord-hulda/">Hulda</a> once came here, and followed the root four centuries into the past, where he wrote the deed that would reach his younger self, and died. The prisoner admits that it misses its teacher and home but does not know how to choose or let the dreams go. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/tarek/">Tarek</a> describes the small ornate cup he stole from its teacher&#x27;s house. Something in the prisoner softens as it listens, and remembers.</p></div>

### Stories That Set Them Free

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="lost-in-the-feywild-session-7" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>The five travelers then tell stories that teach the prisoner to let go; it releases a hundred souls, returns through its well to its alien home, and leaves the party on a hillside in the Feywild.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="intermediate"><p>To help it let go, each traveler offers a story: <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/edric/">Edric</a> invokes the divine passage of souls, Kaito confesses the emptiness of collecting dreams, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/tarek/">Tarek</a> recalls giving away a protective ring, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/ayveen/">Ayveen</a> sings that absent lights endure in memory, and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro/">Txarro</a>&#x27;s ancestors recount how their village released him so he could remain himself. The prisoner frees a hundred souls like milkweed seeds, and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-nonhumans/aurae/">Aurae</a> becomes inert as the prison loses its power. The prison collapses as the prisoner&#x27;s incomprehensible alien form emerges, before it becomes a child, descends the low stone well, and leaves the party beneath the twilight sky in the Feywild.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>To help it let go, each traveler offers a story. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/edric/">Edric</a> prays to <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/mos-numena-pantheon/the-night-queen/">the Night Queen</a> and describes birth and death as twin miracles, condemning the theft that denies every soul its promised passage. Kaito speaks privately to the prisoner about his own habit of stealing dreams and souls, admitting that collecting them never made him whole; only here, in darkness and emptiness, has he begun to see himself and feel happy. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/tarek/">Tarek</a> recalls discovering that a stolen ring protected him from fire, then surrendering it to a desperate father so the man could enter a burning building and rescue a child. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/ayveen/">Ayveen</a> sings of stars unseen by day, the sun remembered at night, and the absent moon: lights remain part of memory even when they are no longer present. Finally, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro/">Txarro</a> calls upon his ancestors. Speaking through him, they compare their orderly village to a fish weir that sustains life but traps whatever enters, and confess that they had to open their hands and release <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro/">Txarro</a> before their love destroyed the person they meant to preserve.</p>
<p>The prisoner opens its hands. A hundred captive souls scatter like milkweed seeds toward a home the travelers cannot yet know. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-nonhumans/aurae/">Aurae</a> wakes only long enough to falter and become inert as the soul power sustaining it and the prison disappears. The <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house/">27th House</a> collapses as the prisoner bursts free in an incomprehensible shape: a seven-story dandelion of darkness, bone, roots, mold, tentacles, and familiar faces. Then it is a child again, seated beside a low stone well. It puts a finger to its lips, descends into the well, and leaves the five travelers on an empty hillside in the Feywild, beneath the first stars of evening.</p></div>

## Cast of Characters

- The [prisoner in the 27th Room](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/prisoner-in-the-27th-room.md>): telepathic entity filling the central prison of the [27th House](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house.md>).
- [Aurae](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/aurae.md>) (construct): clockwork research assistant carried in a makeshift backpack.
- [Menestheus](<../../../people/other-humans/menestheus.md>) (he/him, Drankorian human): transformed mushroom man left outside the prison door.
- [Lord Endless Ending](<../../../people/fey/lord-endless-ending.md>) (they/them, fae): the prisoner's teacher, now Lord of the Ended Revel.
- [Hulda](<../../../people/sembarans/lord-hulda.md>) (he/him, Sembaran human): the older and younger selves joined by a time loop.
- [Kallipseis](<../../../people/dwarves/kallipseis.md>) (she/her, Drankorian dwarf): wizard head of [Arithrimos Lamperum](<../../../groups/drankorian-societies/arithrimos-lamperum.md>), fled across the Multiverse.
- [Rakshasa](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/rakshasa.md>) (he/him, tiger-headed mortal): observer in a roadside cottage.
