### Kallipseis the Gardener

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="lost-in-the-feywild-session-4" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>Pusing onward, the party enters a fungal greenhouse and mets <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dwarves/kallipseis/">Kallipseis</a>, who explains the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house/">27th Room</a> is a moving prison-machine; only the glass key in the prison room can open the way out.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="intermediate"><p>Pushing onward, the party enters a fungal greenhouse and meets <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dwarves/kallipseis/">Kallipseis</a>, an ancient dwarf. She explains the house was built around a locked central prison: its rooms rotate through space and time to keep <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-nonhumans/prisoner-in-the-27th-room/">the prisoner</a> from escaping. She also warns that an infection has overtaken parts of the complex, and the only way out is via a glass key that can unlock the front door.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>Continuing through the maze, the party enters a fungal greenhouse and laboratory filled with terraria, strange helpers, and a partly built fungal golem under construction. There they meet <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dwarves/kallipseis/">Kallipseis</a>, an ancient dwarven gardener who claims to have become head of <a href="/taelgarverse/groups/drankorian-societies/arithrimos-lamperum/">Arithrimos Lamperum</a> after <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/thalestria/">Thalestria</a>&#x27;s disappearance or death.</p>
<p><a href="/taelgarverse/people/dwarves/kallipseis/">Kallipseis</a> explains that &quot;the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house/">27th Room</a>&quot; is the name for a massive mechanical-magical structure built around a single locked prison room at the center (the eponymous <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house/">27th Room</a>). <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-nonhumans/prisoner-in-the-27th-room/">The prisoner</a> inside the center room can somehow grow through the walls to freedom if it stays in one place and time. So the structure around it is like an enormous cube able to spin in three dimensions -- to give a latitude, longitude, and a year. Each time someone steps through a door, they are moving the time or place the central room exists in.</p>
<p>She says other rooms became infected, that white fibers colonize flesh and can eventually produce aggressive bodies with mouths in their chests, and that she is building a fungal golem body so she can leave safely and rebuild the order.</p>
<p>She also says listeners claimed there is another door inside the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house/">27th Room</a>, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-nonhumans/prisoner-in-the-27th-room/">the prisoner</a> may be a child kept from growing up or dying, and the glass key can unlock every locked door in the house, including the front door.</p></div>

## Cast of Characters

- [Kallipseis](<../../../people/dwarves/kallipseis.md>) (she/her, Drankorian dwarf): ancient, possibly insane, dwarven wizard, gardener, fungal researcher, and self-described head of [Arithrimos Lamperum](<../../../groups/drankorian-societies/arithrimos-lamperum.md>) after the death of [Thalestria](<../../../people/other-humans/thalestria.md>). Met while in the process of fashioning a fungal golem -- she planned to transfer her soul into it and wear it like a suit. "All progress involves sacrifice.".
- [The Dreamer](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/prisoner-in-the-27th-room.md>) (unknown): voice in the second listener notebook, associated with wanting and [the shape of possibility](<../../../things/books/the-shape-of-possibility.md>).
- [The Child](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/prisoner-in-the-27th-room.md>) (unknown): voice recorded in the first listener journal, possibly connected to [the prisoner](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/prisoner-in-the-27th-room.md>) or house-being.
- [The Prisoner](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/prisoner-in-the-27th-room.md>) (unknown): being locked inside the central [27th Room](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house.md>), described by listeners as possibly a child kept from growing up or dying.
- [Menestheus](<../../../people/other-humans/menestheus.md>) (he/him, Drankorian human): displaced Drankorian mathematician and mapmaker from [Arithrimos Lamperum](<../../../groups/drankorian-societies/arithrimos-lamperum.md>).
- [Lord Endless Ending](<../../../people/fey/lord-endless-ending.md>) (they/them, fae): fey lord tied to the bargain that sends mortal guests toward the [27th Room](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house.md>).
- [Hulda](<../../../people/sembarans/lord-hulda.md>) (he/him, Sembaran human): original employer whose commission brought the party to [Dandelion House](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/dandelion-house.md>).
- [Thalestria](<../../../people/other-humans/thalestria.md>) (she/her, Drankorian human): former head of [Arithrimos Lamperum](<../../../groups/drankorian-societies/arithrimos-lamperum.md>), linked to the bargain and the house's altered purpose.
- [Rakshasa](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/rakshasa.md>) (he/him, tiger-headed mortal): tiger-headed party guest who previously explained the bargain.
