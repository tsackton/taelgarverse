### Fey Bargains

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="lost-in-the-feywild-session-3" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>After regrouping, the party crosses the ballroom to <a href="/taelgarverse/people/fey/lord-endless-ending/">Lord Endless Ending</a>, answering his demand for gifts with improvised offerings. In response to the lord&#x27;s inquiry, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro/">Txarro</a> admits the party is trapped and seeking a way home. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/fey/lord-endless-ending/">Lord Endless Ending</a> speaks of a way home, and explains his predicatment: a mortal wizard bound him to send guests through the neighbor&#x27;s house, until a glass key from the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house/">27th room</a> is broken. After some debate, the party decides to go, realizing that they can only exit if they have already proceeded to the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house/">27th room</a> in their future.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="intermediate"><p>After regrouping, the party crosses the ballroom to <a href="/taelgarverse/people/fey/lord-endless-ending/">Lord Endless Ending</a> and his anxious <a href="/taelgarverse/people/fey/endless-ending-s-butler/">badger butler</a>, answering his demand for gifts with <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro/">Txarro</a>&#x27;s loon call, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/edric/">Edric</a>&#x27;s blessed water, Kaito&#x27;s musical performance, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/tarek/">Tarek</a>&#x27;s mouse-skull trick, and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/ayveen/">Ayveen</a>&#x27;s moonrise sheet music. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/fey/lord-endless-ending/">Lord Endless Ending</a> receives them with poetry before welcoming the mortals into his home.</p>
<p>Speaking to the Lord, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro/">Txarro</a> admits that they are trapped in the Feywild and seeking a way home. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/fey/lord-endless-ending/">Lord Endless Ending</a> says there may be a way home lies through the nearest neighbor&#x27;s house, the same direction as the woman sent by the clock. When pressed, he describes being tricked by a mortal wizard into an agreement to send guests through that house whenever the clock strikes zero, and says the bargain can be undone if a glass key from the cold-iron <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house/">27th room</a> is brought back and broken.</p>
<p>While <a href="/taelgarverse/people/fey/lord-endless-ending/">Lord Endless Ending</a> steps aside, the party argues through the risks: whether retrieving the glass key helps Hulda, whether the neighbor&#x27;s house is really their best exit, and whether any agreement with a fey lord is too dangerous. In the end, they decide to go, but Kaito carefully secures permission to leave toward the neighbor&#x27;s house without being bound to recover the key. The exit proves stranger than intention: the gate will be there only if the party has already gone through the neighbor&#x27;s door in their future.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>The party crosses the ballroom to <a href="/taelgarverse/people/fey/lord-endless-ending/">Lord Endless Ending</a>, an elegant fey lord attended by an anxious <a href="/taelgarverse/people/fey/endless-ending-s-butler/">badger butler</a>. Kaito reads the butler&#x27;s surface thoughts and finds a servant almost overwhelmed by work needed to clean up the household disorder: broken glass, wine stains, interrupted duties, and the frustration of waiting beside his Lord when there is cleaning to do.</p>
<p>The badger presents the group and asks what gifts they have brought. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro/">Txarro</a> offers the haunting sound of a loon from the mortal world, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/edric/">Edric</a> gives a vial of holy water blessed by <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/mos-numena-pantheon/the-night-queen/">the Night Queen</a>, Kaito turns his entrance into a showy musical performance, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/tarek/">Tarek</a> makes a mouse skull vanish as a fragment of death, and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/ayveen/">Ayveen</a> presents her decorated moonrise composition. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/fey/lord-endless-ending/">Lord Endless Ending</a> answers the each gift with couplets of poetry and welcomes the mortals into his home.</p>
<p><a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro/">Txarro</a> tells <a href="/taelgarverse/people/fey/lord-endless-ending/">Lord Endless Ending</a> that the party came through a door that closed behind them and does not know how to leave. The Lord says there is a way home through his nearest neighbor&#x27;s house to the north -- the same direction as the woman who just left. He explains that a mortal wizard tricked him into an agreement he did not intend: he must send mortal guests through that house whenever the magical clock brought by the wizard and her entourage (of whom one still remains) strikes zero. Through various portents, he has learned that the bargain can be undone if a glass key from the lock of the cold-iron door to the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house/">27th Room</a> is brought back to him and broken. Kaito warns him about the bodies on the road, but he answers that his responsibility ends at the gate.</p>
<p>The party argue through the risks: whether retrieving the glass key would help Hulda, whether the neighbor&#x27;s house is still their best way home, whether the wizard can be trusted, and whether agreeing to anything with a Fey Lord is itself the trap.</p>
<p>Kaito then carefully phrases the party&#x27;s departure so that they are headed toward the neighbor&#x27;s house without promising to retrieve the key. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/fey/lord-endless-ending/">Lord Endless Ending</a> says that if the doorway toward the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house/">27th room</a> is truly their destination, his gates are open, and he does not wish to compel them as he was compelled: the gates will open only if, in the future, they have already gone through the other door. The distinction makes the whole exit feel less like a plan and more like a story already bending around them.</p></div>

### On The Way Out

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="lost-in-the-feywild-session-3" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>Before leaving, they return to study the clock, and note the numbers on its face. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/fey/lord-endless-ending/">Lord Endless Ending</a> points them to a sleeping scholar in the study, and they investigate his books about possibility and <a href="/taelgarverse/things/books/brilliant-numbers/">brilliant numbers</a>.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="intermediate"><p>Before they step through, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/edric/">Edric</a> returns to ask about the clock, which the party notes has strange numbers on its face. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/fey/lord-endless-ending/">Lord Endless Ending</a> points them to a sleeping member of the wizard&#x27;s entourage in the library; Kaito reads the man&#x27;s number-dreams while <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/tarek/">Tarek</a> steals his books: <a href="/taelgarverse/things/books/the-shape-of-possibility/">The Shape of Possibility</a> and <a href="/taelgarverse/things/books/brilliant-numbers/">Brilliant Numbers</a>.</p>
<p><a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro/">Txarro</a> tests the river beside the manor and discovers that swimming around the bend brings him back to the same ending party, leaving him deeply unsettled.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>Before leaving, they return to the clock. Inspecting the grandfather clock which resembles the one they saw in <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/dandelion-house/">Dandelion House</a>, they find a dial with Dwarven Reckoning dates on its face which span from the first year of Drankor to their present day (1740), and four dials which (they later learn) show four six-digit prime numbers. The numbers on the face and four dials all change at once, with no discernable order.</p>
<p>In the library, the party finds the sleeping scholar later known as <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/menestheus/">Menestheus</a>. Kaito reads dreams made of numbers, beauty, deadlines, unfamiliar language, and annoyance at his boss, while <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/tarek/">Tarek</a> lifts two books from the scholar&#x27;s satchel. With <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/ayveen/">Ayveen</a>&#x27;s Comprehend Languages, the group reads &quot;<a href="/taelgarverse/things/books/the-shape-of-possibility/">The Shape of Possibility</a>&quot; and &quot;<a href="/taelgarverse/things/books/brilliant-numbers/">Brilliant Numbers</a>&quot;, finding theories about possible futures, story-like time, and number systems that may encode locations or possibilities.</p>
<p>Still looking for any other way out, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro/">Txarro</a> dives into the river beside the manor and swims only to find himself reappearing near where he began.</p></div>

### The Brilliant Number

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="lost-in-the-feywild-session-3" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>The party returns to the gate as the clock chimes again, where they meet the awakened scholar <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/menestheus/">Menestheus</a>. He explains <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/thalestria/">Thalestria</a>&#x27;s order and leads them to the lone door down the road.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="intermediate"><p>The party returns to the gate as the clock strikes zero; they wait near the gate to follow the next sent guest. It turns out to be the newly awakened scholar, who arrives with his lightened satchel, and explains that he came from Drankor and the 27th house with a large group.</p>
<p>The scholar introduces himself as <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/menestheus/">Menestheus</a>, and realizes that Drankor has been gone for centuries and that everyone he knew is dead. He then explains that <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/thalestria/">Thalestria</a> led an order dedicated to maintaining the boundaries between worlds. He describes <a href="/taelgarverse/things/books/brilliant-numbers/">brilliant numbers</a> as a way to hide precise maps of weak places in plain sight, accepts his book back without noticing the theft, and leads the party down the north road to a single door on a cliff-ringed promontory.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>When the clock strikes zero again, the party waits near the gate and discovers that <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/menestheus/">Menestheus</a> is the next mortal sent. He hails them in a language only <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/ayveen/">Ayveen</a> can understand through magic and  when she answers in Old Tollish, he recognizes Northros, touches a translation ring, and begins speaking with them directly.</p>
<p>He says he is from Drankor, came from the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house/">27th House</a> with two or three dozen members of his order, the <a href="/taelgarverse/groups/drankorian-societies/arithrimos-lamperum/">Arithrimos Lamperum</a>, but having a drank at the party, fell asleep, and woke just now to find everyone gone.</p>
<p>The party&#x27;s questions force <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/menestheus/">Menestheus</a> to confront that he is centuries out of time. Drankor has fallen, its language is no longer spoken, and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/thalestria/">Thalestria</a>&#x27;s warnings about the empire&#x27;s end may have been right. He explains that for centuries the <a href="/taelgarverse/groups/drankorian-societies/arithrimos-lamperum/">Arithrimos Lamperum</a> his society closed or repaired the holes between worlds -- between the world, the Feywild, dream, and other realms -- that could not safely stay open. In the waning days of empire, having has presentiment of what was to come, the wizard <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/thalestria/">Thalestria</a>, his master and the head of the order, redirected them toward permanently sealing those breaches which were still open.</p>
<p><a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/menestheus/">Menestheus</a> explains he is a mathematician and geographer. His work is as a kind of map-reader: four six-digit <a href="/taelgarverse/things/books/brilliant-numbers/">brilliant numbers</a> together serve as encoded location keys: with the right factors memorized, they can hide maps of weak points between planes in plain sight. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/tarek/">Tarek</a> secretly slips the lifted book back into his satchel while <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/menestheus/">Menestheus</a> talks.</p>
<p>With <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/menestheus/">Menestheus</a>, the party presses onward to the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house/">27th House</a> whose entry is a simple door in doorframe at the end of a narrow promontory above cliffs that fall into utter darkness below them. As they stand before it, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/fey/lord-endless-ending/">Lord Endless Ending</a> speaks from the manor, a sonnet which either entreats the party to a future path or describes a past path already taken by the party to bring back the key and set him free.</p>
<p>Shadow walks beside you in the gloaming,
	whisp’ring a name that you forgot to fear.
	Bring its key, I’ll break my oath avowing
	you’ll watch this heavy contract disappear.</p>
<p>Where floorboards cry beneath a hidden weight,
	of ancient seed o’ergrown, long-pruned, unmissed:
	spin the rusted wheels of that weary place,
	And keep us all far from its bright abyss.</p>
<p>Unveil the phantom shape I cannot trace,
	the quiet path a turning labyrinth weaves.
	Unlock the stranger prisoned in that place,
	and strip the shroud your waking minds believe.</p>
<p>So take the secret path I cannot see,
	bring back the key to finally set me free.</p>

## Cast of Characters

Session context includes: [north road](<../../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/north-road.md>), westward footpath, cliff-ringed promontory, and lone doorframe.
