The party connects <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dwarves/kallipseis/">Kallipseis</a>&#x27;s description of infected people to <a href="/taelgarverse/people/sembarans/alden/">Alden</a>&#x27;s pale, round appearance, surprising strength, and fear of fire.</p>

Carrying the clock, the party reaches another mechanical listening room.</p></div>

### The Animal Lab Mushroom Men

  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>In the next room, an animal experimentation laboratory, mushroom men attack <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/menestheus/">Menestheus</a>.   <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="intermediate"><p>In the next room, an animal experimentation laboratory, mushroom men attack <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/menestheus/">Menestheus</a>. The party retaliates, seeking to rescue him, but <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/menestheus/">Menestheus</a> is dragged into a mouth-covered chest and nearly killed. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro/">Txarro</a> heals him before <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/edric/">Edric</a> destroys the last creature and the party burns both bodies.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>The next room is an animal experimentation laboratory filled with cages, restraint devices, dead specimens, an infected ant colony, and humanoid remains. As <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/menestheus/">Menestheus</a> approaches the far exit, a hand reaches from a side passage and drags him out of sight.</p>
<p>The party begins to fight among the laboratory tables and specimen containers, with <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/menestheus/">Menestheus</a> already grappled by a mushroom man. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/edric/">Edric</a> rages and charges the captor, Kaito attacks with Dissonant Whispers, and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/tarek/">Tarek</a>&#x27;s approach awakens a second infected corpse that looks almost identical to <a href="/taelgarverse/people/sembarans/alden/">Alden</a>.</p>
<p>The creatures retaliate with elongated fingers and mouths opening across their chests. One briefly catches <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/tarek/">Tarek</a> while the other pulls <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/menestheus/">Menestheus</a> inward, wounding him badly as he falls unconcious. Ayveen&#x27;s Silvery Barbs blunts the attack, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro/">Txarro</a> becomes a tiger, and the party focuses its attacks on the two infected bodies.</p>
<p><a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro/">Txarro</a> restores <a href="/taelgarverse/people/other-humans/menestheus/">Menestheus</a> with Cure Wounds before <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/edric/">Edric</a> crushes the final mushroom man&#x27;s head. Its death releases a cloud of seeds that <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/txarro/">Txarro</a> breathes in. The party burns both bodies, which flare like dry straw.</p></div>

Weakened after raging and trying to smash his way through, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/other-pcs/tollen-misfits/edric/">Edric</a> accepts the failure as a warning from the gods, and the party moves on.</p></div>

## Cast of Characters

- [Kallipseis](<../../../people/dwarves/kallipseis.md>) (she/her, Drankorian dwarf): ancient dwarven gardener, fungal researcher, and self-described head of [Arithrimos Lamperum](<../../../groups/drankorian-societies/arithrimos-lamperum.md>).
- [Menestheus](<../../../people/other-humans/menestheus.md>) (he/him, Drankorian human): displaced Drankorian mathematician and mapmaker from [Arithrimos Lamperum](<../../../groups/drankorian-societies/arithrimos-lamperum.md>), now showing signs of fungal infection.
- [The Prisoner](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/prisoner-in-the-27th-room.md>) (unknown): voice behind the listening windows, connected to the fungal children and aware that Hulda's five friends are approaching.
- mushroom men: fungus-colonized humanoid bodies with elongated hands and multiple mouths in their chests.
- clockwork library figure: human-sized, porcelain-faced construct anchored to a desk in room 26.
- [Lord Endless Ending](<../../../people/fey/lord-endless-ending.md>) (they/them, fae): fey lord who wants the glass key returned or destroyed.
- [Hulda](<../../../people/sembarans/lord-hulda.md>) (he/him, Sembaran human): original employer whom [the prisoner](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/prisoner-in-the-27th-room.md>) identifies as the dragon-killer bringing five friends.
- [Alden](<../../../people/sembarans/alden.md>) (he/him, Sembaran human): Hulda's servant, whose appearance and behavior match signs of fungal infection.
- [Thalestria](<../../../people/other-humans/thalestria.md>) (she/her, Drankorian human): former head of [Arithrimos Lamperum](<../../../groups/drankorian-societies/arithrimos-lamperum.md>) whose sealed office remains inaccessible.
