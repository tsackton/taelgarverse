# Chronicles of Zadkai's Return and the Scouring Wind

After returning from [Cairn Dor](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/cairn-dor/cairn-dor.md>), the [Silver Tempests](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>) discover that several years have passed, and the world has changed....

## Adventure 21: Airion's Secret Lair

### Session 64

The [Silver Tempests](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>) make their way to a refuge and hall of stories that [Brelith](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/brelith.md>) and [Dinia](<../../../people/other-humans/dinia.md>) have established on the western slopes of the [Fiatara Mountains](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/fiamatara-mountains/fiatara-mountains.md>), following the oligarch takeover of [Voltara](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/northern-provinces/voltara/voltara.md>), where they have a relaxing dinner and long discussion with [Brelith](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/brelith.md>) and [Dinia](<../../../people/other-humans/dinia.md>). Over the course of the next couple of days, the group learns of the events of the past few years, while they were asleep in [Cairn Dor](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/cairn-dor/cairn-dor.md>), and learns more about each other as well. [Mabist](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/mabist.md>) and [Blue](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/blue.md>) try lily pad cakes for the first time, to mixed results. 

After a few days of relaxation and restoration, [Silverstorm](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/silverstorm.md>) arrives, his massive wingspan blocking briefly blocking the sun as he flies over the reuge, seeking to speak with the [Silver Tempests](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>). From [Silverstorm](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/silverstorm.md>), the party learns a little of the politics of the [Elemental Plane of Air](<../../../cosmology/energy-realms/elemental-plane-of-air.md>): [Zadkai](<../../../people/other-humans/zadkai.md>)'s master, [Azravan](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/azravan.md>), Duke of the Scouring Wind, leads a faction who believes that all humanity must be erased from the multiverse as punishment for the sins of the wizards who enslaved [djinn](<../../../creatures/extraplanar/djinn.md>). [Orumei](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/orumei.md>), Duke of the Returning Wind, wants to prevent this war. Having learned that [Zadkai](<../../../people/other-humans/zadkai.md>) is seeking some kind of hidden research or magic of [Airion the Mistspeaker](<../../../people/other-humans/airion.md>), hidden in his [secret lair](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/airion-s-secret-lair.md>), [Orumei](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/orumei.md>) has asked [Silverstorm](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/silverstorm.md>) to find what [Zadkai](<../../../people/other-humans/zadkai.md>) is seeking and bring it to them. Silverstorm has asked the party to help, figuring that stealth is an asset and a roc with a three-hundred-foot wingspan will struggle to be stealthy. 

The party agrees to help, and asks Dinia if she knows anything about [Airion's secret lair](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/airion-s-secret-lair.md>). She tells a tale of a mountain in [Tawir Forest](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/tawir-forest.md>), that was called Bald Mountain long ago, but acquired an impenetrable cloud that hovered over the summit, and gained the name [Mount Graybane](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/mount-graybane.md>). This, she says, must be [Airion](<../../../people/other-humans/airion.md>)'s lair. As she finishes her tale, [Brelith](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/brelith.md>) completes a casting of _Legend Lore_, which reveals the same information. 

Departing the [Fiatara Mountains](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/fiamatara-mountains/fiatara-mountains.md>), the [Silver Tempests](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>) fly on [Silverstorm](<../../../people/other-nonhumans/silverstorm.md>)'s back to the edge of [Tawir Forest](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/tawir-forest.md>), where they camp for the night. The next morning, using _Wind Walk_, they travel quickly over the forest to [Mount Graybane](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/mount-graybane.md>), noting that the [floating tower](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/fiamatara-mountains/airion-s-floating-tower.md>) presumably occupied by [Zadkai](<../../../people/other-humans/zadkai.md>) has landed near the mountian. 

Attempting to fly as mist through a supernatural storm, [Adrik](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/adrik.md>) and [Blue](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/blue.md>) are tossed by the wind and slammed into the ground, forced to shift out of mist form. [Adrik](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/adrik.md>) manages to run out of the storm, while [Blue](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/blue.md>) huddles close to the rocks, calling for help with her thaumaturgically-enhanced voice. With no way to see [Blue](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/blue.md>), it is only [Adrik](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/adrik.md>)'s stone sense and storm affinity that hints at her direction and allows the party to regroup. 

Afteward, they proceed into the storm on foot, tied together by ropes. The wind makes walking difficult, and the climb has some challenging moments, but the real difficulty comes as the rain grows and the party must scale a steep boulder, slick and running with water. [Samso](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/samso.md>) uses his [Staff of the Swamplands](<../treasure/staff-of-the-swamplands.md>) to push the water clear of the rock, simplifying the ascent. Even so, the weaker members of the party need help to reach the top: Samso by shapeshifting to a giant lizard, better suited to climbing, and Blue by simply being dragged to the top, battered by the winds. 

Near the summit, the [Silver Tempests](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>) make a final push through lightning and thunder, and finally attempt to dodge loose rocks that come flying down the mountain, dislodged by the storm. Adrik's stone sense give some warning, though ultimately he decides to simply absorb the blow of the rockslides, deflecting them out of the path of the less robust party members below. 

We end as the party suddenly enters the quiet at the top of the mountain, at the eye of the storm. Standing a short distance away is a large domed building, clearly the entrance to something, the door smashed completely open. 

### Session 65

We begin as the [Silver Tempests](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>) enter the domed building, the entrance to [Airion's Secret Lair](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/airion-s-secret-lair.md>). The doors have been smashed open, as if by a hurricane, and the interior of the building -- a large, domed room about 60 feet in diameter -- has clearly been ransacked and destroyed. The only obvious exit is a spiral stairway descending from the center of the room; the party immediately descends below. The descent is strange, and grinding noise seems to shift the staircase somehow, but the party safely arrives in a small, square room below. One wall contains a heavy metal double door, glowing with heat. One wall contains a moving, animated tapestry depicting a sky with clouds. One wall contains a gemstone mosaic depicting a tunnel leading into the wall, ending in a distant point. The final wall has a silver mirror frame, covered in runes, surrounding a vault door with four elemental keyholes. Shattered pieces of the mirror lay on the ground, and between the stairway and the metal door is a stone-rimmed pool of still, cold, water. After some fruitless investigation, long debate, and realizing they probably need to find four elemental keys to enter the vault, the party steps through the tapestry into a vast expanse of sky, distant clouds blowing across the horizon. With [Blue](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/blue.md>) and [Mabist](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/mabist.md>) under the effects of Blue's fly spell, and [Adrik](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/adrik.md>) riding [Samso](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/samso.md>) in giant-bat form, the party flies across the sky, quickly noticing a distant group of humanoids approaching, and also that the exit is nowhere to be seen. 

The humanoids turn out to be a [djinn](<../../../creatures/extraplanar/djinn.md>) leading a small elemental scouting party, who cannot find the exit. Though they are allies of [Zadkai](<../../../people/other-humans/zadkai.md>) and laugh at the party when they admit to being enemies of the [Scouring Wind](<../../../groups/scouring-wind.md>), the djinn agrees to give the party two diamond gemhearts -- but not the elemental air key -- in exchange for help finding the exit. The party goes along with this initially, even though they also have no idea how to exit; eventually [Samso](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/samso.md>) notices a glitch in the distant clouds that may be a clue, but doesn't reveal this to the djinn. Soon, the party just decides to attack before their fly spell ends. The battle is quick, as Mabist strikes the djinn with a dramatic blow enhanced with eldritch force, and Adrik manages to jump to the djinn and strike it several times while clinging to its back despite its attempt to flee. With the djinn dead, Adrik falls as well, grabbing the diamond gemhearts and the air elemental key, and is chased by Samso who gathers him. 

The party then turns to searching for the exit. The glitch is a clue, but it takes a while for the [Silver Tempests](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>) to release that the door, camouflaged by an illusion, is moving away from them at a constant rate. Once Blue and Adrik teleport some distant away, they realize the glitch is in between them and the rest of the party, and quickly are able to close in on it and grab the door frame, passing through back into the vault room.

The air gemhearts contain an essence of elemental air that allows not only flight, but allows one breath to last for an hour, opening up access to the deep pool of water. With [Samso](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/samso.md>) able to hold his breath for some time, and [Mabist](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/mabist.md>) able to breath water, the two air gemhearts let Samso and [Blue](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/blue.md>) also descent safely through the cold water column. Sinking, the party passes through several hundred feet of increasingly cold and frigid water, until they reach a frozen ice cap at the bottom. An ice cave is visible on the other side. [Adrik](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/adrik.md>) and [Mabist](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/mabist.md>) attempt to shatter the ice, resorting to [Thunderbrand](<../treasure/thunderbrand.md>) and _Booming Blade_, which draws attention. Two ice sharks, and a larger, tentacled ice creature, come to investigate, seeking warm-blooded food, and attack. Samso shifts into an orca, and Mabist finishes shattering the ice, which then leads to the [Silver Tempests](<../../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>) and the ice sharks, but not the larger creature, tumbling into the cave below, which fills with water, though the ice plug quickly begins to refreeze. We end as the sharks are driven off, dead or badly wounded, and the party swims through freezing water of the ice cave to a tunnel, leading slightly up, where they can stagger, wet and cold, out of the water onto solid ice.
