The Rings of Elemental Protection are a set of five gold rings, each set with a different gemstone: ruby, sapphire, emerald, topaz, or opal.


The [Dunmar Fellowship](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship.md>) recovered the ruby, sapphire, and emerald rings from [Apollyon's Tower](<../../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/apollyon-s-tower.md>). These three rings have braided gold bands.




## Mechanics

Each ring grants resistance to a damage type determined by its gemstone:

- Ruby: fire
- Sapphire: cold
- Emerald: acid
- Topaz: lightning
- Opal: thunder

A wearer can attune to any two of these rings at the same time.
