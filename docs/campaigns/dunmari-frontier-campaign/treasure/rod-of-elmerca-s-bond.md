The Rod of Elmerca's Bond is a silver magical rod decorated with a spiraling motif and holding a pearly orb.


The [Dunmar Fellowship](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship.md>) recovered the rod from a concealed chest near the forge in [Apollyon's Tower](<../../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/apollyon-s-tower.md>). It is carried by [Delwath](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath.md>).


## Mechanics

The rod functions as a +1 Rod of the Pact Keeper. Once per day, while casting a spell, its wielder can briefly see into the future and learn whether the target will succeed or fail on its saving throw. Immunity counts as success.
