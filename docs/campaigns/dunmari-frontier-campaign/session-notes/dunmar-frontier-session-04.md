# Dunmari Frontier - Session 4

!!! info "Shakun's Wane: in which the party learns dire secrets about Karawa"
	*Featuring: [Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>), [Wellby](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby.md>), [Delwath](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath.md>), and [Seeker](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker.md>)*
	*In Taelgar: Mar 26, 1748 DR to Mar 29, 1748 DR*
	*On Earth: Thursday, July 9, 2020*
	*[Dunmari Fort (Gomat)](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/dunmari-fort-gomat.md>), [Karawa Desert](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/karawa-desert.md>), and [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>)*

Returning to [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>) with treasure from the fort and many open questions, the party investigates the trail of the petrified explorer, [Arcus](<../../../people/chardonians/arcus.md>); encounters a cursed halfling; and finally speaks at length with [Candrosa](<../../../people/dunmari/candrosa.md>), who tells them that Shakun's ancient protection has failed.

## Timeline



- Mar 26, 1748 DR: The party leaves the [Dunmari fort](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/dunmari-fort-gomat.md>) and heads west across the [Daraar Canyon](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/daraar-canyon.md>) towards [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>).
- Mar 27, 1748 DR: The party continues traveling west toward [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>).
- Mar 28, 1748 DR: The party continues west towards [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>) through the [Karawa Desert](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/karawa-desert.md>).
- Mar 29, 1748 DR: The party reaches [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>) in the late afternoon, now swollen with people for the [Festival of Rebirth](<../../../gods-and-religions/holidays-and-festivals/dunmari-festivals/festival-of-rebirth.md>), after an uneventful journey. They report to Elder [Kisa](<../../../people/dunmari/kisa.md>) about the fort, and hear about the [Summer of Red Storms](<../../../events/1700s/1709/summer-of-red-storms.md>). Then they speak to [Ikram](<../../../people/dunmari/ikram.md>), who identifies the petrified explorer as [Arcus](<../../../people/chardonians/arcus.md>) and directs them to the smith, [Jasu](<../../../people/dunmari/jasu.md>). They speak with [Jasu](<../../../people/dunmari/jasu.md>) as evening approaches, and then meet the [Charmhearts](<../../../groups/halfling-families/charmhearts.md>) and overhear Ander Charmheart's cursed mutterings. After nightfall, they speak with [Candrosa](<../../../people/dunmari/candrosa.md>) and learn about [Shakun's Protection](<../../../gods-and-religions/religions/five-siblings/shakun-s-protection.md>).

### Following Arcus's Trail

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="dunmari-frontier-session-4" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>There, the party reports their success to Elder <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/kisa/">Kisa</a>, who recalls the <a href="/taelgarverse/events/1700s/1709/summer-of-red-storms/">Summer of Red Storms</a> and directs them to <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a> and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> for more information. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a> identifies the <a href="/taelgarverse/people/chardonians/arcus/">petrified explorer</a> as <a href="/taelgarverse/people/chardonians/arcus/">Arcus</a>, and, over dinner at <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/ikram-s/">Ikram&#x27;s</a>, the smith <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/jasu/">Jasu</a> describes <a href="/taelgarverse/people/chardonians/arcus/">Arcus</a>&#x27;s interest in <a href="/taelgarverse/events/1500s/great-war/">Great War</a> artifacts.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>Right away, the party finds Elder <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/kisa/">Kisa</a> and reports to her that they seem to have destroyed the source of the disturbances. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/kisa/">Kisa</a> has been quite worried, as news of several more attacks has arrived with travelers returning for the festival. Unable to explain the staff and taken aback by its Draconic inscription, she directs them to <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a>. Worried, she talks about the <a href="/taelgarverse/events/1700s/1709/summer-of-red-storms/">Summer of Red Storms</a>, a time about forty years ago when some half-human, half-hyena creatures rampaged across the border for two months before they were stopped. She also tells them the <a href="/taelgarverse/people/chardonians/arcus/">petrified explorer</a> depicted in <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a>&#x27;s sketch is not someone from <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a>, directing them to <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a> if they want to learn more. As they leave, she tells them that the town will pay for their stay at <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/ikram-s/">Ikram&#x27;s</a> in gratitude for the aid they provided.</p>
<p>Heading to the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/ikram-s/">caravanserai</a>, the party meets <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a>, who is eager to sit, talk, share stories, and meet new people. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a> tells them a bit about the <a href="/taelgarverse/people/chardonians/arcus/">petrified explorer</a>, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/chardonians/arcus/">Arcus</a>. He passed through town maybe a month ago, keeping to himself. He also wasn’t the only stranger in town: several other quiet types passed through over the past few months. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a> recalls that <a href="/taelgarverse/people/chardonians/arcus/">Arcus</a> spoke with a foreign accent and paid with unusual coin that <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> recognizes from the description as Chardonian. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/chardonians/arcus/">Arcus</a> was interested in artifacts, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a> says, and asked who might be able to identify them. He was directed to <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/jasu/">Jasu</a>, the smith. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a> also identifies <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/alesh/">Alesh</a> as the local expert on Dunmari history and artifacts recovered along the road, and reports news of folk attacked by rampaging beasts. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/akan/">Akan</a>, who the party met at the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/gomat/">Gomat</a> oasis, has made it safely to town with his surviving family. Another herder, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/devana/">Devana</a>, and his family were attacked by axebeaks and lost several family members.</p>
<p>After a bit more conversation with <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a> about lighter subjects, including the upcoming axebeak riding competition before the <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/holidays-and-festivals/dunmari-festivals/festival-of-rebirth/">Festival of Rebirth</a>, the party heads for <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/jasu/">Jasu</a>’s shop to ask about <a href="/taelgarverse/people/chardonians/arcus/">Arcus</a>. Reaching him just as he is closing his forge for the evening, they return to <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/ikram-s/">Ikram&#x27;s</a> with <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/jasu/">Jasu</a> for a light dinner. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/jasu/">Jasu</a> tells them <a href="/taelgarverse/people/chardonians/arcus/">Arcus</a> was asking about artifacts, especially anything from the <a href="/taelgarverse/events/1500s/great-war/">Great War</a>. He left a couple of silvered spearpoints behind and asked <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/jasu/">Jasu</a> to haft them so they could be used. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/chardonians/arcus/">Arcus</a> apparently expected to be back in town within a few weeks, and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/jasu/">Jasu</a> finds it strange that he has not returned. The silvered spearpoints were odd, too, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/jasu/">Jasu</a> says: hard to work with and resistant to forging.</p></div>

A disturbed <a href="/taelgarverse/people/halflings/ander-charmheart/">Ander Charmheart</a> emerges from his wagon and speaks joyfully of a master who will consume him and the world.</p></div>

<p>Leaving <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/jasu/">Jasu</a> to his dinner, the party heads to meet a group of halflings -- marked by their colorful wagons -- in town for the <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/holidays-and-festivals/dunmari-festivals/festival-of-rebirth/">Festival of Rebirth</a> and its associated markets. The matriarch of the group introduces herself as <a href="/taelgarverse/people/halflings/bree-charmheart/">Bree Charmheart</a> and welcomes the party to sit and chat for a bit. After some small talk with <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> about their shared Sembaran origins, she strangely grows chilly and unwelcoming. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> asks her about her sick companion, and she responds abruptly, acting as though they have overstayed their welcome. Just before the party leaves, a <a href="/taelgarverse/people/halflings/ander-charmheart/">disheveled halfling</a> staggers out of one of the wagons, looking dazed but with an incongruous look of joy on his face. As the party hurries away, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> informs the party that this <a href="/taelgarverse/people/halflings/ander-charmheart/">disheveled halfling</a> hears an unceasing voice that speaks to him: “The master is waiting. You&#x27;ll find joy with the master. The master wants you to come and join us.&quot; Over and over and over. When <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> asked him to name the master, he only said: &quot;The master is my master. The master will consume the world and bring us joy.&quot;</p></div>

### Candrosa's Confession

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="dunmari-frontier-session-4" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>After leaving the halflings, the party splits for the evening. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath/">Delwath</a>, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a>, and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> head to the oasis, while <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> returns to <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/ikram-s/">Ikram&#x27;s</a> to talk and drink. Well past sunset, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> emerges and summons the group together, then reveals that <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/dunmari-pantheon/shakun/">Shakun</a>&#x27;s ancient border protections are failing and begs the party to help defend <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/dunmar/">Dunmar</a>.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>After leaving the halflings, the party splits for the evening. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath/">Delwath</a> and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> settle beside the oasis to meditate and seek insight from <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/dunmari-pantheon/shakun/">Shakun</a>, while <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> keeps watch and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> returns to <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/ikram-s/">Ikram&#x27;s</a> to talk and drink late into the night. Around eleven, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> interrupts the vigil with news that demands an immediate conversation. He sends <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> to retrieve <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a>; <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a>&#x27;s halfling sobering ritual with three eggs and a stick ends in a volley of thrown eggs, but succeeds in drawing <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> away.</p>
<p>As the party assembles outside the temple, an armored rider, later identified as <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/havdar/">Havdar</a>, gallops toward the encampments on the outskirts of town. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> then leads the party into a private side chamber in the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/temple-of-shakun/">Temple of Shakun</a>. As they begin to talk, it is immediately clear that <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> is nervous and hiding something. When asked directly, he seems almost relieved, but also uncertain whether he can trust the party and how much to reveal. After hearing the stories of why the party is there, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> casts Zone of Truth and asks whether they are there to aid or hinder <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a>. Reassured, he opens up, telling the party about the magic of <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/religions/five-siblings/shakun-s-protection/">Shakun&#x27;s Protection</a> that guards the borders of <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/dunmar/">Dunmar</a>, creating illusions and misdirection that prevent enemies from finding their way in the desert. This magic has kept <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/dunmar/">Dunmar</a> safe for many generations, but now it seems to be gone, as if <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/dunmari-pantheon/shakun/">Shakun</a> herself has abandoned the Dunmari. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/havdar/">Havdar</a> has returned from fighting half-human, half-hyena attackers to the south. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> also reports an unending lightning storm to the north. The news of Ander&#x27;s apparent madness only deepens <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a>&#x27;s fear. He is terrified and asks for their aid, saying “If <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/dunmari-pantheon/shakun/">Shakun</a> cannot protect us, we will have to rely on more mundane forces.”</p>
<p>He asks them to keep the failure of <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/religions/five-siblings/shakun-s-protection/">Shakun&#x27;s protection</a> secret through the <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/holidays-and-festivals/dunmari-festivals/festival-of-rebirth/">Festival of Rebirth</a> and to speak with <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/havdar/">Havdar</a> in the morning. Our session ends as <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> gives each member of the <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship/">Dunmar Fellowship</a> a vial of healing mud as thanks and a token of trust, and the party heads to bed.</p>

## Treasure and Things

- Recovered Dunmari temple relics: Portable chalices, amulets, candlesticks, and other sacred objects carried away from the fort, returned to Suma in exchange for a Scroll of Cure Wounds.
- Sketch of [Arcus](<../../../people/chardonians/arcus.md>): Seeker's detailed drawing of the petrified explorer, used to identify him in [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>).
- Wellby's map: A map of his travels around [Dunmar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/dunmar.md>), compared with Seeker's observations of [ley lines](<../../../cosmology/planar-concepts/ley-lines.md>).
- Healing mud vials: Four small vials of perpetually moist healing mud, one given to each party member.
- Arcus's silvered spearpoints: Two apparently new spearpoints that resist Jasu's attempts to heat, reforge, or haft them, left with [Jasu](<../../../people/dunmari/jasu.md>) to be hafted but never claimed.
