# Dunmari Frontier - Session 3

!!! info "Curses and Cockatrices: in which an old fort reveals its secrets"
	*Featuring: [Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>), [Wellby](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby.md>), [Delwath](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath.md>), and [Seeker](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker.md>)*
	*In Taelgar: Mar 25, 1748 DR to Mar 26, 1748 DR*
	*On Earth: Thursday, July 2, 2020*
	*[Dunmari Fort (Gomat)](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/dunmari-fort-gomat.md>) and [Nashtkar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/nashtkar.md>)*

The [Dunmar Fellowship](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship.md>) explores a ruined Dunmari fort, where they uncover and destroy the source of the unnatural aggression afflicting animals in the [Nashtkar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/nashtkar.md>).

## Timeline



- Mar 25, 1748 DR, afternoon: The party explores the [ruined fort](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/dunmari-fort-gomat.md>) and kills an ankheg that ambushes [Wellby](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby.md>). Searching the caves below, they find a [petrified explorer](<../../../people/chardonians/arcus.md>), and defeat the undead bearer of a malignant black iron staff.
- Mar 25, 1748 DR, evening: The party shatters the staff's obsidian crown, ending its aura of rage, and then makes camp for the night in the temple.

## People and Places

### NPCs

- [Arcus](<../../../people/chardonians/arcus.md>) (*AR-koos*; he/him, Chardonian human): petrified explorer in the cockatrice caves beneath the fort.

### Locations

- [Dunmari Fort (Gomat)](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/dunmari-fort-gomat.md>) (fort in [Nashtkar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/nashtkar.md>), [Greater Dunmar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/greater-dunmar.md>)): a ruined Dunmari fort whose preserved temple and underground caverns held Dunmari relics, petrified creatures, and the source of the animals' unnatural rage. Session context includes: abandoned campsite and causeway, ruined headquarters, temple of the [Five Siblings](<../../../gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/dunmari-pantheon/dunmari-pantheon.md>), gate towers, ankheg tunnels, cockatrice nursery, dry cistern channel, and southern cavern.
- [Nashtkar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/nashtkar.md>) (region in [Greater Dunmar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/greater-dunmar.md>)): desert surrounding the ruined fort, noticeably calmer after the obsidian's influence ended. Session context includes: temple at Dunmari Fort.

### Entering the Fort

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="dunmari-frontier-session-3" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>We begin on the causeway outside the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/dunmari-fort-gomat/">Dunmari fort</a>, where the party uncovers an abandoned campsite and tracks leading into the fort. Following the trail, the party survives an ankheg ambush and discovers dense animal tracks in the caves below, but they decide to find a safe place to rest before exploring further.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>We begin on the causeway outside the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/dunmari-fort-gomat/">Dunmari fort</a>. Searching the scene, the <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship/">Dunmar Fellowship</a> finds a campsite in the foundations of the outbuildings outside the fort. The camp itself is several days to several weeks old and has clearly been disturbed by animals (signs of scattered food -- salt pork and dried fish and the like that had been partially eaten by animals). Whoever made the camp had left most of their stuff, except weapons and water, and gone somewhere.</p>
<p>The camper&#x27;s trail runs from the disturbed burial mound, across the causeway, and into the ruins, and the party decides to follow it into the fort. Inside, only three buildings still have walls: a central headquarters building, a five-sided temple (which retains a metal roof), and two towers guarding the entryway. In addition, there is an empty cistern, cracked at the bottom.</p>
<p>The sounds of a large clacking and chittering creature from the central building draw the party in. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> begins sneaking quietly towards the walls to investigate, as the sounds go quiet momentarily. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath/">Delwath</a> suddenly remembers tales of a large desert monstrosity that hunts by tunneling underground and ambushing prey, but before he can say anything, a giant insect, an ankheg, a cross between a centipede and a praying mantis, bursts from underground and grabs <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> in its mandibles. Despite a few tense moments as <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> is in danger of being eaten, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a>’s quick action to whack the creature across the mandibles frees <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a>, and the ankheg is dispatched by <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath/">Delwath</a>’s longsword blow after being knocked prone by <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> excavating the loose earth under its back legs.</p>
<p>Inside the creature&#x27;s lair, the party finds the remains of a lion and a natural passage marked by tracks of many animals, including lions, hyenas, lizards, moving in both directions. Wary, the group postpones descent and seeks shelter for a short rest.</p></div>

## Combat

**Ankheg Ambush.** An ankheg seizes [Wellby](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby.md>) in the courtyard; the party frees him, kills it, and traces its burrow beneath the fort headquarters.

**Guardians of the Staff.** Three skeletons and an undead hobgoblin warlock attack the party; the party destroys them and captures the still-active staff.

## Treasure and Things

- [Aagir's Everlight](<../../../things/magic-items/aagir-s-everlight.md>) (Dunmari magic wonderous item): silver spearpoint statuette with an unconsuming flame, later used as a wedge against the obsidian, kept by [Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>).
- Dunmari temple relics: including chalices, candlesticks, amulets, and other sacred objects, gathered by the party.
- [Horn of Silent Alarm (Dunmari)](<../../../things/magic-items/horn-of-silent-alarm-dunmari.md>) (common Dunmari magic horn): magical ram's horn decorated with silver filigree in a running-horse design, kept by [Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>).
- [Floating Disc Coin](<../treasure/floating-disc-coin.md>) (magic coin): heavy old Chardonian coin that unfolds into a hovering disc when flipped, kept by [Seeker](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker.md>).
- Black Iron Staff: weapon crowned with malignant obsidian that radiated rage even after its bearer died, carved with a quote in Draconic: "I bind you to the black wind. I bind you to my flame. I bind you in my power until the sun fails and the moon is dead." Kept by the party.
- Cha'mutte symbol necklace: silver wing-and-flame necklace with a black pearl, tentatively associated with Cha'mutte's armies, kept by the party.
- Obsidian shards: mundane fragments collected after the staff's magical crown was shattered.
