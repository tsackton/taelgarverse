# Dunmari Frontier - Session 6

!!! info "The Festival of Rebirth: in which Karawa’s festival becomes a fight for survival"
	*Featuring: [Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>), [Wellby](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby.md>), [Delwath](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath.md>), [Seeker](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker.md>), and [Riswynn](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/riswynn.md>)*

	*On Earth: Thursday, August 6, 2020*
	*[Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>)*

## Timeline


- Apr 04, 1748 DR, afternoon: After the council meeting, the party prepares to set out for [Raven's Hold](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold.md>).
- Apr 04, 1748 DR, evening: [Alesh](<../../../people/dunmari/alesh.md>) leaves to scout the borders with two companions; the party plans defenses for the [Temple of Shakun](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/temple-of-shakun.md>).
- Apr 05, 1748 DR: [Seeker](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker.md>) works to fortify the [Temple of Shakun](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/temple-of-shakun.md>). [Riswynn](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/riswynn.md>) joins the party. The party and all of [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>) attends the burning of Jeevali's shrine to begin the [Festival of Rebirth](<../../../gods-and-religions/holidays-and-festivals/dunmari-festivals/festival-of-rebirth.md>).
- Apr 06, 1748 DR: During the procession celebrating Jeevali's rebirth, gnolls and monsters attack [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>); the party comes to the town's defense.
- Apr 07, 1748 DR, morning: The Dunmari begin to evacuate west, while the party follows gnoll tracks north towards [Raven's Hold](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold.md>).

## People and Places

### NPCs

- [Callie Charmheart](<../../../people/halflings/callie-charmheart.md>) (she/her, halfling): Halfling trader and Ander's older sister, seeking help for his affliction.
- [Ander Charmheart](<../../../people/halflings/ander-charmheart.md>) (he/him, halfling): Halfling caravan scout afflicted at [Raven's Hold](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold.md>), who tries to be devoured by the attackers.
- [Bree Charmheart](<../../../people/halflings/bree-charmheart.md>) (she/her, halfling): Halfling trader and Charmheart matriarch, who retrieves her grandson [Ander](<../../../people/pcs/mawar/ander.md>) after the battle.
- [Ikram](<../../../people/dunmari/ikram.md>) (he/him, Dunmari human): Karawa's friendly innkeeper, who introduces [Riswynn](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/riswynn.md>) to the party.
- [Candrosa](<../../../people/dunmari/candrosa.md>) (he/him, Dunmari human): Speaker of the [Shakun Mystai](<../../../groups/dunmari-mystery-cults/shakun-mystai.md>), preparing to remain behind in Karawa's fortified temple.
- [Suma](<../../../people/dunmari/suma.md>) (she/her, Dunmari human): Karawa's priestess of the [Dunmari gods](<../../../gods-and-religions/religions/five-siblings/dunmari-religion.md>), who leads the [Festival of Rebirth](<../../../gods-and-religions/holidays-and-festivals/dunmari-festivals/festival-of-rebirth.md>).
- [Alesh](<../../../people/dunmari/alesh.md>) (she/her, Dunmari human): Dunmari scout and messenger, who shares Raven's Hold's history before riding south.
- [Havdar](<../../../people/dunmari/havdar.md>) (he/him, Dunmari human): Brash Dunmari war leader whose warriors escort Karawa's evacuees west.
- [Kisa](<../../../people/dunmari/kisa.md>) (she/her, Dunmari human): Respected elder and unofficial leader of [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>), helping prepare the villagers to evacuate.
- [Eberk Brawnanvil](<../../../people/dwarves/eberk-brawnanvil.md>) (he/him, dwarf): Riswynn's great-great-uncle, a dwarven elder who supplied her map of [Raven's Hold](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold.md>).

### Organizations

- [Shakun Mystai](<../../../groups/dunmari-mystery-cults/shakun-mystai.md>) (Dunmari mystery cult): Mystery cult devoted to Shakun, whose initiates prepare to remain in Karawa's fortified temple.
- [Havdar's Warband](<../../../groups/havdar-s-warband.md>) (Dunmari warband): Dunmari riders and warriors under Havdar's command, guarding the villagers' westward evacuation.
- [Brawnanvils](<../../../groups/dwarven-clans/brawnanvils.md>) (dwarven clan): Dwarven clan of [Tharn Todor](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/nardith/tharn-todor.md>) that sent [Riswynn](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/riswynn.md>) to recover an ancestral artifact from [Raven's Hold](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold.md>).

### Locations

- [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>) (town in [Eastern Dunmar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/eastern-dunmar.md>), [Dunmar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/dunmar.md>)): Oasis village beneath the [Red Mesa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/red-mesa.md>), attacked during the [Festival of Rebirth](<../../../gods-and-religions/holidays-and-festivals/dunmari-festivals/festival-of-rebirth.md>) and evacuated the next morning. Session context includes: Ikram's caravanserai, halfling camp, market, Shakun's temple and the [Red Mesa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/red-mesa.md>) cliff, and desert ceremony site.

### Preparations for the Journey

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="dunmari-frontier-session-6" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>After the council meeting, the party gathers information and supplies for their expedition to <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold/">Raven&#x27;s Hold</a>, while refugees continue to arrive in <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a> and the Dunmari people prepare to flee west.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>After the council meeting, the party reviews the outcome of the discussion: <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/havdar/">Havdar</a> will lead <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a>&#x27;s people west toward Tokra, and hopefully safety; <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/alesh/">Alesh</a> will scout south, trying to find information about the cause of these attacks; and the party will investigate <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold/">Raven&#x27;s Hold</a>, a journey they expect to take six or seven days. Rumors are starting to circulate around <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a>, and the celebratory atmosphere of the fesetival is fading into worry and fear.</p>
<p>In the afternoon, the party splits up to take care of errands and preparation. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> visits the halfling camp to talk to the <a href="/taelgarverse/groups/halfling-families/charmhearts/">Charmhearts</a> again, learning that they had a hard time at the market, as <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/mawar/ander/">Ander</a> is not improving, and disrupted some of their selling days. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/halflings/callie-charmheart/">Callie</a> tells <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> a bit more about <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold/">Raven&#x27;s Hold</a>: it is set in a river valley a few miles east of the road, near where a small river flows out from the mountains. Probing <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/mawar/ander/">Ander</a>&#x27;s thoughts, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> glimpses his passage through water, a waterfall, ruined walls, a tower, and vines, followed by overwhelming hunger and pain.</p>
<p><a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> asks <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> about the north; the Speaker says that its garrisons likely would have favored Aagir, but admits that <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/alesh/">Alesh</a> knows more about history than he does.</p>
<p><a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> asks <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a> about the mysterious runebook, though <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a> has little new to say: it appeared with a note for the heroes of <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a>, but no identified sender. He spends the rest of the afternoon examining the cliff around the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/temple-of-shakun/">Temple of Shakun</a>, where the stone of the mesa appears newer than the temple built into it, almost as though the mesa is growing.</p>
<p><a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath/">Delwath</a> checks in with people he knows from living in town for several months, and spends some time trading coin for gear and potions.</p>
<p>Meanwhile, refugees from <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/bas-udda/">Bas Udda</a> crowd into <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a>, survivors of the gnoll attacks there.</p></div>

### Fortifying the Temple

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="dunmari-frontier-session-6" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>After seeing <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/alesh/">Alesh</a> off to scout south, the party works to strengthen and fortify the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/temple-of-shakun/">Temple of Shakun</a> for those who will remain behind.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>That evening, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/alesh/">Alesh</a> arrives with two mounted scouts to say farewell, before she heads out past <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/bas-udda/">Bas Udda</a> and toward the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/garamjala-plateau/garamjala-desert/">Garamjala Desert</a>. The party plans to meet her again in <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a> in three weeks time, after their respective scouting missions. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/alesh/">Alesh</a> also explains that <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold/">Raven&#x27;s Hold</a> is a holdfast from before the <a href="/taelgarverse/events/1500s/great-war/">Great War</a>, and once the site of a trading outpost of the <a href="/taelgarverse/creatures/species/dwarves/">dwarves</a> of the southern mountains. She and her companions ride south before dark.</p>

This work takes much of the day; it is early evening when the party returns to <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a>&#x27;s for a meal before the <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/holidays-and-festivals/dunmari-festivals/festival-of-rebirth/">Festival of Rebirth</a>.</p></div>

### Riswynn and the Festival of Rebirth

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="dunmari-frontier-session-6" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>At <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a>&#x27;s for an evening meal, the party meets <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/riswynn/">Riswynn</a>, who will join them for the journey to <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold/">Raven&#x27;s Hold</a>. That evening is the beginning of the <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/holidays-and-festivals/dunmari-festivals/festival-of-rebirth/">Festival of Rebirth</a>, when the story of <a href="/taelgarverse/primary-sources/the-miracle-of-jeevali-s-resurrection/">Jeevali’s miracle</a> is told and shrine of Jeevali is burned; <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/suma/">Suma</a> interprets the burning shrine&#x27;s westward-drifting embers as Jeevali&#x27;s blessing for the evacuation.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>At <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a>&#x27;s caravanserai, the party meets <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/riswynn/">Riswynn</a> Brawnanvil over dinner. She arrived that morning from <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/nardith/tharn-todor/">Tharn Todor</a>, sent by the <a href="/taelgarverse/groups/dwarven-clans/brawnanvils/">Brawnanvils</a> to recover a clan artifact from <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold/">Raven&#x27;s Hold</a>, prompted by rumors of Chardonian treasure hunters seeking artifacts and relics left behind from the <a href="/taelgarverse/events/1500s/great-war/">Great War</a> era. A large dwarven expedition has set out for the old strongholds of Ardith, but fearing it would ignore a minor holding far to the east like <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold/">Raven&#x27;s Hold</a>, the Brawnanvil clan sent their own treasure hunter. During their conversation, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/riswynn/">Riswynn</a> tells of the map her great-great-uncle Eberk supplied from memory; <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> explains <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/mawar/ander/">Ander</a>&#x27;s affliction; <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> shows her <a href="/taelgarverse/things/books/the-runes-of-creation/">The Runes of Creation</a>; and their shared destination gives <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/riswynn/">Riswynn</a> reason to join the party.</p>
<p>After this meeting, the <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/holidays-and-festivals/dunmari-festivals/festival-of-rebirth/">Festival of Rebirth</a> begins. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/suma/">Suma</a>, the priestess of Jeevali, and a number of litter bearers carry a tall sculpture shaped like a tree, and seemingly made of vines, and dried flowers, and bits of wood and seeds all pulled together into a trunk, with a design that spirals up to a crown of branches and leaves at the top. They carry the sculpture to the desert, where <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/suma/">Suma</a> tells the story of <a href="/taelgarverse/primary-sources/the-miracle-of-jeevali-s-resurrection/">Jeevali’s miracle</a>.</p>
<p>At the climax of the story, the tree-shrine is set alight. As the shrine catches on fire, as it bursts into flames, its embers drift strangely, almost like fireworks, floating mostly west. And as these embers drift to the west, a sigh goes through the crowd, as <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/suma/">Suma</a> says that Jeevali is with us, Jeevali is going west and we will follow her. The ashes are dispersed, and a night of fasting precedes the next day&#x27;s renewal.</p>

### The Attack on Karawa

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="dunmari-frontier-session-6" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>The next day, the procession celebrating Jeevali&#x27;s renewal is interrupted by gnolls and monstrous maw creatures; the party comes to <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a>&#x27;s defense, though <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> nearly dies in the battle, and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/mawar/ander/">Ander</a> tries to be devoured.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><figure class="taelgar-session-zoom__image taelgar-image taelgar-image--aside taelgar-image--size-standard taelgar-image--align-right">
  <img src="/taelgarverse/assets/campaign/dufr-006-maw-creature.webp" alt="A squat, clawed creature with scattered eyes and an enormous mouth full of teeth advances between the camp&#x27;s tents.">
  <figcaption>One of the monstrous mouth creatures attacking <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a>.</figcaption>
</figure>
<p>The following morning brings a solemn procession to renew Jeevali&#x27;s shrine for the next year. By late afternoon, the townspeople&#x27;s offerings are beginning to fuse together when dust, shouts, and screams draw the party&#x27;s attention. Three gnolls carrying crude swords advance towards the villagers, alongside three grotesque creatures made of mouths, eyes, and limbs.</p>
<p>The party leaps to <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a>&#x27;s defense. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a>&#x27;s sleep magic drops one gnoll; <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> takes cover on a nearby building and shoots into the fighting as <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a>, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath/">Delwath</a>, and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/riswynn/">Riswynn</a> engage. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> hurls a ball of force, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/riswynn/">Riswynn</a> strikes with a flaming warhammer, and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath/">Delwath</a> fights with a shadow blade and leaping green fire. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> heats a wounded gnoll&#x27;s sword until the creature burns to death.</p>
<p><a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/mawar/ander/">Ander</a> then stumbles from the camp trailing broken rope. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> hears his thoughts welcoming the master and begging to be eaten as he struggles toward the maws of the waiting monsters. <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="dunmari-frontier-session-6" data-zoom="short">

The party follows the attackers&#x27; tracks north at dawn, while <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/havdar/">Havdar</a> and his warband being the task of escorting the villagers safely west.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>After the battle is over, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> meditates silently, and the party deals with the aftermath. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> recalls an illustration in a book he once read, Intrusions From the Darker Planes, showing beings resembling the maw creatures, described as foot soldiers of a demon lord of hunger and suggesting a possible extraplanar origin for the attack, though how the creatures reached <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a> remains a mystery. <p><a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/havdar/">Havdar</a> reports having defeated more gnoll-creatures; he is unsettled by the rapidly decaying, fading remains of the maw creatures, and urges everyone to leave at first light. ## Combat

**Defense of [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>).** The party defeats three gnolls and three maw creatures that attack [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>) during the [Festival of Rebirth](<../../../gods-and-religions/holidays-and-festivals/dunmari-festivals/festival-of-rebirth.md>); [Riswynn](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/riswynn.md>) saves [Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>), and the one gnoll incapacitated by Seeker's magic is killed after questioning.
