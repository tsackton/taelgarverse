# Dunmari Frontier - Session 5

!!! info "Karawa at the Crossroads: in which a council debates how to save the village"
	*Featuring: [Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>), [Wellby](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby.md>), [Delwath](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath.md>), and [Seeker](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker.md>)*
	*In Taelgar: Mar 30, 1748 DR to Apr 04, 1748 DR*
	*On Earth: Friday, July 24, 2020*
	*[Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>)*

The party joins in the games and celebrations for the [Festival of Rebirth](<../../../gods-and-religions/holidays-and-festivals/dunmari-festivals/festival-of-rebirth.md>), until troubling reports from returning scouts force a divided council to confront Karawa’s future.

## Timeline

!!! info "Table Notes"
	The party reaches level 3.

- Mar 30, 1748 DR, dawn: [Candrosa](<../../../people/dunmari/candrosa.md>) sends [Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>) into the Dreamworld, where a jade-armed figure directs him toward the [Order of the Awakened Soul](<../../../groups/dunmari-mystery-cults/order-of-the-awakened-soul.md>).
- Mar 30, 1748 DR, afternoon: The party visits [Jasu](<../../../people/dunmari/jasu.md>) to examine Arcus’s enchanted spearpoints, where [Seeker](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker.md>) commissions a chain throwing net.
- Mar 31, 1748 DR: The party receives gifts from Karawa’s grateful townspeople, including a [mysterious Dwarven book](<../../../things/books/the-runes-of-creation.md>) from an unknown benefactor for [Seeker](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker.md>).
- Apr 01, 1748 DR: The festival games begin. [Seeker](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker.md>) studies, and [Delwath](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath.md>) gathers rumors around [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>).
- Apr 02, 1748 DR: [Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>) wins the staff-dancing tournament, inspiring [Seeker](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker.md>) to illustrate his victory. Later, the party discusses the rumors [Delwath](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath.md>) has gathered.
- Apr 03, 1748 DR: [Wellby](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby.md>) and [Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>) compete in the axebeak race, with [Wellby](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby.md>) placing third. [Alesh](<../../../people/dunmari/alesh.md>) and [Havdar](<../../../people/dunmari/havdar.md>) return with urgent news. The party agrees to attend the next morning’s council.
- Apr 04, 1748 DR, morning: Karawa's council meets, and debates the fate of the village.

## People and Places

### NPCs

- [Candrosa](<../../../people/dunmari/candrosa.md>) (he/him, Dunmari human): Speaker of the [Shakun Mystai](<../../../groups/dunmari-mystery-cults/shakun-mystai.md>) and leader of Karawa’s [Temple of Shakun](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/temple-of-shakun.md>).
- jade-armed robed man: An unidentified figure encountered by [Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>) in the Dreamworld, who directs him toward the [Order of the Awakened Soul](<../../../groups/dunmari-mystery-cults/order-of-the-awakened-soul.md>).
- [Jasu](<../../../people/dunmari/jasu.md>) (he/him, Dunmari human): Karawa’s blacksmith, a solidly built older man and frequent patron of Ikram’s.
- [Ikram](<../../../people/dunmari/ikram.md>) (he/him, Dunmari human): The friendly, optimistic owner of Karawa’s caravanserai, fond of travelers, gossip, and stories.
- [Jayden](<../../../people/dunmari/jayden.md>) (she/her, Dunmari human): A young Dunmari woman, exceptionally quick and agile, and a skilled staff-dancing competitor.
- [Callie Charmheart](<../../../people/halflings/callie-charmheart.md>) (she/her, halfling): A halfling merchant traveling with the Charmheart family, and Ander’s older sister.
- [Alesh](<../../../people/dunmari/alesh.md>) (she/her, Dunmari human): A skilled Dunmari scout, messenger, and rider from [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>), with an enthusiastic interest in ancient Dunmari history.
- [Havdar](<../../../people/dunmari/havdar.md>) (he/him, Dunmari human): A brash, confident Dunmari war leader whose band of mounted warriors helps defend the eastern frontier.
- [Kisa](<../../../people/dunmari/kisa.md>) (she/her, Dunmari human): A respected village elder and unofficial leader of [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>), deliberate in speech and protective of its people.
- [Suma](<../../../people/dunmari/suma.md>) (she/her, Dunmari human): Priestess of Karawa’s temple to the [Dunmari gods](<../../../gods-and-religions/religions/five-siblings/dunmari-religion.md>), particularly devoted to Jeevali; a warm and generous community leader.
- [Ander Charmheart](<../../../people/halflings/ander-charmheart.md>) (he/him, halfling): Callie’s younger brother and a scout for the Charmheart caravan, afflicted with ravenous hunger and strange ravings after exploring [Raven’s Hold](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold.md>).
- [Arcus](<../../../people/chardonians/arcus.md>) (*AR-koos*; he/him, Chardonian human): An adventurer and treasure hunter found petrified in the ruined fort east of Gomat.
- [Nayan Karnas](<../../../people/dunmari/nayan-karnas.md>) (he/him, Dunmari human): The reigning Samraat of [Dunmar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/dunmar.md>), a ruler of the [Nayan dynasty](<../../../groups/dunmari-dynasties/nayan-dynasty.md>).

### Organizations

- [Shakun Mystai](<../../../groups/dunmari-mystery-cults/shakun-mystai.md>) (Dunmari mystery cult): Candrosa's order guides Kenzo's spirit journey and prepares to remain behind in Karawa's temple.
- [Order of the Awakened Soul](<../../../groups/dunmari-mystery-cults/order-of-the-awakened-soul.md>) (Dunmari mystery cult): A Dreamworld figure tells [Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>) to seek the order's stories.
- [Great Library](<../../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/great-library.md>) (library in [Precinct IV](<../../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/precinct-iv.md>), [Chardon](<../../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/chardon.md>)): Rumor accuses the Chardonian institution of seeking or plundering Dunmari artifacts.
- [Havdar's Warband](<../../../groups/havdar-s-warband.md>) (Dunmari warband): Havdar's forty riders become the planned escort for Karawa's evacuation.

### Locations

- [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>) (town in [Eastern Dunmar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/eastern-dunmar.md>), [Dunmar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/dunmar.md>)): eastern Dunmari oasis settlement whose festival gives way to preparations for evacuation as threats close in along the border. Session context includes: the summit of the [red mesa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/red-mesa.md>) overlooking the town.

### Into the Dreamworld

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="dunmari-frontier-session-5" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>On the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/red-mesa/">Red Mesa</a> before dawn, and guided by <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> and the <a href="/taelgarverse/things/artifacts-of-power/dreamweaver-staff/">Dreamweaver Staff</a>, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> enters the Dreamworld, where he meets a jade-armed figure who tells him to seek stories of the <a href="/taelgarverse/groups/dunmari-mystery-cults/order-of-the-awakened-soul/">Order of the Awakened Soul</a>. When the others join him on the mesa, the party discusses their experiences with visions.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><figure class="taelgar-session-zoom__image taelgar-image taelgar-image--hero taelgar-image--size-standard">
  <img src="/taelgarverse/assets/dunmar-session-05-kenzo-dreamworld-vision.webp" alt="A hooded figure with a jade arm shattering into fragments amid multicolored lights">
  <figcaption>The jade-armed figure in <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a>&#x27;s Dreamworld vision.</figcaption>

<p>Before dawn, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> wakes <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> and leads him to the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/red-mesa/">Red Mesa</a> above the villages, where, using the <a href="/taelgarverse/things/artifacts-of-power/dreamweaver-staff/">Dreamweaver Staff</a>, he sends <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> into the Dreamworld. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> passes through a vast expanse of flickering, multicolored lights before meeting a robed man with a jade arm, who tells him, &quot;You are not alone,&quot; and directs him to seek stories of the <a href="/taelgarverse/groups/dunmari-mystery-cults/order-of-the-awakened-soul/">Order of the Awakened Soul</a>.</p>
<p>At breakfast at <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a>’s caravanserai, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> shares a <a href="/taelgarverse/campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/letters-and-notes/letter-from-candrosa/">letter from Candrosa</a>. Acknowledging his suspicion during their meeting the previous evening, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> now appeals to the party for help. <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/dunmari-pantheon/shakun/">Shakun</a> remains silent, the enchantments protecting the frontier are fading, and scouts are riding to discover what dangers lie beyond the border. He asks the party to remain in <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a> as defenders, attend the council when the scouts return, and keep his warning private until then. After being informed that <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> is meditating at the summit of the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/red-mesa/">Red Mesa</a> on the southern border of town, the party heads up to meet him; on the way <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> is reminded of the geological oddity that the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/temple-of-shakun/">Temple of Shakun</a>, carved into the cliff wall of the mesa, seems to be older than the mesa itself.</p>
<p>Reunited on the mesa, some discussion ensues of various pre-dawn encounters. The lights <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> describes remind <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> of the star-filled void of the <a href="/taelgarverse/cosmology/spiritual-realms/heart-of-the-mountain/">Heart of the Mountain</a> from his Dwarven coming-of-age rite; <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath/">Delwath</a> reveals that touching a dark circlet after the <a href="/taelgarverse/events/1500s/great-war/">Great War</a> trapped him for two hundred years in a gray pale imitation of the world where time seemed not to pass. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> shares what he learned during the night from Ander&#x27;s sister, Callie. At <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold/">Raven&#x27;s Hold</a>, Ander heard someone chanting and was wounded by a thorny vine oozing blood-red sap. Within a few days, he began craving raw flesh and speaking of a master who would consume the world.</p></div>

### At Jasu's Forge

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="dunmari-frontier-session-5" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>That afternoon, the party heads to <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/jasu/">Jasu</a>&#x27;s forge, and learns more about the spearpoints <a href="/taelgarverse/people/chardonians/arcus/">Arcus</a> found. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> presents his design for a weighted chain throwing net, and persuades <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/jasu/">Jasu</a> to build it for payment on delivery.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>That afternoon, the party returns to <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/jasu/">Jasu</a>’s workshop to inspect the two silvered spearpoints <a href="/taelgarverse/people/chardonians/arcus/">Arcus</a> left behind. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> recognizes designs suggesting a weapon made to overcome undead defenses, and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath/">Delwath</a> detects abjuration magic. The spearpoints date from the <a href="/taelgarverse/events/1500s/great-war/">Great War</a>, but <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/jasu/">Jasu</a> cannot reshape their enchanted metal to repair the fittings needed to mount them on shafts. The party also confirms that <a href="/taelgarverse/people/chardonians/arcus/">Arcus</a> had promised to return within a week or two and is now several weeks overdue. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> then unrolls a design for a lightweight throwing net made from weighted chain. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/jasu/">Jasu</a> hesitates after <a href="/taelgarverse/people/chardonians/arcus/">Arcus</a>&#x27;s unpaid commission, but <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> convinces him that the party can cover the cost, and the smith agrees to begin work.</p></div>

### The Festival of Rebirth

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="dunmari-frontier-session-5" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>Over the next several days, the games and festivities leading up to the <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/holidays-and-festivals/dunmari-festivals/festival-of-rebirth/">Festival of Rebirth</a> occupy the party and the village. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> wins the staff-dancing tournament, and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a> places third in the axebeak race. But the party&#x27;s mood shifts when they learn <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/alesh/">Alesh</a> and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/havdar/">Havdar</a> have returned to <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a> with troubling news.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>Over the next several days, <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a> settles into the games and festivities leading up to the <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/holidays-and-festivals/dunmari-festivals/festival-of-rebirth/">Festival of Rebirth</a>. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/ikram/">Ikram</a> delivers gifts of silver and trade goods from the grateful town, and an anonymous benefactor leaves <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> an apparently blank Dwarven volume titled <a href="/taelgarverse/things/books/the-runes-of-creation/">The Runes of Creation</a>. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> spends time studying his new book.</p>
<p>As <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath/">Delwath</a> listens for news, he finds a town mostly eager to celebrate. Many believe the danger ended with the defeat of the rampaging beasts, and dismiss stories of hyena-like raiders to the south as confused accounts of that attack. Yet the festivities carry an undercurrent of grief for those lost, and some wonder whether news of strange storms that don&#x27;t move, and spreading violence, mean the gods have abandoned them. Many rumors involve ruins and artifacts: the name <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold/">Raven&#x27;s Hold</a> is mentioned, as is the claim that the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/temple-of-shakun/">Temple of Shakun</a> hides a number of ancient artifacts, and the angry fear that <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/chardon/">Chardon</a> is trying to steal <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/dunmar/">Dunmar</a>’s heritage.</p>
<p><a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> enters the staff-dancing tournament, in which contestants fight with quarterstaffs on a balance beam and the first to fall loses. He draws a growing crowd as he advances through its early rounds. In the final he faces the swift and balanced <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/jayden/">Jayden</a>, then bursts forward and sweeps her from the beam, earning his victory, then honoring her skill. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> begins transforming the contest into an illustrated sequence called The Legendary Staff of <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a>, naming his new art form the &quot;graphic novel.&quot;</p>
<p>The axebeak race brings another burst of celebration, and some of the largest crowds of the festival, with each rider judged by how far they travel before falling. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby/">Wellby</a>&#x27;s offering of goat meat earns him a bleeding hand, but he stays mounted long enough to finish third, while <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a>&#x27;s acrobatic start sends him backward almost immediately. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/havdar/">Havdar</a> returns during the riding competitions and insists on competing despite his exhaustion. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/alesh/">Alesh</a> arrives after dinner, exhausted and travel-stained, and, bearing sober news, asks the party to join the council in the morning.</p>

### The Fate of Karawa

  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>The next morning, at the council meeting, the scouts reveal their news, pointing to dangers from many directions. The council decides to evacuate <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a>&#x27;s people, but still arranges to fortify <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/dunmari-pantheon/shakun/">Shakun</a>&#x27;s temple, as the mystai will never leave it. The <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/dunmar/">Dunmar</a> Fellowship agrees to scout <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold/">Raven&#x27;s Hold</a>, while <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/alesh/">Alesh</a> scouts south, and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/havdar/">Havdar</a> and his warband protect the fleeing villagers.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>The next morning, at the council meeting, the scouts reveal their news. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/havdar/">Havdar</a> describes armed hyena-like raiders destroying settlements to the south and argues for an immediate a counterattack, while <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/alesh/">Alesh</a> reports a stationary storm, a black stream that smells of blood, and dangerous bands moving elsewhere along the border. She urges an appeal to Samraat <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/nayan-karnas/">Nayan Karnas</a>. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/kisa/">Kisa</a> and <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> favor fortification and defense, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/suma/">Suma</a> favors flight toward Tokra, and the leaders turn to the party to break the deadlock. Their questions expose the limits of every choice: <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a> can muster riders and endure a siege for a time, but Tokra is weeks away and royal aid may take months. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> offers to raise earthworks, but <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> quietly admits that he is no longer certain <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/dunmari-pantheon/shakun/">Shakun</a>&#x27;s power can protect the ancient temple.</p>
<p><a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker/">Seeker</a> spreads his map across the table and conjures glowing, moving figures above it, representing the fleeing population, their defenders, the storm, and the bloody stream. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo/">Kenzo</a> shapes the competing proposals into a plan centered on survival and information. After <a href="/taelgarverse/people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath/">Delwath</a> warns that a reckless attack might result in <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/havdar/">Havdar</a>&#x27;s own riders returning transformed to enemies, the war leader accepts the need for caution. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/suma/">Suma</a> argues that &quot;<a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a> is its people&quot; and urges them to protect those who can flee, though <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> refuses to abandon the temple and its history.</p>
<p>After much debate, it is decided that the vast majority of the people will flee west, with <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/havdar/">Havdar</a> and his troops providing a rear-guard defense for this retreat. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/candrosa/">Candrosa</a> and a few other acolytes of <a href="/taelgarverse/gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/dunmari-pantheon/shakun/">Shakun</a>, who will not abandon their temple, will fortify themselves and hope to hide from any attack. <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/alesh/">Alesh</a> and a few companions will scout again to the south, trying to learn more about the threat there. An emissary will be found to ride to the king with all speed, and argue for a muster and a defense of the border. And the party will head north, to <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/raven-s-hold/">Raven&#x27;s Hold</a>, to seek what information may lie there.</p>

## Treasure and Things

- [Dreamweaver Staff](<../../../things/artifacts-of-power/dreamweaver-staff.md>) (unique Dunmari staff): [Candrosa](<../../../people/dunmari/candrosa.md>) uses the staff to send [Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>) into the Dreamworld.
- [Letter from Candrosa](<../letters-and-notes/letter-from-candrosa.md>): [Candrosa](<../../../people/dunmari/candrosa.md>) asks the party to remain in [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>) for the scouts' return and the council meeting.
- Seeker's chain throwing net: [Jasu](<../../../people/dunmari/jasu.md>) accepts Seeker's design and agrees to build the weighted net for payment on delivery.
- Karawa's gifts: The town rewards each party member with silver and trade goods worth roughly fifty gold pieces.
- [The Runes of Creation](<../../../things/books/the-runes-of-creation.md>) (unique magical book): An anonymous benefactor leaves [Seeker](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker.md>) the Dwarven book, whose pages initially appear blank.
- The Legendary Staff of [Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>): [Seeker](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker.md>) turns Kenzo's tournament performance into an illustrated sequential narrative.
