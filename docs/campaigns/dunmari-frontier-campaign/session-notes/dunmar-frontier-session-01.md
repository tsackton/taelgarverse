# Dunmari Frontier - Session 1

!!! info "Hyena Rampage: in which the party meets and confronts dangers in Karawa"
	*Featuring: [Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>), [Wellby](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby.md>), [Delwath](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath.md>), and [Seeker](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker.md>)*
	*In Taelgar: Mar 22, 1748 DR to Mar 23, 1748 DR*
	*On Earth: Friday, May 29, 2020*
	*[Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>), [Karawa Desert](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/karawa-desert.md>), [Stoneway](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/roads/stoneway.md>), and [Gomat Oasis](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/gomat.md>)*

Four travelers band together to protect [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>) from giant hyenas, then investigate the escalating attacks by following the trail to a herders' camp at [Gomat Oasis](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/gomat.md>).

## Timeline

!!! info "Table Notes"
	[Kenzo](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>), [Seeker](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker.md>), [Wellby](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/wellby.md>), and [Delwath](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath.md>) meet, and our campaign begins at level 1.

- Mar 22, 1748 DR, morning: Four strangers band together to protect [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>) from giant hyenas. Afterward, Elder [Kisa](<../../../people/dunmari/kisa.md>) and Speaker [Candrosa](<../../../people/dunmari/candrosa.md>) ask [Delwath](<../../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath.md>) and companions for help.
- Mar 22, 1748 DR, afternoon: The party follows the hyenas' trail into the canyonlands, and meets [Alesh](<../../../people/dunmari/alesh.md>) on the [Stoneway](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/roads/stoneway.md>). They turn east towards [Gomat Oasis](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/gomat.md>), making a cold overnight camp on the road.
- Mar 23, 1748 DR: A long day of travel brings the party to the [Gomat Oasis](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/gomat.md>) at moonrise, where they find giant lizards feeding on dead sheep.

## People and Places

### NPCs

- [Beli](<../../../people/dunmari/beli.md>) (she/her, Dunmari human): Shakun initiate who fights beside the strangers.
- [Badya](<../../../people/dunmari/badya.md>) (she/her, Dunmari human): a teahouse worker.
- [Candrosa](<../../../people/dunmari/candrosa.md>) (he/him, Dunmari human): the Speaker of the [Shakun Mystai](<../../../groups/dunmari-mystery-cults/shakun-mystai.md>).
- [Kisa](<../../../people/dunmari/kisa.md>) (she/her, Dunmari human): elder and unofficial spokeswoman for [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>).
- [Alesh](<../../../people/dunmari/alesh.md>) (she/her, Dunmari human): Dunmari scout returning from Gomat.

### Organizations

- [Shakun Mystai](<../../../groups/dunmari-mystery-cults/shakun-mystai.md>) (Dunmari mystery cult): The secretive order centered on the [Temple of Shakun](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/temple-of-shakun.md>).

### Locations

- [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>) (town in [Eastern Dunmar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/eastern-dunmar.md>), [Dunmar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/dunmar.md>)): A desert settlement around an oasis beneath a red sandstone mesa, where the four travelers meet, repel a giant hyena attack, and accept Kisa's request to investigate. Session context includes: Ikram's, the village edge, and and Badya's teahouse.
- [Karawa Desert](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/karawa-desert.md>) (desert in the [Hara Basin](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/hara-basin.md>), [Greater Dunmar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/greater-dunmar.md>)): Rocky badlands where the hyenas' trail vanishes.
- [Stoneway](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/roads/stoneway.md>) (road in [Dunmar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/dunmar.md>), [Greater Dunmar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/greater-dunmar.md>)): An ancient route where the party meets [Alesh](<../../../people/dunmari/alesh.md>); further east, it passes through cataclysm-scarred desert before reaching the [Gomat Oasis](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/gomat.md>).
- [Gomat Oasis](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/gomat.md>) (oasis in [Eastern Dunmar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/eastern-dunmar.md>), [Dunmar](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/dunmar.md>)): A herders' oasis where the party finds empty tents, dead sheep, and three giant lizards feeding in the moonlight.

Climbing via grappling hook to a high point, he looks around, seeing nothing stirring across the canyonlands, which extended for miles to the east towards a large, distant canyon. To the north, a cloud of dust points towards something moving along the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/roads/stoneway/">Stoneway</a>, the old trade road.</p></div>

### The Road to Gomat

<div class="taelgar-session-zoom__beat" data-session-zoom-beat data-session-zoom-key="dunmari-frontier-session-1" data-zoom="short">
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="short"><p>Investigating the dust cloud, the party meets a Dunmari scout, <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/alesh/">Alesh</a>, on the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/roads/stoneway/">Stoneway</a>, the old Drankorian trade road. Her warning sends the party toward <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/gomat/">Gomat</a>. After a long march the next day, the party reaches <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/gomat/">Gomat</a> to find empty tents and giant lizards feeding on dead sheep.</p></div>
  <div class="taelgar-session-zoom__level" data-zoom-level="long"><p>The dust cloud belongs to <a href="/taelgarverse/people/dunmari/alesh/">Alesh</a>, a Dunmari scout returning from <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/gomat/">Gomat</a>, where she had spent the night with Akan and his family, who were grazing their herds in the area. She speaks with the party, discussing the attacks on the town, and how in her childhood people were scared, but for the past decade the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/nashtkar/">Nashtkar</a>, the blasted plains, has seemed, if not safe, at least less of a constant threat to <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a>. But perhaps that was changing, she mused. She rides on for the grazing lands north of <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa/">Karawa</a>, while the party turns east for <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/gomat/">Gomat</a>.</p>
<p>They follow the <a href="/taelgarverse/gazetteer/greater-dunmar/roads/stoneway/">ancient road</a> east through land twisted by cataclysm and make a cold camp by the side of the road. The next morning, the party resumes travel before dawn, for a long, punishing march through the heat of the day. By evening, exhausted from the long, hard day of travel, they arrive at the oasis, only to see tents, yes, but also three enormous lizards, ten feet long and scaly, ripping into dead sheep scattered across the ground.</p>

## Combat

**Giant Hyenas in [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>).** Two giant hyenas charge into [Karawa](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/eastern-dunmar/karawa.md>) and maul a villager; the four travelers kill both beasts and stabilize the victim, though Beli's divine magic inexplicably fails during the fight.

## Treasure and Things

- Red ochre healing paste: A muddy temple preparation that [Beli](<../../../people/dunmari/beli.md>) uses to close Delwath's and Kenzo's wounds.
