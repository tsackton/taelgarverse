## Timeline

- May 28, 1715 DR, afternoon: The party continues across the [Highmoor](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/highmoor.md>) towards [Brantor](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/brantor.md>) and Caradoc's cottage.
- May 29, 1715 DR: The party crosses the [Highmoor](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/highmoor.md>) and arrives at Caradoc's in the evening, where they discuss the return of the heart to the Singing Tree. After dinner, they head out towards [Haldrenn](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/haldrenn.md>), walking for three hours before camping. 
- May 30, 1715 DR, morning: The party walks the rest of the way to [Haldrenn](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/haldrenn.md>), and tells the villagers their plans. 
- May 30, 1715 DR, afternoon: The party walks to [Nightsong Roots](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/nightsong-roots.md>), and uses Caradoc's fey-crossing potion to enter [Thornweald](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/thornweald.md>), [Morlaith](<../../people/extraplanar-powers/archfey/morlaith.md>)'s domain in the [Feywild](<../../cosmology/feywild.md>). They save a weeping willow from deadly butterflies and meet a pair of curious foxes who set them on their way to find a guide.

## Narrative

### Friday, May 28th, 2 Robert I

The party continues walking all day in pleasant spring weather, making camp on the [Highmoor](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/highmoor.md>).

### Saturday, May 29th, 2 Robert I

The party crosses the [Highmoor](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/highmoor.md>) and arrives at [Caradoc](<../../people/addermarians/caradoc.md>)'s cottage outside [Brantor](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/brantor.md>). They update [Caradoc](<../../people/addermarians/caradoc.md>) on the events at [Serethwyn Tor](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/serethwyn-tor.md>), and plan to go back to the [Feywild](<../../cosmology/feywild.md>) to return the Singing Tree's heart. They discuss [Thornweald](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/thornweald.md>) and the [Briarheart](<../../people/extraplanar-powers/archfey/morlaith.md>) with [Caradoc](<../../people/addermarians/caradoc.md>), who gives them several pieces of advice: seek a guide, the fey of the [Thornweald](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/thornweald.md>) may be strange, but they are not inherently evil, and hurry, because in a few days the Singing Tree will regrow and the challenge will be much greater. He also gives the party a small vial of a dense liquid that, when smashed on the ground, connects the [Feywild](<../../cosmology/feywild.md>) and the [Material Plane](<../../cosmology/material-plane.md>) for a short moment, allowing passage. He also gives them a vine-covered wooden wand that, if broken, will summon [Lady Calanthë](<../../people/extraplanar-powers/archfey/calanthe.md>), Duchess of the Sunset Forest and Guardian of the Gloaming Hills, who will help, but for a price, most likely in service. 

After dinner and conversation, the party decides to walk back to [Haldrenn](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/haldrenn.md>) to hopefully be closer to their [Feywild](<../../cosmology/feywild.md>) destination, though [Caradoc](<../../people/addermarians/caradoc.md>) warns them distant in the Feywild is tricky. After three hours walking under increasingly cloudy skies, they camp for the night on the [Highmoor](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/highmoor.md>).

### Sunday, May 30th, 2 Robert I

The party crosses the [Highmoor](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/highmoor.md>) back to [Haldrenn](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/haldrenn.md>) under cloudy skies, arriving around noon and warning the villagers of their plan to return the heart. They then use Caradoc's vial to enter the [Feywild](<../../cosmology/feywild.md>) near the currently-closed [Nightsong Roots](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/nightsong-roots.md>) portal, arriving in the [Thornweald](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/thornweald.md>).

!!! image taelgar-image taelgar-image--hero taelgar-image--size-standard " "
	![Adma 021 Fork in the Thornweald](/taelgarverse/assets/campaign/adma-021-fork-in-the-thornweald.webp)
	*A fork in the paths of the Thornweald.*

They arrive on a path, at a fork, in the late afternoon. The sun is hidden by clouds and trees, and a light drizzle drips from the leaves of the dense woods. To the left, a path leads into a dark, tangled forest, heading up a short hill; to the right, the path continues into the close forest, but seems to be more open, with patches of sunlight. The party goes up the hill, following the dark path, and come out into a grassy meadow filled with incongruous sunshine. In the meadow, a lone willow tree stands, covered in bright butterflies that, to [Yvan](<../../people/pcs/addermarch/yvan-greenrabbit.md>), smell slightly of rot and decay. As soon as the party steps into the meadow, the butterflies attack, swarming around [Drou](<../../people/pcs/addermarch/drou.md>) with poisonous bites. Both [Yvan](<../../people/pcs/addermarch/yvan-greenrabbit.md>) and [Fazoth](<../../people/pcs/addermarch/fazoth-de-brune.md>) strike the swarm with Witch Bolts, and it quickly weakens and is temporarily driven off by [Drou](<../../people/pcs/addermarch/drou.md>)'s Dissonant Whispers. The willow, confused, strikes out at Yvan, dragging him close in retaliation for his attack on the swarm. But the swarm is soon killed by [Yvan](<../../people/pcs/addermarch/yvan-greenrabbit.md>)'s sorcerous burst, and with the corrupting influence of the butterflies gone, Drou is able to calm the willow, and draw out [its story](<handouts/the-story-of-sweetwillow.md>). 

!!! image taelgar-image taelgar-image--figure taelgar-image--size-standard " "
	![Adma 021 Willow and Butterflies](/taelgarverse/assets/campaign/adma-021-willow-and-butterflies.webp)
	*Bright butterflies gather on the willow in the sunlit clearing.*

After calming the willow, [Yvan](<../../people/pcs/addermarch/yvan-greenrabbit.md>) climbs to its top to scout, seeing a narrow animal track leaving the clearing, descending gradually towards a river, across which a small number of large trees rise above the canopy. With a sense that is the direction of the Singing Tree, the party proceeds down the narrow path, and prompted gets lost in the forest. 

!!! image taelgar-image taelgar-image--aside inline end taelgar-image--align-right taelgar-image--size-standard " "
	![Adma 021 Kip and Flo](/taelgarverse/assets/campaign/adma-021-kip-and-flo.webp)
	*Kip, left, and his sister Flo.*

Luckily, a pair of young foxlings, [Kip](<../../people/fey/kip.md>) and [Flo](<../../people/fey/flo.md>), are following them out of curiosity. Yvan hears them, and [Fazoth](<../../people/pcs/addermarch/fazoth-de-brune.md>), with the help of his pseudodragon, is able to spot them: for a few moments, the foxes follow the party, while the pseudodragon follows the foxes. [Fazoth](<../../people/pcs/addermarch/fazoth-de-brune.md>) stops to speak to them, and quickly charms them, a surprising example in their eyes of a "person who can speak" (meaning, talk in Sylvan). After some playful banter, a discussion of the willow, and a race that Fazoth wins by using Misty Step, the foxlings suggest that [Gwydren](<../../people/fey/gwydren.md>) is a good guide to find; he lives across the river near the big trees. As they are completely lost, the party asks for help to find their way. The foxlings happily oblige, and lead the party towards the river road, a much wider path sloping gently down towards the river basin. Fazoth invites them to join, but they decline, saying they are not allowed to cross the river. 

We end as the party turns towards the river on the wide path.
