![Saka](/taelgarverse/assets/saka.webp){align="right"; width="350"}Saka is an eldery woman, and a Master of the [Mystai of Bhishma](<../../groups/dunmari-mystery-cults/order-of-the-awakened-soul.md>). She grew up outside [Tokra](<../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/central-dunmar/tokra/tokra.md>), and now travels with her horse-herding family on the [Varashan](<../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/hara-basin/varashan.md>), helped by her niece [Jita](<jita-of-tokra.md>).

Saka opposed the order’s attempt to cleanse the [Plaguelands](<../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/plaguelands.md>) in DR 1718, believing it too dangerous, and refused to participate. After the [attempt failed](<../../events/1700s/1718/awakened-soul-disaster.md>), she fled west and did her best to train new apprentices to preserve what she could of the Mystai. 

Wary of outsiders and skeptical of grand heroic quests, she believes in service to ordinary people and the preservation of their stories. For Saka, Dunmar’s strength lies in guarding its people against the darkness, and she fears that the pursuit of impossible victories will cost the order what little remains.
