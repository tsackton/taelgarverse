Govir is a Dunmari scholar and the Master of Records at the [Tokra Archives](<../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/central-dunmar/tokra/archives.md>). Devout and single-minded, he is gathering [accounts of the victories and miracles](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/session-notes/session-35-dufr.md>) which he believes shows that the [gods](<../../gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/dunmari-pantheon/dunmari-pantheon.md>) have upheld and supported the [Nayan Dynasty](<../../groups/dunmari-dynasties/nayan-dynasty.md>). He holds [Samraat Nayan Yarad](<../historical-figures/dunmari-rulers/yarad.md>) in particular esteem, seeing in his victories evidence of divine favor.

His planned work centers on five such deliverances: the [Northern War](<../../events/1600s/northern-war.md>), the [expulsion of the Fraternity of the Empty Moon](<../../events/1600s/exile-of-fraternity-of-the-empty-moon.md>) from Tokra, the [Battle of Shrevandurg](<../../events/1600s/battle-of-shrevandurg.md>), the [Defeat of the Plague Trees](<../../events/1600s/defeat-of-the-plague-trees.md>), and the [Summer of Red Storms](<../../events/1700s/1709/summer-of-red-storms.md>). He is particularly interested in finding accounts from soldiers, including surviving veterans, that report how the presence of [Aagir](<../../gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/dunmari-pantheon/aagir.md>) in battle or the mercy of [Jeevali](<../../gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/dunmari-pantheon/jeevali.md>) when wounded felt to them, personally. 


In DR 1748, [letters and a map](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/tales-and-stories/govir-s-story.md>) concerning the [expulsion of the Fraternity of the Empty Moon](<../../events/1600s/exile-of-fraternity-of-the-empty-moon.md>) from Tokra, uncovered by his colleague [Hada](<hada.md>), led Govir to investigate ruined fortifications north of Tokra, seeking signs of the gods' influence in the protection against the madness of [Pandemonium](<../../cosmology/spiritual-realms/pandemonium.md>) reported by eyewitnesses. Members of the [Fraternity of the Empty Moon](<../../groups/fraternity-of-the-empty-moon.md>) followed him back to the Archives, stole the map, and kidnapped him to explain its secrets. [During a month of captivity](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/tales-and-stories/govir-s-story.md>), he was afflicted with lycanthropy and feared he had killed Hada while transformed.

The [Dunmar Fellowship](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship.md>) [rescued Govir](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/session-notes/session-37-dufr.md>) on Jul 02, 1748 DR, and [Riswynn](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/riswynn.md>) [cured his lycanthropy](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/session-notes/session-38-dufr.md>) that evening. Returning to the Archives, he found neglected and misfiled records that might have warned of the Fraternity’s return. At the Fellowship’s urging, [Ardan](<ardan.md>) [entrusted him with putting the records in order](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/session-notes/session-41-dufr.md>) and provided funds for additional scribes on Jul 09, 1748 DR.




Over the next few months, Govir aided the [Dunmar Fellowship](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship.md>) through his knowledge of the Archives, tracing the [Cloak of Rainbows](<../../things/artifacts-of-power/cloak-of-rainbows.md>) [in the records](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/letters-and-notes/letter-from-govir.md>) of the [Aagir Mystai](<../../groups/dunmari-mystery-cults/aagir-mystai.md>) and [finding the poem](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/session-notes/session-71-dufr.md>) needed to free [Hralgar](<../giants/hralgar.md>).





[Sura](<sura.md>) also [asked Govir to search the records](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/scrying-and-spying/scrying-delwath-tollen-downtime.md#dream-messages-febmarch-1749>) for signs of [Agata](<../fey/agata.md>)’s influence on Dunmari history.
