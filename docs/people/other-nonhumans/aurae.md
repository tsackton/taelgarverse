# Aurae
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>

    A construct  
    { .bio }

</div>


Aurae is a human-sized clockwork and painted-porcelain automaton created as a synthesizer of research and information. Consisting of a torso and head without arms, hands, or legs, Aurae was stationed in the library outside the central prison of the [27th Room](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house.md>). Aurae has read every book in the library, helped researchers find information, and measured the passage of centuries by listening to unpreserved paper age.

Aurae chose a name and left the library with the adventurers described in [the party's exploration of the 27th Room](<../../campaigns/one-shots/lost-in-the-feywild/lost-in-the-feywild-episode-06.md>), carried in a makeshift backpack. There current whereabouts are unknown.
