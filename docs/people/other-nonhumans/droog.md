# Droog
:speaker:{ .middle } *(DROOG)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
- :octicons-info-24:{ .lg .middle } __Biographical Information__

    A goblin (he/him)  
    { .bio }

</div>


Droog is a fat, oddly friendly goblin cook who speaks in loud whispers. He was forced to prepare food and serve the redcaps at the Fortress of Ianto the Red.


After the death of Ianto, he fled and disappeared in the [Grey Mists](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/grey-mists.md>) on the borders of [Serenveil’s realm](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-s-edge.md>). Whether he survived is not known.
