Megren is a [dragonet](<../../creatures/bestiary/dragonets.md>) who lives in a well-maintained tower on the [Circular Island](<../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/circular-island.md>), with extensive grounds and a small village among the surrounding ruins. She received ownership of the tower from [Ra'ghemdros](<ra-ghemdros.md>) as a reward for bringing her a [fragment of Rai’s jade hand](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/treasure/jade-piece-of-rai-s-hand.md>) found on the mainland. Despite accepting this reward, she held little loyalty to the black dragon.
