Canopy, also known as the Eldest, is an awakened ceiba tree and the informal leader of the talking plants and animals of the [Awakened Jungle](<../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/awakened-jungle.md>). They remember the magical awakening that first granted awareness to the jungle’s inhabitants.


In the spring of DR 1749, Canopy was afflicted by chaotic corruption that spread through the jungle’s interconnected roots. On May 16, 1749 DR, the [Dunmar Fellowship](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship.md>) destroyed the corrupted vines surrounding them, allowing Canopy and the jungle to begin recovering.
