Mehrangeesa is a djinni from [Sulmana](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/plane-of-air/sulmana.md>), a city of the [Elemental Plane of Air](<../../cosmology/energy-realms/elemental-plane-of-air.md>). He was imprisoned in [Airion's Floating Tower](<../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/fiamatara-mountains/airion-s-floating-tower.md>) for many years, and later returned to the Plane of Air after he was freed by the [Silver Tempests](<../pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>). In gratitude for their aid, he assisted them in the [defense](<../../events/1700s/1747/battle-of-voltara.md>) of [Voltara](<../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/northern-provinces/voltara/voltara.md>) against [Grumella's Horde](<../../groups/orc-hordes/grumella-s-horde.md>).
