# Drubgrak
:speaker:{ .middle } *(DRUB-grak)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>

    { .bio }

</div>


Drubgrak is a winged kobold and Vikka’s sister. Both served Vraedhul in the [Plaguelands](<../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/plaguelands.md>) before mysterious events associated with the [War of the Ashen Horde](<../../events/1700s/1720/war-of-the-ashen-horde.md>) destroyed the dragon’s lair in DR 1720. They fled north with Vraedhul’s offspring, Krauzeth, but became disillusioned with him. Drubgrak led those who wanted to leave his service and live independently in the [Maerwyn Mountains](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/maerwyn-mountains.md>).


Near Marwick, Drubgrak and her followers surrendered to the [Heroes of Cleenseau](<../pcs/cleenseau/heroes-of-cleenseau.md>) and helped arrange the ambush that killed Krauzeth.
