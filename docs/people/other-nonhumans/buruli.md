Buruli is a [merfolk](<../../creatures/species/merfolk.md>) warlock associated with the mysterious entity [Gazankoa](<../extraplanar-powers/gazankoa.md>).


While investigating rumors of a garden of jade associated with merfolk off the coast of [Apporia](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/apporia/apporia.md>), the [Dunmar Fellowship](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship.md>) learned that Buruli had brought ruin to [Omi](<../../gazetteer/faraway-places/omi.md>) a decade earlier, in DR 1739. Through [visions shared by the Sentient Ocean](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/dreams-and-visions/visions-of-the-sentient-ocean.md>), they saw how his envy of the Garden of Names had led him to bargain with Gazankoa. Empowered by his patron, Buruli seized the [fragment of Rai’s jade hand](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/treasure/jade-piece-of-rai-s-hand.md>) that had strengthened the garden, and used its power to open and sustain a portal to the [Elemental Plane of Water](<../../cosmology/energy-realms/elemental-plane-of-water.md>). The [Sentient Ocean](<../extraplanar-powers/sentient-ocean.md>) sealed Omi behind a protective barrier to contain the invasion, while Buruli retreated through the portal with his followers and captives.

After [exploring Omi](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/session-notes/session-100-dufr.md>), the Dunmar Fellowship [crossed the portal](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/session-notes/session-101-dufr.md>) to the [Elemental Plane of Water](<../../cosmology/energy-realms/elemental-plane-of-water.md>), where they found prisoners forced to sort artifacts excavated from Omi, seeking clues to the Sentient Ocean’s origins. After rescuing [Milo Thistlefoot](<../halflings/milo-thistlefoot.md>), [Tilly Brineheart](<../halflings/tilly-brineheart.md>), and their surviving crew, they confronted Buruli in his temple to Gazankoa, built inside a vast leviathan skeleton. [Kenzo](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>) recovered the jade, and the Fellowship escaped over their rainbow bridge to [Vindristjarna](<../../things/ships/vindristjarna.md>). Buruli pursued them onto the ship above [Xurkhaz](<../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/xurkhaz/xurkhaz.md>), where he was killed by [Faldrak Bronzehammer](<../dwarves/faldrak-bronzehammer.md>) and [Delwath](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/delwath.md>).
