Ra’ghemdros, the Light Stealer and the Final Shadow, the Devourer of Drankor and the Last Empress of Hkar, the Eternal Night and the Enslaver of the Secret Sunset, is an ancient black [dragon](<../../creatures/species/dragons.md>) and a daughter of [Cha’mutte](<../extraplanar-powers/cha-mutte.md>). 



She ruled the [Circular Island](<../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/circular-island.md>) for more than seven centuries. She was an immense beast, with scales of an intense, unbroken black, her eyes luminous green. Along her left side, almost from shoulder to tail, a scar shimmered red and orange, like a sunset burned into her flesh. Her deadly breath mingled acid and darkness, and she could vanish from sight or become an incorporeal shadow to move with an incongruous stealth given her size. She was killed in DR 1749 by the [Dunmar Fellowship](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship.md>) and their allies. 

## Background

Many centuries ago, Ra'ghemdros served as a lieutenant for her father, and fought in his attack on [Amberglow](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/amberglow/amberglow.md>) against the [Cloudspinner](<../extraplanar-powers/archfey/cloudspinner.md>). Ra'ghemdros was badly wounded, and scarred, but the [Cloudspinner](<../extraplanar-powers/archfey/cloudspinner.md>) was captured and imprisoned. Later, when [Cha'mutte](<../extraplanar-powers/cha-mutte.md>) withdrew from the [Circular Island](<../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/circular-island.md>) following the destruction of [Drankor](<../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/drankor/drankor.md>), he left Ra’ghemdros in command, and charged with guarding [Cloudspinner](<../extraplanar-powers/archfey/cloudspinner.md>)'s prison. She established her lair beneath the ruined thermal baths of the [Concordia Pyrae](<../../groups/drankorian-societies/concordia-pyrae.md>), close to Cloudspinner’s prison. 



## Reign

Long after her father’s death, she remained in her lair, gathering the treasures of the island and obsessively keeping watch over her hated enemy, Cloudspinner, via a scrying mirror, until eventually all thought of the outside world faded from her mind. Beneath the baths, she constructed her domain, which opened into caverns of corrosive pools, acid geysers, and grasping tendrils of darkness. Her [hoard](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/hoards/ra-ghemdros-hoard.md>) filled many chambers, and was her second obsessive, after the Cloudspinner; she spent hours each day tending and rearranging the collection. She arranged her treasures with extraordinary care, sorted and displayed to please her. At the time of her death, her treasures included uncountable coins and thousands of pieces of jewelry, surrounded displays of ancient art and magical treasures, with fanciful displays of rings and earrings, arranged by color, decorating the walls. 

Despite her wealth, and grand titles, and contempt for mortals, she feared Apollyon and remained consumed by her hatred of Cloudspinner. She coveted trophies she was too cowardly to take for herself, especially treasures from Apollyon's tower, and was always willing to bargain with any who would to take risks on her behalf. 

Indeed, she considered all the treasure of the Circular Island her rightful possessions; she forced island's [dragonets](<../../creatures/bestiary/dragonets.md>) to compete for her favor by scouring the ruins of Alectia and beyond for new treasures for her hoard. Concealing treasure could mean death; bringing her a sufficiently pleasing gift could win a tower and the security of her protection. Generations of dragonets scoured the island for tribute, eventually venturing to the mainland as new treasures became scarce. Though some served her with devotion, many simply learned to flatter her, satisfy her demands, and keep out of her way. She was too lazy to emerge from her lair more than on rare occasions, but fear of her power sustained her rule.

## Death

Her rule ended in May 1749 DR, when the [Dunmar Fellowship](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship.md>) carried Cloudspinner’s crystal prison out of the chambers beneath the villa. Ra’ghemdros pursued them to the summit temples with her dragonet followers and chimeras. There, on [May 21](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/session-notes/session-110-dufr.md>), [Kenzo](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>) struck the killing blow. The long scar along her side cracked open, spilling the colors of sunset around the entire horizon as the sun rose. Her body crashed through the roof of the Temple of [the Night Queen](<../../gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/mos-numena-pantheon/the-night-queen.md>).
