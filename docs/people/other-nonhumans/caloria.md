Caloria was a [merfolk](<../../creatures/species/merfolk.md>) woman who fled [Buruli](<buruli.md>) and sought refuge with [Dorian Almare](<../chardonians/dorian-almare.md>) near [Tamaro Bay](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/apporia/tamaro-bay.md>).


When the [Dunmar Fellowship](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship.md>) met Dorian in DR 1749, he recalled Caloria entrusting him with a vial of strange water, asking him to keep it safe. His fragmented memories included two other merfolk pursuing her. The Fellowship found her bones and a golden bracelet among rocks just offshore; Dorian recognized the bracelet, confirming her death.
