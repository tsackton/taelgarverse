Ithu'rax was an aboleth active in the [Northwest Coast](<../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/northwest-coast.md>) region in the DR 1740s. He made a lair beneath ruined structures near a lake upriver from the village of [Suwi](<../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/suwi.md>), and used the mercenary group the [Havoc Host](<../../groups/havoc-host.md>) and psychically controlled servants in a series of kidnappings of unclear purpose. He was killed by the [Silver Tempests](<../pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>) in DR 1748.
