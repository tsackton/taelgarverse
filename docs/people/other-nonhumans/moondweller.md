Moondweller is a [Stoneborn](<../../creatures/species/stoneborn.md>) guide and mystic, based in the high peaks above [Am'khazar](<../../gazetteer/central-highlands/dwarven-kingdoms/am-khazar.md>). When [Morgana Frostclaw](<morgana-stoneclaw.md>) established herself over these mountains and brought endless ice and cold, he remained behind while his village fled. 

He met the [Silver Tempests](<../pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>), and helped orient them to the events in the high peaks before directing them towards [Vangebekkr](<../../gazetteer/central-highlands/vangebekkr.md>) and [Morgana Frostclaw](<morgana-stoneclaw.md>)'s lair.
