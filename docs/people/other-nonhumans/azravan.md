Azravan, known as the Prince of the Unconquered Air and the Duke of the Scouring Wind. Little is known about him, except that he is the patron of [Zadkai](<../other-humans/zadkai.md>), and he leads the [Scouring Wind](<../../groups/scouring-wind.md>), a group that seeks to erase humanity from the multiverse, blaming them collectively for the sins of wizards who enslaved genies.
