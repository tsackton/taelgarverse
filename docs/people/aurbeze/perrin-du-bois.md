![Perrin Du Bois](/taelgarverse/assets/perrin-du-bois.webp){align="right"; width="400"}Perrin is an Aurbeze beekeeper and father of four. He is a smallholder of his own farm, and has recently been elected to represent the scattered smallholds and isolated farms across the [Aurbez Plateau](<../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/aurbez-plateau.md>) in Alliance councils. 

Perrin sees himself as an  advocate for rural households, lending a steady if anxious voice to Alliance deliberations. He is regarded as a practical delegate who presses for civilian safety and realistic defenses for outlying farms.

Perrin is rarely seen without his straw hat, and has a weathered look of someone who works the land for a living. 

## Events
- Aug 07, 1749 DR: Joined the war council at [Three Wells](<../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/refounded-alliance-of-aurbez/three-wells.md>), where he cautioned against plans that gambled civilian safety on uncertain outcomes, while acknowledging the need to protect the Heartroot.
