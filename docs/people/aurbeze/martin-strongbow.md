![Martin](/taelgarverse/assets/martin.webp){align="right"; width="400"}Martin is an Isinguer human and a captain of the [Rangers](<../../groups/rangers.md>). He is a seasoned tactician and often serves as the coordinator of Ranger operations within the [Refounded Alliance of Aurbez](<../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/refounded-alliance-of-aurbez/refounded-alliance-of-aurbez.md>).

Martin is a well-built, dashing man in his 30s, with dark hair and a tidy beard. He generally wears chain mail and a river-green cloak. 

## Background

Martin was the eldest son of wildcat miners on the northern edge of the [Aurbez Plateau](<../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/aurbez-plateau.md>), raised with stories of his [Isinguer](<../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/isingue.md>) heritage. He grew up poor, and joined [Rangers](<../../groups/rangers.md>) young, with a zeal for the cause of [Isingue](<../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/isingue.md>) instilled by his parents. Although sometimes rash, his brilliant tactical acumen and daring style serve him well; he led numerous raids into the ash storms of the [Plaguelands](<../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/plaguelands.md>) during his 20s, making a name for himself across the [Refounded Alliance of Aurbez](<../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/refounded-alliance-of-aurbez/refounded-alliance-of-aurbez.md>). 

More recently, he has honed a reputation for discipline and clear‑headedness in command. Within [Rangers](<../../groups/rangers.md>) he is very popular with the rank-and-file, and widely deferred to on strategic questions, though at times he is too eager to hear all sides and encourage lengthy discussion.
