# Taquin
:speaker:{ .middle } *(tah-KEEN)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
- :octicons-info-24:{ .lg .middle } __Biographical Information__

    A [Zimka](<../../groups/cultures/zimka.md>) [human](<../../creatures/species/humans.md>)  
    { .bio }

</div>


Taquin was a resident of [Melusa](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/zefya-s-realm/melusa.md>) and the parent of [Zahara](<zahara.md>).


In [January 1730](<../../campaigns/one-shots/into-the-chasm/into-the-chasm-episode-04.md>), a soul identifying itself as Taquin spoke to [Finnan](<../pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/finnan-oakstride.md>), through the infant [Samir](<samir.md>). Its last memory was falling toward [the labyrinth](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/zefya-s-realm/zeyfa-s-labyrinth.md>), looking up at Zahara, who stood with tears on their face and a look of determination. Taquin remembered nothing after the fall; how the soul came to inhabit Samir is unexplained, but apparently not uncommon in [Melusa](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/zefya-s-realm/melusa.md>).
