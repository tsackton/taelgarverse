Iskander is an older Zimka man from [Melusa](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/zefya-s-realm/melusa.md>), balding and somewhat heavyset. He is an ally of [Zahara](<zahara.md>), and part of their dissident circle of rebels. 


In January of DR 1730, he helped the [Chasm Explorers](<../pcs/other-pcs/into-the-chasm/chasm-explorers.md>) orchestrate an escape from Melusa. He helped gather  villagers willing to leave, and joined those [evacuated from Melusa](<../../campaigns/one-shots/into-the-chasm/into-the-chasm-episode-06.md>).
