The Ravager of Winter is the name used by an orc war leader in northern [Zimkova](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/zimkova.md>).


In August 1729, her horde emerged from the [Sentinels](<../../gazetteer/sentinel-range.md>) and rampaged across northern Zimkova. On 28 August, she was killed and her army defeated in the forests east of the [Berze](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/rivers/teft-watershed/berze.md>) by dwarves from [Nidzahar](<../../gazetteer/central-highlands/dwarven-kingdoms/nidzahar.md>), supported by a substantial [Stoneborn](<../../creatures/species/stoneborn.md>) company.
