Jorundr was a storm giant wanderer and student of history. He was born under the [Green Sea](<../../gazetteer/green-sea.md>). Eager, impulsive, and optimistic, he left his underwater home while young and wandered widely, especially across the [deserts east](<../../gazetteer/faraway-places/great-desert.md>) of the [Drankorian Empire](<../../history/historical-realms/drankorian-empire.md>). He became a friend and pupil of [Hralgar](<hralgar.md>), spending a century or more with him, and hoped for a new age of harmony between humans and giants.

After learning that Hralgar had been imprisoned, Jorundr set out to rescue him. He died north of [Drankor](<../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/drankor/drankor.md>) in DR 1059, in what is now the Garamjala Desert, caught in the [magical catastrophe](<../../events/1000s/1059/first-plague.md>) that brought about the [Fall of Drankor](<../../events/1000s/1059/fall-of-drankor.md>).
