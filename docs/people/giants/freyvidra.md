Freyvidra was a frost giant who ruled [Kaldhalla](<../../gazetteer/northern-green-sea/kaldhalla.md>) from DR 1552 to DR 1673, after seizing the kingdom from [Storvalda](<storvalda.md>) in the aftermath of the [Great War](<../../events/1500s/great-war.md>). She was an ally of the children of [Vimfrost](<../historical-figures/vimfrost.md>); she encouraged raids against dwarves, stoneborn, the Vos, and the Vargaldi, collecting tribute she claimed was owed as compensation for Vimfrost’s death. During her reign, [Isenborg](<../../gazetteer/northern-green-sea/isenborg.md>) fell into disrepair, and resentment grew among the giants, as she focused on reparations to the dragons to the north. 

In DR 1673, [Brimskarda](<brimskarda.md>) overthrew her in a coup, defeating her in single combat. She was killed during the overthrow.
