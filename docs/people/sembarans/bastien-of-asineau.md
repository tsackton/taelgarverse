Bastien is a former [Army of the West](<../../groups/sembaran-army/army-of-the-west.md>) soldier who came to [Asineau](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/asineau.md>) in early May 1720 seeking work as a guard. His husband, [Cedric](<cedric.md>), had been killed by the undead while Bastien was away with the army. He joined Asineau's guard and hoped [El](<el.md>) could help him with his grief. [Celyn](<../pcs/cleenseau/celyn.md>) spent time with him and made a point of seeing that he had what he needed.
