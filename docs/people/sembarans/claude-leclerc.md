Claude Leclerc is General of the [Army of the West](<../../groups/sembaran-army/army-of-the-west.md>). Previously a colonel commanding its South Watch, he unofficially supported [Ida Rosfeld](<ida-rosfeld.md>) in returning to [Cleenseau](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/cleenseau/cleenseau.md>) during the undead attacks of early DR 1720. By April that year, he had been promoted to command the army.
