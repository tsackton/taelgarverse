Samuel Cordwaner is [Odo's](<odo-cordwaner.md>) younger brother and an apprentice smith in [Asineau](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/asineau.md>), training under [Roaric Ferrystone](<../dwarves/roaric-ferrystone.md>). Presumed dead after fleeing zombies, he was found hiding in the hills and escorted to safety in early DR 1720.
