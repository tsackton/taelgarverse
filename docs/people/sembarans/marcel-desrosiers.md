Marcel Desrosiers is a Sembaran human and captain of the household guard of [Jacques Bellemont](<jacques-bellemont.md>), Duke of Wisford. During the [Undead Attacks in Sembara](<../../events/1700s/1720/01/undead-attacks-in-sembara.md>) in DR 1720, he coordinated with soldiers and regimental commanders in the duke’s army and was instrumental in restoring order along the [Enst](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/rivers/wistel-enst-watershed/enst.md>).
