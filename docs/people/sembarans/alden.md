# Alden

    An alien fungal entity (he/him)  
    Died October 7th, 1740  
    { .bio }

    Lived in the [Dandelion House](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/dandelion-house.md>), the [Varrow Forest](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/varrow-forest.md>), the [Duchy of Telham](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/duchy-of-telham.md>)

Alden is an alien fungal child of the [Prisoner in the 27th Room](<../other-nonhumans/prisoner-in-the-27th-room.md>), who appeared as a human servant and guide in [Lord Hulda](<lord-hulda.md>)'s employ at [Dandelion House](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/dandelion-house.md>). He is short, round, pale, quiet, and stronger than he looks. Although attentive and usually smiling, he rarely volunteers much conversation. He has a pronounced fear of fire, keeping his distance from campfires and open flame. He has a deep, almost rapturous, affection for Dandelion House and its dandelion imagery.

Alden, along with many other fungal aliens, was killed by [Lord Hulda](<lord-hulda.md>) when the [Prisoner in the 27th Room](<../other-nonhumans/prisoner-in-the-27th-room.md>) was set free and [disappeared to a strange alien realm](<../../campaigns/one-shots/lost-in-the-feywild/lost-in-the-feywild-episode-07.md>).
