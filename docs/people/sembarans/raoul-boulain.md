Raoul Boulain was a Sembaran human cleric and elder of [The Night Queen](<../../gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/mos-numena-pantheon/the-night-queen.md>), prominent in the goddess’s councils. During the [Undead Attacks in Sembara](<../../events/1700s/1720/01/undead-attacks-in-sembara.md>) in DR 1720, he, [Lenora Belles](<lenora-belles.md>), and their companions played a central role in defeating Malach, destroying his phylactery and killing the lich. Raoul died on March 20, DR 1720.
