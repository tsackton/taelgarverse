Charlotte is a Sembaran actor with [Mahaut's Miracle Players](<../../groups/mahaut-s-miracle-players.md>), who formerly traveled with [Celyn](<../pcs/cleenseau/celyn.md>). After the siege of [Fellburn](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/heartlands/fellburn.md>), she met Celyn at the Oracle of Hope while traveling with [Hugh Cosson](<hugh-cosson.md>) and delivered a cryptic message from a stranger who had told her fortune.
