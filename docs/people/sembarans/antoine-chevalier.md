Antoine Chevalier is the lord of [Maudorville](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/maudorville.md>). He takes little interest in affairs beyond his manor and is slow to respond to problems in the surrounding settlements. He is overly solicitous toward celebrated visitors.
