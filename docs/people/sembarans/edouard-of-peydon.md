# Edouard
:speaker:{ .middle } *(ay-DWAR)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>

    A [Sembaran](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/sembara.md>) [human](<../../creatures/species/humans.md>) (he/him)  
    Died DR 1720  
    { .bio }

</div>


Edouard was the ferryman at [Peydon](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/peydon.md>) and a member of [Gareth’s](<gareth-of-tollen.md>) criminal gang. He later worked for the [Midnight Lady](<../fey/midnight-lady.md>), helping draw travelers into her debt. He was killed after escaping custody following her death.
