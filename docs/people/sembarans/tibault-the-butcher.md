Tibault was a butcher in [Peydon](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/peydon.md>) and a member of [Gareth of Tollen](<gareth-of-tollen.md>)’s criminal gang. A burly man with rough hands, he used his shop as a front for stolen goods and money laundering and handled the more violent side of the gang’s business.

After the [Midnight Lady](<../fey/midnight-lady.md>)’s death, Tibault became more concerned with self-preservation. He was killed attempting to escape custody by the [Heroes of Cleenseau](<../pcs/cleenseau/heroes-of-cleenseau.md>).
