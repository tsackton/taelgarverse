Juliana Westby is the young lady of [Cranford](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cranford.md>). She inherited the position after her mother died during the [undead attacks of early DR 1720](<../../events/1700s/1720/01/undead-attacks-in-sembara.md>). Later that winter, she was involved in a complex murder case -- one of her suitors, [Lambert Talwrey](<lambert-talwrey.md>), was murdered by [Areschera](<../fey/areschera.md>), a member of the [Gloomshaper's](<../extraplanar-powers/archfey/lord-umbraeth.md>) court. Areschera was impersonating her other suitor, [Tobias of Cranford](<tobias-of-cranford.md>), who was almost executed for the crime by [Thomas Dyerson](<thomas-dyerson.md>) until the timely intervention of the [Heroes of Cleenseau](<../pcs/cleenseau/heroes-of-cleenseau.md>). After [Tobias of Cranford](<tobias-of-cranford.md>) was exonerated they were married in the fall of 1720.

This incident was one of several significant fey incursions into the [Barony of Aveil](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/barony-of-aveil.md>) during the winter of 1720, and concern around these fey incursions is said to have motivated [Elaine II](<../historical-figures/sembaran-royalty/elaine-ii.md>) when she appointed Cadfael de Dufferin, from [Tyrwingha](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/tyrwingha/tyrwingha.md>), as the baron of [Aveil](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/barony-of-aveil.md>).
