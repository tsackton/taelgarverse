Tobias of Cranford is the husband of [Juliana Westby](<juliana-westby.md>), lady of [Cranford](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cranford.md>). They married in the autumn of DR 1720.

Earlier that year, the shapeshifter [Areschera](<../fey/areschera.md>) impersonated Tobias while murdering Juliana’s other suitors, [Lambert Talwrey](<lambert-talwrey.md>) and [Piers](<piers-of-houille.md>). Tobias was falsely accused and nearly executed by [Thomas Dyerson](<thomas-dyerson.md>) before the intervention of the [Heroes of Cleenseau](<../pcs/cleenseau/heroes-of-cleenseau.md>) led to his exoneration.
