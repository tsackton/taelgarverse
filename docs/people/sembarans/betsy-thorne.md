The daughter of [Jon Thorne](<jon-thorne.md>), cousin of [Beatrix Thorne](<beatrix-thorne.md>) and a member of the Cleenseau town watch.

In late April 1720, Betsy left [Rosalind's](<rosalind-essford.md>) guard for [Asineau](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/asineau.md>), where she joined the guard. She wanted a fresh start away from comparison with her cousin and felt that Ames, her former captain, was a poor teacher.
