# Major Edmund Hartwell
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>

    A [Sembaran](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/sembara.md>) [human](<../../creatures/species/humans.md>)  
    { .bio }

</div>


Edmund Hartwell commanded the Enst Regiment of the [Army of the West](<../../groups/sembaran-army/army-of-the-west.md>). On January 8, 1720, he ordered its detached garrisons to march to [Dunmore](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/western-marches/dunmore.md>) under General [Ilsa Lemoine's](<ilsa-lemoine.md>) orders. 
By April DR 1720, [Ida Rosfeld](<ida-rosfeld.md>) had been promoted to command the Enst Regiment.
