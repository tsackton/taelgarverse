Phillip Evynwood is a wealthy timber merchant based in [Aslain](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/aslain.md>), with strong connections to [Rinburg](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/rinburg.md>) and the d’Aslain family. He grew up in [Ashcombe](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/ashcombe.md>) and is the younger son of [Ameline Evynwood](<ameline-evynwood.md>), its lady.
