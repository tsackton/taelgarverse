Lenora Belles is a paladin of [The Night Queen](<../../gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/mos-numena-pantheon/the-night-queen.md>) from [Embry](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/heartlands/embry.md>), prominent in the goddess’s councils. She rides a jet-black pegasus named Tinugin.

During the [undead attacks of DR 1720](<../../events/1700s/1720/01/undead-attacks-in-sembara.md>), Lenora, [Raoul Boulain](<raoul-boulain.md>), and their companions played the central role in defeating Malach. They destroyed his phylactery and killed the lich.
