Arnault Daverre was the husband of Emeric Daverre, lord of [Peydon](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/peydon.md>). He studied the old [Kestavo](<../../gods-and-religions/religions/northern-folk-religions/kestavo.md>) traditions and collected an inscribed stone associated with them. He died during the [Undead Attacks in Sembara](<../../events/1700s/1720/01/undead-attacks-in-sembara.md>).
