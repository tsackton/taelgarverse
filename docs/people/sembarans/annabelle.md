# Annabelle
:speaker:{ .middle } *(ah-nah-BELL)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>

    A [Sembaran](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/sembara.md>) [human](<../../creatures/species/humans.md>) (she/her)  
    { .bio }

</div>


Annabelle is a steely-eyed warrior devoted to [The Night Queen](<../../gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/mos-numena-pantheon/the-night-queen.md>) and a companion of [Raoul Boulain](<raoul-boulain.md>), [Lenora Belles](<lenora-belles.md>), and Lucien of the Night Queen. She defended [Sembara](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/sembara.md>) during the [Undead Attacks in Sembara](<../../events/1700s/1720/01/undead-attacks-in-sembara.md>). After the attacks, she helped destroy Malach’s phylactery and defeat the lich.
