Bartholomew Meeke is a tin trader who spent time in [Aslain](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/aslain.md>). Enchanted by [The Hunter](<../fey/the-hunter.md>), he carried a fey letter intended for the [Heroes of Cleenseau](<../pcs/cleenseau/heroes-of-cleenseau.md>) while confused and disoriented. He later recovered in the care of the halflings in Aslain, where [Callie Riverstone](<../halflings/callie-riverstone.md>) sheltered him. The two have a friendly relationship.
