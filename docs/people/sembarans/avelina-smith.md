An important smith and leader of the metalworking community of [Cleenseau](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/cleenseau/cleenseau.md>). She trained with dwarves in her youth.

After injuring her shoulder and right arm in the troubles of 1720, Avelina left her Cleenseau weaponsmithing shop to her journeymen. She went to [Asineau](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/asineau.md>) to spend time with its dwarven smiths and became the manor's armoury master.
