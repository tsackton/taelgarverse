![Jonathon Henwyn](/taelgarverse/assets/jonathon-henwyn.webp){align="right"; width="320"}The steward of the [Essford Manor](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/cleenseau/essford-manor.md>) in [Cleenseau](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/cleenseau/cleenseau.md>), Jonathon is responsible for its bookkeeping and upkeep, as well as collecting the rents from farmers and other manorial administration. 

He has a keen interest in history, and is a competent clerk and accountant. He took over as steward from his father-in-law in 1715. His wife is a childhood friend of [Rosalind](<rosalind-essford.md>) and assists him. They and their three children live at [Essford Manor](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/cleenseau/essford-manor.md>).
