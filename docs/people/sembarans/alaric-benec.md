Alaric Benec is the Lord Mayor of [Rinburg](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/rinburg.md>) and an able merchant who built his career within the city’s Mercantile Guild. During the [Undead Attacks in Sembara](<../../events/1700s/1720/01/undead-attacks-in-sembara.md>), he grew desperate, and many in Rinburg felt his attempts to protect the city were inadequate.
