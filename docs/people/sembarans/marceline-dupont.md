Marceline Dupont is the Captain of the Horse for the [Barony of Aveil](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/barony-of-aveil.md>), based in [Veltor](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/veltor.md>). She often travels across the barony on the Baroness’s business. A career soldier and brilliant cavalry commander, she is fiercely loyal to the soldiers under her command. She is a practical warrior, but out of her depth when facing supernatural threats, frightened by enemies that tactics and steel cannot defeat.
