Pakrinek was a kenku warrior of [Kemeko Monastery](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/kemeko-monastery.md>). 


In January 1730, Pakrinek accompanied the [Vejo Vaikai](<../../groups/vejo-vaikai.md>) expedition led by [Eidrikas](<eidrikas.md>) into the [Great Chasm](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/great-chasm.md>) as one of the warriors assigned to protect the mystics. He was killed by orcs at a runic mechanism in the chasm.
