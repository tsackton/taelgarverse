Victorine Rosseau is the Chief Magistrate of the [Barony of Aveil](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/barony-of-aveil.md>), based in [Veltor](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/veltor.md>), where she has served for decades. She is strongly committed to justice and procedure. Outwardly loyal to [Baroness Isabeau](<isabeau-d-aslain.md>), she is wary of the Baroness’s hands-off approach and the strange events surrounding her rule, and willing to clash with authority to maintain her sense of justice. Her three assistant magistrates include [Thomas Dyerson](<thomas-dyerson.md>).
