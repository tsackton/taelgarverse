Sarabeth Asa was a scout and spellcaster who distinguished herself in the [Third Hobgoblin War (Sembara)](<../../events/1600s/third-hobgoblin-war-sembara.md>), receiving the [Manor of Asineau](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/manor-of-asineau.md>) as a reward. Originally from a small village in the [Mostreve Hills](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/mostreve-hills.md>), near the source of the [Clavert](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/rivers/wistel-enst-watershed/clavert.md>) in the [Barony of Brumecliff](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/borderlands/barony-of-brumecliff.md>), she refounded [Asineau](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/asineau.md>) after the war, joined by refugees from her home village.

She never settled into manorial life and disappeared in DR 1667, leaving behind her wife and young heir, [Lucas Asa](<lucas-asa.md>).
