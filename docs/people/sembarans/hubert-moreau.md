# Hubert Moreau
:speaker:{ .middle } *(oo-BEHR moh-ROH)*

    { .bio }

</div>


Hubert Moreau was the intended heir to the barony of [Fellburn](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/heartlands/fellburn.md>) and is the twin brother of [Elise Moreau](<elise-moreau.md>). During the [undead attacks of DR 1720](<../../events/1700s/1720/01/undead-attacks-in-sembara.md>), he panicked and ordered the city gates closed, leaving his father and farmers outside to be killed.

On January 25, 1720, [Jacques Bellemont](<jacques-bellemont.md>) awarded the barony to Elise and exiled Hubert from the Duchy of Wisford, permitting him to return when he had found bravery and better judgment.
