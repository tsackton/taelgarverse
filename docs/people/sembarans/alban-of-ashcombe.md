    Based in [Ashcombe](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/ashcombe.md>), the [Barony of Aveil](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/barony-of-aveil.md>), [Sembara](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/sembara.md>)

Alban is the current administrator of [Ashcombe](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/ashcombe.md>)'s temple to [The Wildling](<../../gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/mos-numena-pantheon/the-wildling.md>), and [Rufus](<rufus-of-ashcombe.md>), his adopted son, serves as his apprentice. Alban lost a leg in an accident several years ago. He remains a repository of village wisdom, though he tends toward bitterness about his injury.
