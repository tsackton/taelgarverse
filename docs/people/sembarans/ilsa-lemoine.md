# General Ilsa Lemoine

    A [Sembaran](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/sembara.md>) [human](<../../creatures/species/humans.md>) (she/her)

Ilsa Lemoine commanded the [Army of the West](<../../groups/sembaran-army/army-of-the-west.md>). In January 1720, during the undead crisis, she ordered companies and garrisons not already marching to [Dunmore](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/western-marches/dunmore.md>) to depart for the city. By April DR 1720, [Claude Leclerc](<claude-leclerc.md>) had been promoted to command the army.
