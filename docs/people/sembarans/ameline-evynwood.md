![Lady Ameline Evynwood](/taelgarverse/assets/lady-ameline-evynwood.webp){align="left"; width="200"} Ameline Evynwood is the lady of [Ashcombe](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/ashcombe.md>). During the fey attacks of DR 1720, she accompanied its displaced villagers to [Aslain](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/aslain.md>) and sought support from her family connections there. After the Crisis of Malach, she returned with the villagers and has been dedicated to rebuilding. 

Ameline is a widow whose sons have left home. Her older son, Richard, was part of the retinue of [Isabeau D'Aslain](<isabeau-d-aslain.md>) but has returned home after the Crisis of Malach. Her younger son, [Phillip Evynwood](<phillip-evynwood.md>), is based in [Aslain](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/aslain.md>) in the household of the [d'Aslain](<../../groups/sembaran-noble-houses/d-aslains.md>) family.

She is sharp and dedicated to her people, but the wider crisis of DR 1720 seems to overmatch her.
