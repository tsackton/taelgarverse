Brigitte “Brick” was an enforcer in [Gareth of Tollen](<gareth-of-tollen.md>)’s criminal gang in [Peydon](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/peydon.md>), collecting debts and roughing up troublemakers. She was a towering, broad-shouldered woman with a broken nose and a thick forearm scarred in a knife fight.

Brigitte had worked as a carpenter’s apprentice but was dismissed after breaking a man’s arm in a tavern brawl. Gareth recruited her for her strength.

She was killed attempting to escape custody by the [Heroes of Cleenseau](<../pcs/cleenseau/heroes-of-cleenseau.md>).
