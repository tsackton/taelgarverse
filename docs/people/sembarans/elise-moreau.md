Elise Moreau is the Baroness of [Fellburn](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/heartlands/fellburn.md>) and the twin sister of [Hubert Moreau](<hubert-moreau.md>). During the [undead attacks of DR 1720](<../../events/1700s/1720/01/undead-attacks-in-sembara.md>), she and others countermanded Hubert’s order to keep the city gates closed and rode out to rescue survivors.

On January 25, 1720, [Jacques Bellemont](<jacques-bellemont.md>) awarded Elise the barony and exiled Hubert for abandoning the people outside the gates, including their father, who was killed.
