    Based in the [Dandelion House](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/dandelion-house.md>), the [Varrow Forest](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/varrow-forest.md>), the [Duchy of Telham](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/duchy-of-telham.md>)

Holda is a scarred, powerfully built man in his mid-to-late forties, with a grizzled gray beard and the wary bearing of an experienced fighter. He spent many years as an adventurer, sellsword, guard, and mercenary around [Sembara](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/sembara.md>), [Tollen](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/tollen/tollen.md>), and the wider [Green Sea](<../../gazetteer/green-sea.md>). 

After killing a young dragon near Tollen in a fight that left the rest of his company dead, he received a brittle old deed that named him heir to Dandelion House and its treasures, but only if he brought a treaty beyond the hidden door to an end. 

He is proud, bitter, resentful at times, and deeply invested in becoming lord of Dandelion House.
