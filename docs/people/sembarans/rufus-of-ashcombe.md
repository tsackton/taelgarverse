# Rufus

    A [Sembaran](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/sembara.md>) [human](<../../creatures/species/humans.md>) (he/him)  
    { .bio }

    Based in [Ashcombe](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/ashcombe.md>), the [Barony of Aveil](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/barony-of-aveil.md>), [Sembara](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/sembara.md>)

Rufus is the adopted son and apprentice of [Alban of Ashcombe](<alban-of-ashcombe.md>), administrator of the temple to [The Wildling](<../../gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/mos-numena-pantheon/the-wildling.md>) in [Ashcombe](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/ashcombe.md>). A trans man originally from a small village along the [Enst](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/rivers/wistel-enst-watershed/enst.md>), he is rapidly learning Alban’s knowledge of local traditions. He rarely speaks about his family background, and his opinions are respected by villagers, particularly young families. Some villagers say he occasionally displays a dark anger.
