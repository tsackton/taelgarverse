:octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } Undead form destroyed by the [Addermarch Mercenaries](<../pcs/addermarch/addermarch-mercenaries.md>) on April 28th, 1715 in [Torvaine Watch](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/torvaine-watch.md>)  


Bartoz was the master smith and informal quartermaster of [Torvaine Watch](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/torvaine-watch.md>). When the cursed [Serpentine Dagger](<../../things/magic-items/serpentine-dagger.md>) surfaced, temple steward [Wulfram](<wulfram.md>) brought it to Bartoz, who wanted to destroy it immediately, suggesting he melt it in his forge. Wulfram preferred to send it to a temple for examination, and got his way, eventually leading to Sir [Einrik Arpaad](<einrik-arpaad.md>) corruption under the weapon’s malign influence, and Bartoz's death at his commander's hand. He rose again as an [undead](<../../creatures/bestiary/undead.md>), clinging to the forge in a grim echo of the responsibility he refused to abandon, until the [Addermarch Mercenaries](<../pcs/addermarch/addermarch-mercenaries.md>) cut him down and put him permanently to rest.

- Apr 28, 1715 DR: Undead remains defeated by [Fazoth de Brune](<../pcs/addermarch/fazoth-de-brune.md>), [Drou](<../pcs/addermarch/drou.md>), and [Yvan Greenrabbit](<../pcs/addermarch/yvan-greenrabbit.md>) during the watch’s liberation.
