Emilia Vetella is the Lord Admiral of the Chardonian Navy and a trusted advisor to [Mitus Verina Auratan](<mitus-verina-auratan.md>). In DR 1749, Emilia was appointed admiral of the fleet for the [Chardon-Dunmar War](<../../events/1700s/chardon-dunmar-war.md>), whose naval campaign culminated in a Chardonian victory outside [Darba](<../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/coastal-dunmar/darba/darba.md>).
