Quintus Percomia is a wealthy caravan master and [chalyte](<../../things/materials/chalyte.md>) trader based in [Voltara](<../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/northern-provinces/voltara/voltara.md>). He dresses well, and keeps his short black hair and beard neatly trimmed. 

His routes connect [Voltara](<../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/northern-provinces/voltara/voltara.md>) with [Chardon](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/chardon.md>), carrying ore south and returning with grain, wine, salt, and other staple goods. He is known to purchase collectables, gems, and other valuables at times.
