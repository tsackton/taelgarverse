General Soria Caronius is the current commander of the [Fort Zarnato](<../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/northern-provinces/fort-zarnato.md>), the northeastern-most fortification in the [Chardonian Empire](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardonian-empire.md>). She is a middle aged woman, heavily armored and tough, with a bit of a chip on her shoulder about the challenges and value of defending this forgotten corner of the world. 


In late DR 1747 and early DR 1748, Soria was faced with raids on Fort Zarnato's winter food supplies, from the north. The raids were eventually traced by the [Silver Tempests](<../pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>) to bullywugs displaced by the dragon [Nymthrax](<../other-nonhumans/nymthrax.md>); the crisis ended after Nymthrax was killed.
