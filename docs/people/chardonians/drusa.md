Drusa Ioniallus is a Chardonian mage and a captain in the Mage Guard. Originally from a high-ranking family, she was sent to Dunmar by Magistros [Mitus Verina Auratan](<mitus-verina-auratan.md>) at the request of [Nayan Karnas](<../dunmari/nayan-karnas.md>) and seconded to the Dunmari army. In DR 1748, she was serving under Commander [Illyan](<../dunmari/illyan.md>) in [Tokra](<../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/central-dunmar/tokra/tokra.md>). 


Drusa encountered the Dunmar Fellowship in [July 1748](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/session-notes/session-36-dufr.md>). She does not particularly like them, given their attempt to scry on [Illyan](<../dunmari/illyan.md>), which Drusa detected, and their refusal to agree to simple magic protocols.
