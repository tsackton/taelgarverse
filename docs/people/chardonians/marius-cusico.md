Marius Cusico is a Chardonian general of considerable renown. He was a former member of the mageguard; though never the most powerful wizard he was skilled in command and worked his way up the ranks. In DR 1749, he was appointed to command the legion in the [Chardon-Dunmar War](<../../events/1700s/chardon-dunmar-war.md>), proposed as a compromise acceptable to both [Mitus Verina Auratan](<mitus-verina-auratan.md>) and [Fausto](<fausto.md>). He was disoriented and confused after returning from the war, and soon suffered a mysterious collapse that left him unable to speak. He now lies in the care of the priests of the Hospital of the Mother.


Marius had been secretly controlled by [Fausto](<fausto.md>) through the [Crown of Purity](<../../things/artifacts-of-power/crown-of-purity.md>). On May 24, 1749 DR, [Kenzo](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>) took control of the crown and compelled him to halt the assault on [Songara](<../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/central-dunmar/songara.md>) and withdraw from [Dunmar](<../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/dunmar.md>), a command supported by [Riswynn](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/riswynn.md>) after she took control. His eventual release from the crown’s influence left him struggling to recover from prolonged magical domination.
