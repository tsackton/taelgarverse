Marius Cusico is a Chardonian general and former member of the mageguard. In DR 1749, he was appointed to command the land forces in the [Chardon-Dunmar War](<../../events/1700s/chardon-dunmar-war.md>) as a compromise acceptable to both [Mitus Verina Auratan](<mitus-verina-auratan.md>) and [Fausto](<fausto.md>). Shortly after returning from the war, he suffered a mysterious collapse that left him unable to speak, and he now lies in the care of the priests of the Hospital of the Mother.


Marius had been secretly controlled by [Fausto](<fausto.md>) through the [Crown of Purity](<../../things/artifacts-of-power/crown-of-purity.md>). On DR 1749-05-24, [Kenzo](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/kenzo.md>) took control of the crown and compelled him to halt the assault on [Songara](<../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/central-dunmar/songara.md>) and withdraw from [Dunmar](<../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/dunmar/dunmar.md>). His eventual release from the crown’s influence left him struggling to recover from prolonged magical domination.
