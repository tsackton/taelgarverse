# Francisca

Francisca, mother of [Xavier](<xavier.md>) and [Adina](<adina.md>), is a poor seamstress who does piece-work sewing and mending out of the small apartment she shares with her children near the [Foundry Market](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/foundry-market.md>). In good times, she has a small stall on the edge of the [Foundry Market](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/foundry-market.md>); in bad times, she hides in her apartment and yells at her children. She is middle aged, but looks older. 

Her chief hope is that her children will have a better life than she has had.
