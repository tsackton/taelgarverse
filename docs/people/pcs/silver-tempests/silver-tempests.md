# The Silver Tempests

The Silver Tempests are a renowned adventuring party based out of [Voltara](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/northern-provinces/voltara/voltara.md>), on the northern frontier of the [Chardonian Empire](<../../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardonian-empire.md>). Originally hired by [Lyra](<../../chardonians/lyra.md>) to seek the [Elemental Scrolls of Airion](<../../../things/artifacts-of-power/elemental-scrolls-of-airion.md>), the Silver Tempests became famous after their role in [Grumella's War](<../../../events/1700s/1747/grumella-s-war.md>), and established an elaborate manor, the [Tempest Towers](<../../../campaigns/great-library-campaign/tempest-towers.md>) in the center of [Voltara](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/northern-provinces/voltara/voltara.md>), near the baths and market. 

After vanishing for several years, during which they traveled to [Cairn Dor](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/cairn-dor/cairn-dor.md>) and freed many [Lizardfolk](<../../../creatures/species/lizardfolk.md>) who had been captured by [Yeshara](<../../extraplanar-powers/yeshara.md>) during the [Lizardfolk Village Disappearances](<../../../events/1700s/lizardfolk-village-disappearances.md>), they returned to find Voltara changed, no longer part of the [Chardonian Empire](<../../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardonian-empire.md>). 

The current members of the Silver Tempests are:

- [Adrik](<adrik.md>) Boulderbeard, a lightning-touched barbarian from the [Yuvanti Mountains](<../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/yuvanti-mountains.md>), far to the south, and an original member of the group.
- [Samso](<samso.md>), a lizardfolk druid from the [Yeraad Watershed](<../../../gazetteer/major-rivers/yeraad-watershed/yeraad-watershed.md>), and an original member of the group.
- [Mabist](<mabist.md>), a changeling warlock from the [Feywild](<../../../cosmology/feywild.md>), with a mysterious past, who befriended [Dinia](<../../other-humans/dinia.md>) and joined the group during [their rescue of Dinia from an aboleth](<../../../campaigns/great-library-campaign/session-notes/great-library-session-notes-arc-4.md#adventure-19-saving-grandma-yoda-this-shits-kinda-fishy>).
- [Blue](<blue.md>), a mysterious traveler from far to the east, who joined the group before their [adventures in Cairn Dor](<../../../campaigns/great-library-campaign/session-notes/great-library-session-notes-arc-5.md>).

Former members of the Silver Tempest are:

- [Brelith](<brelith.md>), a dwarven cleric from [Zarkandur](<../../../gazetteer/central-highlands/dwarven-kingdoms/zarkandur.md>), who retired to open a [restaurant](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/northern-provinces/voltara/the-hero-s-feast.md>) in [Voltara](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/northern-provinces/voltara/voltara.md>); he now operates a refuge and hall of stories in the western foothills of the [Fiatara Mountains](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/fiamatara-mountains/fiatara-mountains.md>) with [Dinia](<../../other-humans/dinia.md>)
- [Aelar](<aelar.md>), an elven shadow monk, who grew up an orphan wandering around Chardon, and has since grown so stealthy that he disappeared into the background

The Silver Tempests traveled with three other adventurers in the past:

- [Scordith](<scordith.md>), a human sorcerer, who briefly joined the Silver Tempests on a few early quests;
- [Aglath](<aglath.md>), a stoneborn warrior, who was born far away, and left to find his kin in the [Sentinels](<../../../gazetteer/sentinel-range.md>) before turning to a life of crime;
- [Alton](<alton.md>), a halfling rogue and proprietor of a dive bar outside [Voltara](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/northern-provinces/voltara/voltara.md>) known for offering recreational axe throwing to its patrons. Alton was originally hired by [Lyra](<../../chardonians/lyra.md>) and traveled briefly with the Silver Tempests in their early days, but left her service and traveled alone for some time before rejoining the group in the [Goldpeak Mines](<../../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/fiamatara-mountains/goldpeak-mines.md>).
