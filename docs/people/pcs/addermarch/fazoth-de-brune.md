![Fazoth Full](/taelgarverse/assets/fazoth-full.webp){align="right"; width="400"}Fazoth de Brune, a younger son of the wealthy de Brune family, was born on his family's manor on the outskirts of [Eskbridge](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/heartlands/eskbridge.md>), a fortified town along the [Enst](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/rivers/wistel-enst-watershed/enst.md>) river. He grew up grew up with a life of privilege, thanks to his family's wealth and position. 

At the age of 22, he left [Sembara](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/sembara.md>) for [Addermarch](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/addermarch.md>).
