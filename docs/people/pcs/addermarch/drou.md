![Drou Large](/taelgarverse/assets/drou-large.webp){align="right"; width="400"}Drou is a graceful dancer, singer, and musician from [Orenlas](<../../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/orenlas/orenlas.md>), who has wandered around [Tyrwingha](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/tyrwingha/tyrwingha.md>) and [Addermarch](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/addermarch.md>) bringing joy to people's lives through music and dance.

She was born n DR 1637, part of the 37th ka, in the elven forest of [Orenlas](<../../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/orenlas/orenlas.md>). She woke from the dreaming with her generation-mates in DR 1712, and quickly showed a talent for music and dance, even to the extent of channeling the magic of the elves through her art. After spending a few years learning the songs of Orenlas, Drou left early in her first leya, wanting to be useful to the world, and bring joy to people's lives.

Drou traveled through Tyrwingha, Addermarch, and southern Sembara for a few years, learning songs and sharing music. Until recently, when she heard of the alchemist Caradoc recruiting mercenaries to his cause. Curious, and motivated by a sense of wanting to help the world, Drou left for Roscombe.
