Mossfoot is [Edric's](<edric.md>) pony: stalwart, stubborn, loyal to Edric, but not especially brave. After escaping the destruction of [Dandelion House](<../../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/dandelion-house.md>) in DR 1740, Mossfoot lived for several years in [Varrow Forest](<../../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/northlands/varrow-forest.md>), before meeting his end.
