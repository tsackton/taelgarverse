Tarek is a Tollish human adventurer, pickpocket, and salvager from [Tollen](<../../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/tollen/tollen.md>), and a member of the [Tollen Misfits](<tollen-misfits.md>). Quick-handed and resourceful, he collects objects that often prove useful to his companions, and works with [Kaito Min](<kaito-min.md>) on their performance and pickpocket routines.
