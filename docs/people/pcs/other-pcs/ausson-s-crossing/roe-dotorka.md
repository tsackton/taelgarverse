Roe DoTorka is an Ardikhun dwarf who survived the fall of [Ardith](<../../../../gazetteer/central-highlands/dwarven-kingdoms/ardith.md>) during the [Great War](<../../../../events/1500s/great-war.md>). Unlike most survivors, he did not join the migration south to [Nardith](<../../../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/nardith/nardith.md>). He lives on the plateau above [Ausson's Crossing](<../../../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/refounded-alliance-of-aurbez/ausson-s-crossing.md>) and can transform into beasts. Cranky and sometimes unpleasant company, he is nevertheless a dependable ally.
