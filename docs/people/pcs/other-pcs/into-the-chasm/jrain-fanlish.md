Jrain Fanlish is a traveler, messenger, and archer who sometimes works with the Mezhai in the forests of [Breva](<../../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/breva.md>). She uses birds to carry messages and scouts with her hawk companion, [Rala](<rala.md>). She is a member of the [Chasm Explorers](<chasm-explorers.md>).
