Eolo is a brash young kenku from the [Far North](<../../../../gazetteer/faraway-places/far-north.md>) and a member of the [Chasm Explorers](<chasm-explorers.md>). After a white dragon destroyed his village, he spent much of his childhood among the [Stoneborn](<../../../../creatures/species/stoneborn.md>) of the [Sentinels](<../../../../gazetteer/sentinel-range.md>). He later traveled to [Kemeko Monastery](<../../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/kemeko-monastery.md>), seeking out its kenku monks. His flight and psionic powers make him a mobile fighter, though his impulsiveness often carries him into danger.
