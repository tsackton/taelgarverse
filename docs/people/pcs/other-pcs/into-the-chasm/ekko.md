Ekko is a kenku mystic of the [Vejo Vaikai](<../../../../groups/vejo-vaikai.md>) and a student of [Eidrikas](<../../../sembarans/eidrikas.md>). Raised at [Kemeko Monastery](<../../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/kemeko-monastery.md>), he left the monastery to live among the mystics along the [Selgrava](<../../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/rivers/teft-watershed/selgrava.md>) in [Breva](<../../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/zimkova/breva.md>). A member of the [Chasm Explorers](<chasm-explorers.md>), he can take animal forms, using this ability to scout and fight.
