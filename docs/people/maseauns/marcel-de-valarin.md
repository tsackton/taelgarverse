Marcel de Valarin was an Isinguer human from [Valarin](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/duchy-of-maseau/valarin.md>) and one of the founders of the [Order of the Charitable Wanderer](<../../groups/order-of-the-charitable-wanderer.md>). He often worked with the [Rangers](<../../groups/rangers.md>) and was frequently found investigating troubles in [Maseau](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/duchy-of-maseau/duchy-of-maseau.md>), [Addermarch](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/addermarch.md>), and southern [Sembara](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/sembara.md>).
