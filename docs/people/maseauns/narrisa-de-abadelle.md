Narrisa de Abadelle is a paladin of [The Wanderer](<../../gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/mos-numena-pantheon/the-wanderer.md>) from the [Duchy of Maseau](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/duchy-of-maseau/duchy-of-maseau.md>) and a member of the [Order of the Charitable Wanderer](<../../groups/order-of-the-charitable-wanderer.md>). She was trapped in Duskmire for some time before being rescued when the fey portal in [Cleenseau Wood](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/cleenseau-wood.md>) opened in DR 1719.
