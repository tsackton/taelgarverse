Rhiannon de Vaux is the Earl of High Tyrn in eastern [Addermarch](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/addermarch/addermarch.md>), based at Marwick. During Krauzeth's attacks in DR 1720, she encouraged non-essential personnel and upland herdsmen to retreat to the lowlands.
