Milo Kentbrush was a human adventurer, an overconfident would-be aboleth-slayer, whose sobbing ghost lingered in flooded caverns near [Suwi](<../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/suwi.md>), still tied to the weapon he had brought to aid in his quest. He had sought to kill the aboleth [Ithu’rax](<../other-nonhumans/ithurax.md>), but was killed in the attempt. 

The [Silver Tempests](<../pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>) recovered his arrow of aberration slaying from the caverns, and Milo followed them, bound to the arrow as they continued toward Ithu'rax's lair.
