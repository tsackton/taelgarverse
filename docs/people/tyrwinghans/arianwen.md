Arianwen is a captain of the [Rangers](<../../groups/rangers.md>) who led a five-person embassy from [Tyrwingha](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/tyrwingha/tyrwingha.md>) to seek advice and aid from [Orenlas](<../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/orenlas/orenlas.md>) following strange events in the [Plaguelands](<../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/plaguelands.md>) south of Tyrwingha. Calm and direct, she builds agreement without demanding obedience.
