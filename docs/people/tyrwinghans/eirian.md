Eirian is a trans woman and acolyte of the Wyrdling in [Ruthin](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/tyrwingha/ruthin.md>), known in the region for her gender-affirming herbal care and skill as a teacher. She teaches herbal treatments and techniques of dress and presentation to help people born “out of season” live as themselves.
