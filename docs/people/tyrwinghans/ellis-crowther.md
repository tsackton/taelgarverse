Ellis Crowther is a singer and left-handed crwth player from [Clawyn](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/tyrwingha/clawyn.md>), and the mirror twin of Llew Crowther. He comes from a vineyard-working family and was [Celyn’s](<../pcs/cleenseau/celyn.md>) childhood sweetheart.

Tall and slender, with golden-brown skin and black hair, Ellis is charismatic and generous with his affections. He is bisexual and enjoys flirting with people who welcome it, without being unkind or pushy.
