Ellis is one of the Crowther twins of [Clawyn](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/tyrwingha/clawyn.md>), a family of vineyard workers and musicians. He and his brother [Llew](<llew-crowther.md>) are mirror twins, about six months older than [Celyn](<../pcs/cleenseau/celyn.md>), with autumn birthdays. Ellis is left-handed and Llew is right-handed; Celyn could always tell them apart by such details, though the twins did not realize how he did it. Both are tall, slender, good-looking, with golden-brown skin and black hair. Their family supports the harvest and winepress work with music, and their older sister seemed likely to take on that role.

Ellis sings and plays the crwth, the instrument from which the Crowther name derives. He is more expressive and easygoing than Llew, but can be unexpectedly firm about something he refuses to do. He is a romantic who imagines becoming a successful performer and would like to be a reliable presence for a partner who needs one. His relationships have generally been shorter than Llew's, apart from his childhood romance with Celyn. Ellis is bisexual or pansexual and enjoys flirting without pressuring others. Neither twin is particularly inclined toward monogamy, although either might agree to it; both could imagine a primary partner alongside less lasting romances. Both brothers speak Tyrwinghan and Sembaran; Ellis might know a little Sylvan, but the player's sketch did not settle that.

The twins have talked of someday leaving the village and made their twin act part of their imagined performance career. Ellis knows the village regards them as pleasant but unlikely to settle into steady work. He tries not to be bothered by that reputation. The brothers regularly earn money in a market town. The sketch describes Ellis as especially perceptive, deft with his hands, and good with people.
