Kallipseis is an ancient dwarven wizard, gardener, and fungal researcher who has been in the [27th House](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-kingdom/27th-house.md>) for many, many years. She claims to have become head of the [Arithrimos Lamperum](<../../groups/drankorian-societies/arithrimos-lamperum.md>) after [Thalestria's](<../other-humans/thalestria.md>) disappearance or death.
