Roaric Ferrystone is an elderly, semi-retired blacksmith originally from the [Ferrystones](<../../groups/dwarven-clans/ferrystones.md>) of [Rinburg](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/rinburg.md>) who lived in [Beury](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/beury.md>) for many years. In DR 1720, he helped establish a smithy in [Asineau](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/asineau.md>) and remained there to train [Samuel Cordwaner](<../sembarans/samuel-cordwaner.md>).
