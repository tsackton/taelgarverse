The cousin of [Hagrim](<hagrim.md>), trapped in [Morkalan](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/morkalan/morkalan.md>) for many years. During that time lost much of his memory and mind, and became known to the people of [Morkalan](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/morkalan/morkalan.md>) as the Mad Priest.


Delig escaped Morkalan, along with many other dwarves, when [Riswynn](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/riswynn.md>) and companions brought the [Chalice of the Runepriest](<../../things/artifacts-of-power/chalice-of-the-runepriest.md>) into the realm and put [Hagrim](<hagrim.md>) to rest, ending his domain. He traveled to [Tharn Todor](<../../gazetteer/greater-dunmar/realms/nardith/tharn-todor.md>) in the autumn of DR 1748, but his subsequent fate is not recorded.
