Gwydren is a beastfolk, alternatively described as a large moose-headed humanoid or a stag-antlered creature, who has lived in the [Thornweald](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/thornweald.md>) for many years and knows its old paths. He spends most of his time around the large, old trees of the deep woods. He was a friend of many dryads and nymphs of the forest, including  [Sweetwillow](<sweetwillow.md>).
