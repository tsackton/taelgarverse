Dame Elowen is the fey hostess of Orchard Close in [Twilight's Grace](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/twilight-s-grace/twilight-s-grace.md>). Welcoming, maternal, and kind, she seems to know every family, recipe, and guest, ensuring that no table lacks food or company. Her appearance is difficult to recall; visitors remember her warmth more clearly than her physical features.
