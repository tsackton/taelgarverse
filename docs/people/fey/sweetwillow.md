Sweetwillow was a dryad of the [Thornweald](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/thornweald.md>). She occupied a large willow incongruously growing in the center of a bright, sunny hilltop meadow. She was killed by a swarm of death butterflies, a corruption from [Lord Umbraeth](<../extraplanar-powers/archfey/lord-umbraeth.md>), [Harrow](<../extraplanar-powers/archfey/harrow.md>), [Wend](<../extraplanar-powers/archfey/wend.md>), or something else, unable to be stopped by the [Briarheart](<../extraplanar-powers/archfey/morlaith.md>) at a low ebb of his powers.
