Izkir is a Deno'qai godcaller from the [Highveil Forest](<../../gazetteer/central-highlands/highveil-forest.md>), associated with [Bita](<../../gods-and-religions/gods/tanshi/bita.md>), the tanshi of bears. He served as a guide and divine spellcaster during the [Silver Tempests](<../pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>)' campaign against [Nymthrax](<../other-nonhumans/nymthrax.md>) in the [Blackwater Fens](<../../gazetteer/northern-sentinels/blackwater-fens.md>).
