Itar is the Godcaller of the [Bek'eni](<../../groups/cultures/deno-qai-tribes/elderwood-tribes/bek-eni.md>) tribe of Deno'qai. He is a young-looking man, ostentatiously dressed in a style that seems to be a cheap imitation of Elven elegance, wearing far more jewelry than is customary among the Deno'qai. He is obsequious toward elves but smarmy and aloof toward others; even at his most ingratiating, there is something faintly unsettling about him.

[Mezzar](<../other-nonhumans/mezzar.md>) elevated Itar to Godcaller after deposing [Theba](<theba.md>), and Itar remains under Mezzar’s sway. Despite his title, he does not hear the [tanshi](<../../gods-and-religions/gods/tanshi/tanshi.md>) with the clarity expected of a Godcaller. He sometimes harbors quiet doubts about Mezzar but carefully conceals them.
