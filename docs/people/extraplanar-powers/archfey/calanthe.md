# Lady Calanthë

Lady Calanthë, Duchess of the Sunset Forest and Guardian of the Gloaming Hills, is an archfey of [Sunward Reach](<../../../gazetteer/extraplanar/feywild/sunward-reach.md>) who protects against incursions by [Harrow](<harrow.md>) and [Wend](<wend.md>) across the Sunset Gate.
