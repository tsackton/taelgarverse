# Amloch
:speaker:{ .middle } *(AM-lock)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>

    A [Hkaran](<../../history/historical-realms/hkar.md>) [human](<../../creatures/species/humans.md>) (he/him)  
    { .bio }

</div>


Amloch was a wizard who lived in [Hkar](<../../history/historical-realms/hkar.md>) before the [Downfall](<../../events/ancient/the-downfall.md>), and is chiefly known for the [Binding Stones](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/treasure/binding-stones.md>), a powerful magical artifact attributed to him.


According to a [letter](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/letters-and-notes/philosopher-s-information-concerning-binding-stones.md>) from the [Ancient and Honorable Guild of Philosophers](<../../groups/tollen-guilds/ancient-and-honorable-guild-of-philosophers.md>), he captured energy released by the creation of [Sarqon](<../../gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/sarqon.md>)’s extraplanar prison in five stones.
