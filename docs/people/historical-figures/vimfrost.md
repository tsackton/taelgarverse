Vimfrost was an ancient white dragon who long ruled over the icy wastes of the [Far North](<../../gazetteer/faraway-places/far-north.md>). In the early DR 1500s, he led an army of orcs, trolls, ogres, and other creatures against [Vostok](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/vostok/vostok.md>), in what became known as [Vimfrost's War](<../../events/1500s/vimfrost-s-war.md>). In DR 1511, he was defeated and killed by an alliance of humans, dwarves, stoneborn, and frost giants led by [Derik III](<sembaran-royalty/derik-iii.md>) and [Bjarnfrost](<../giants/bjarnfrost.md>), that banded together to protect themselves from his aggression. His descendants continued to threaten the northern [Green Sea](<../../gazetteer/green-sea.md>) long after his death.
