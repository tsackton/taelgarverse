Maglar was a peronar natural historian and former student of [Minarith](<minarith.md>). As [Apollyon](<drankorian-emperors/apollyon.md>)’s persecution intensified, Minarith had encouraged Maglar not to flee, but in DR 1048, facing arrest by the [Omnis Pura](<../../groups/drankorian-societies/omnis-pura.md>), Minarith wrote to Maglar, acknowledging that his hope had failed and urging Maglar to run. The letter was never sent. Maglar’s subsequent fate is unknown.
