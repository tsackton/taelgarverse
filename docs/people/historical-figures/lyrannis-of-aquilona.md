Lyrannis of [Aquilona](<../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/aquilona.md>) was an influential cosmological philosopher and natural historian. She wrote extensively on elemental beings, especially genies, but is best known for [On Elemental Forms](<../../things/books/on-elemental-forms.md>), in which she argues that creatures with a pure elemental essence are overwhelmingly associated with Fire, Water, Earth, or Air.

Her work is frequently cited in debates over the number and nature of the [Energy Realms](<../../cosmology/energy-realms/energy-realms.md>), and remains one of the premier sources of information on elementals.
