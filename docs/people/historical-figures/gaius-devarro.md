# Gaius Devarro
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>

    A [Chardonian](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardonian-empire.md>) [human](<../../creatures/species/humans.md>) (he/him)  
    b. DR 916 - d. DR 997, died at age 81 years  
    { .bio }

</div>


Gaius Devarro was a Chardonian metaphysical cosmologist, widely recognized as the father of the [Standard Multiversal Model](<../../cosmology/planar-concepts/standard-multiversal-model.md>). He was born in [Chardon](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/chardon.md>) during the reign of Imrodel, the last peronar emperor of [Drankor](<../../history/historical-realms/drankorian-empire.md>), and joined the [University of Chardon](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/university-of-chardon.md>) as part of the founding faculty of the [Faculty of Metaphysics](<../../groups/chardonian-organizations/faculty-of-metaphysics.md>). He lived in [Chardon](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/chardon.md>) his entire life, and was prominent in the early establishment of the [University of Chardon](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/university-of-chardon.md>), but after the [Drankorian Civil War](<../../events/900s/drankorian-civil-war.md>) turned away from public life. 

Gaius wrote wildly on many planar topics, including on [Limbo](<../../cosmology/spiritual-realms/limbo.md>), [Ley Lines](<../../cosmology/planar-concepts/ley-lines.md>), [Echo Realms](<../../cosmology/demiplanes-and-echo-realms/echo-realms.md>), and the broader structure of the [Multiverse](<../../cosmology/multiverse.md>). His works include [On Minds and Chaos](<../../things/books/on-minds-and-chaos.md>) and [An Exploration of Magical Connections Between the Planes](<../../things/books/an-exploration-of-magical-connections-between-the-planes.md>).
