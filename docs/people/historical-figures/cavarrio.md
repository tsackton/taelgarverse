Cavarrio was a Chardonian scholar and member of the [Faculty of History](<../../groups/chardonian-organizations/faculty-of-history.md>) in the [University of Chardon](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/university-of-chardon.md>). He is most famous for his seminal work on the history of the [Drankorian Empire](<../../history/historical-realms/drankorian-empire.md>), the  [Chronicles of Drankor](<../../things/books/chronicles-of-drankor.md>), which he finished in DR 1209. It has become the standard source on Drankorian chronology in the modern era.
