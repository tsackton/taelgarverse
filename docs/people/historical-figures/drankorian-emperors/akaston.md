Akaston was the emperor of [Drankor](<../../../history/historical-realms/drankorian-empire.md>) from DR 980 until his death in DR 991. He succeeded [Vestian](<vestian.md>) and was succeeded by Persa. His reign was a temporary reprieve from internal conflict and division across the Empire, though Akaston was only able to hide, not mend, the damage of the [Drankorian Civil War](<../../../events/900s/drankorian-civil-war.md>).
