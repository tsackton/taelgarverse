Anates was the first human emperor of [Drankor](<../../../history/historical-realms/drankorian-empire.md>), ruling from DR 331 to DR 366. During his reign, Drankor expanded north through the Mostreve Gap and established its first presence in [Sembara](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/sembara.md>).
