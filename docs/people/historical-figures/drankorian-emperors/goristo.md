Goristo was the emperor of [Drankor](<../../../history/historical-realms/drankorian-empire.md>) from DR 366 until his death in DR 433. A renowned general and mage, he negotiated several agreements with the peoples and kingdoms of the Sembaran river valleys, culminating in the [Treaty of Marhavn](<../../../events/400s/402/treaty-of-marhavn.md>), signed in DR 402, which established the [Volta](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/rivers/volta-watershed/volta.md>) as the empire’s northern border. In DR 423, he signed the [Treaty of Tyrwingha](<../../../events/400s/423/treaty-of-tyrwingha.md>) with the [Archfey Ethlenn](<../../extraplanar-powers/archfey/archfey-ethlenn.md>), establishing the formal relationship between Drankor and [Tyrwingha](<../../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/tyrwingha/tyrwingha.md>).
