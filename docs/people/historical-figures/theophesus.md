# Theophesus
:speaker:{ .middle } *(thee-oh-FEE-sus)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
- :octicons-info-24:{ .lg .middle } __Biographical Information__

    A [Drankorian](<../../history/historical-realms/drankorian-empire.md>) [human](<../../creatures/species/humans.md>) (he/him)  
    { .bio }

</div>


Theophesus was a Drankorian scholar whose work helped define the concept of [Echo Realms](<../../cosmology/demiplanes-and-echo-realms/echo-realms.md>). He collected some of the first maps of the [Feywild](<../../cosmology/feywild.md>) and proposed that the Feywild has a physical correspondence with the [Material Plane](<../../cosmology/material-plane.md>). 

In later writing, Theophesus applied a similar idea to the [Dreamworld](<../../cosmology/dreamworld.md>) and the [Plane of Souls](<../../cosmology/plane-of-souls.md>), treating the Dreamworld as a metaphysical echo of the [Plane of Souls](<../../cosmology/plane-of-souls.md>). 

In the modern age, his writing is chiefly influential via his work's impact on [Gaius Devarro](<gaius-devarro.md>) and the development of the [Standard Multiversal Model](<../../cosmology/planar-concepts/standard-multiversal-model.md>).
