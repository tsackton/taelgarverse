Adèle Laurent was an Isinguer human member of [the Rangers](<../../groups/rangers.md>) based in [Rinburg](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/rinburg.md>), where she helped coordinate investigations up and down the [Enst](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/rivers/wistel-enst-watershed/enst.md>) valley. She died in DR 1689.
