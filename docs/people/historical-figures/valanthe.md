Valanthe was an elven bard and playwright of the 32nd ka, born in [Rostaurë](<../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/rostaure.md>). She came to [Drankor](<../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/drankor/drankor.md>) in DR 948, during her first leya, during the reign of [Helea](<drankorian-emperors/helea.md>), before the [Drankorian Civil War](<../../events/900s/drankorian-civil-war.md>) and the rise of [Omnis Pura](<../../groups/drankorian-societies/omnis-pura.md>). By the time [Apollyon](<drankorian-emperors/apollyon.md>) came to power in DR 1011, she was a bard of significant renown. Her magic drew on the power of her oratory and the cunning of her words.

Under Apollyon’s rule, her art became resistance. Her satirical plays, often performed in secret, mocked the emperor, the [Ashen Cloaks](<../../groups/drankorian-societies/ashen-cloaks.md>), and the [Omnis Pura](<../../groups/drankorian-societies/omnis-pura.md>). Pamphlets carrying her words circulated widely. Her work later became a rallying cry for the [Radiant Path](<../../groups/drankorian-societies/radiant-path.md>) and [Fides Lucaris](<../../groups/drankorian-societies/fides-lucaris.md>), although she formally joined neither movement.

Apollyon ordered her capture early in his reign. Valanthe evaded his agents through the 1020s using magic and wit, but was betrayed and captured during the Hearth Riots in DR 1031. Taken in chains to the [Circular Island](<../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/circular-island.md>), she remained imprisoned until she was sacrificed in DR 1053, becoming the first of seven souls bound into [Apollyon's Phylactery](<../../things/artifacts-of-power/apollyon-s-phylactery.md>). 

She was considered a martyr by the Radiant Path, and her life was commemorated in the [Book of Martyrs of the Radiant Path](<../../things/books/book-of-martyrs-of-the-radiant-path.md>).


On May 24, 1749 DR, [Seeker](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/seeker.md>) freed her soul in the [Land of the Dead](<../../cosmology/land-of-the-dead.md>) by mocking Apollyon with *Vicious Mockery*, rekindling her laughter and memories of her satirical works so she could pass on.
