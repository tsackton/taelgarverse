# Thardrum Deepseeker
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>

    A [dwarf](<../../creatures/species/dwarves.md>) (he/him)  
    { .bio }

</div>


Thardrum Deepseeker was an ancient dwarven scholar who wrote about the [Far Realms](<../../cosmology/far-realms.md>), best remembered for his claim that all Far Realms need be home to dangerous and evil creatures.

However, history does not record his fate beyond speculation of his dangerous interactions with Far Realms entities. After the rediscovery of his work in [Isingue](<../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/isingue.md>) in the years before the [Great War](<../../events/1500s/great-war.md>), his presumed death during his research cast doubt on his central thesis.
