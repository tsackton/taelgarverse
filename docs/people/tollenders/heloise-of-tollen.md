# Heloise of Tollen
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>

    A [Tollish](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/tollen/tollen.md>) [human](<../../creatures/species/humans.md>) (she/her)  
    { .bio }

</div>


Heloise of Tollen is a theological cosmologist and arcane mathematician. Based at the[University of Tollen](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/tollen/places/university-of-tollen.md>), her most notable work is a careful mathematical study of the [Plane of Creation](<../../cosmology/plane-of-creation.md>), which implies that it may be an infinitely dense mass of [Soulstuff](<../../cosmology/planar-concepts/soulstuff.md>), impenetrable to anything except the [Divine Presence](<../../gods-and-religions/gods/high-gods/divine-presence.md>).

Heloise also popularized a folk theory that [Arborea](<../../cosmology/spiritual-realms/arborea.md>) arises from the fundamental desire of creation to live.
