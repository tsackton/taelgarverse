Tilly Brineheart is a halfling sailor, wanderer, adventurer, and spellcaster of considerable talent. She loves exploration and seeing new places above all else, with the possible exception of her husband [Milo Thistlefoot](<milo-thistlefoot.md>), who shares her love of adventurer. 

Tilly is rarely content to remain in one place for long. Aboard the [Stormdancer](<../../things/ships/stormdancer.md>), she has sailed across the [Sea of Storms](<../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/sea-of-storms.md>) to the [Far South](<../../gazetteer/faraway-places/far-south.md>) and traveled the uncharted southwestern coasts of [Apporia](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/apporia/apporia.md>).  She is protective of her crew and confident in her abilities, trusting her magic and command of water to keep her safe. She is not one to pass up a challenge when she thinks she can help, no matter the danger. 

Her attitude is cheerful even under trying circumstances. She is brisk, resourceful, and capable of humor under pressure, with enough pragmatism to keep resentment from dictating the plan. She is sustained by her close, affectionate relationship with her husband, [Milo Thistlefoot](<milo-thistlefoot.md>), with whom she travels


In DR 1749, Tilly, [Milo Thistlefoot](<milo-thistlefoot.md>), and the crew of the [Stormdancer](<../../things/ships/stormdancer.md>) investigated a strange quiet disturbance off the northwestern coast of [Apporia](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/apporia/apporia.md>), and were captured by allies of [Buruli](<../other-nonhumans/buruli.md>) near the undersea merfolk city of [Omi](<../../gazetteer/faraway-places/omi.md>). Tilly was imprisoned in the [Elemental Plane of Water](<../../cosmology/energy-realms/elemental-plane-of-water.md>) until the [Dunmar Fellowship](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship.md>) rescued her and her companions, including [Milo Thistlefoot](<milo-thistlefoot.md>). 

She then traveled with the [Dunmar Fellowship](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship.md>) for a time, as far as the [Circular Island](<../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/circular-island.md>). Here, she helped defeat [Ra'ghemdros](<../other-nonhumans/ra-ghemdros.md>) before departing with Milo to seek their former crew and outfit a new ship.
