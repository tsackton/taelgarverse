# Tilly Brineheart
:speaker:{ .middle } *(TILL-ee BRYNE-hart)*

Tilly Brineheart is a halfling spellcaster who sailed aboard the [Stormdancer](<../../things/ships/stormdancer.md>) with its captain, [Milo Thistlefoot](<milo-thistlefoot.md>). She can shape water, transform into sea creatures, and wield healing magic.


Captured with Milo and members of their crew near [Omi](<../../gazetteer/faraway-places/omi.md>), Tilly was imprisoned in the [Elemental Plane of Water](<../../cosmology/energy-realms/elemental-plane-of-water.md>) until the [Dunmar Fellowship](<../pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship.md>) rescued them. She later helped the Fellowship fight [Ra'ghemdros](<../other-nonhumans/ra-ghemdros.md>) before departing with Milo to seek their former crew and outfit a new ship.
