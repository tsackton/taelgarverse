Quent Merriweather is an older, one-eyed halfling with a keen fashion sense who leads a three-wagon [Merriweather](<../../groups/halfling-families/merriweathers.md>) trading caravan. The caravan visited [Asineau](<../../gazetteer/greater-sembara/sembara/barony-of-aveil/cleenseau-region/asineau.md>) in May DR 1720.
