# Ashspire
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A compound elemental realm in the [Energy Realms](<../energy-realms.md>)  
</div>


Ashspire is a poorly described [Energy Realm](<../energy-realms.md>), a land of explosive destruction, ashy wastes, and death. Few venture here, and reliable reports are hard to come back. 

It is variously considered a compound elemental realm, mixing the [Elemental Plane of Fire](<../elemental-plane-of-fire.md>) and the [Plane of Magic](<../../plane-of-magic.md>); a border region of the [Elemental Plane of Fire](<../elemental-plane-of-fire.md>); or its own unique realm of death and destruction.
