# Ironroot
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A compound elemental realm in the [Energy Realms](<../energy-realms.md>)  
</div>


Ironroot is a poorly described [Energy Realm](<../energy-realms.md>), associated in [Airion the Mistspeaker](<../../../people/other-humans/airion.md>)'s system with strength, metal, and the mingling of Earth and Magic.

It is variously considered a compound elemental realm, mixing the [Elemental Plane of Earth](<../elemental-plane-of-earth.md>) and the [Plane of Magic](<../../plane-of-magic.md>); a border region of the [Elemental Plane of Earth](<../elemental-plane-of-earth.md>); or its own unique realm of metal. Some consider this to be what the dwarves describe as the [Elemental Plane of Metal](<../elemental-plane-of-metal.md>).
