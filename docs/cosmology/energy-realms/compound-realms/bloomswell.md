# Bloomswell
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A compound elemental realm in the [Energy Realms](<../energy-realms.md>)  
</div>


Bloomswell is a poorly described [Energy Realm](<../energy-realms.md>), associated by [Airion the Mistspeaker](<../../../people/other-humans/airion.md>) with abundance, and described as a vast ocean bursting with color, lush with vibrant life filling the waters of the realm. 

It is variously considered a compound elemental realm, mixing the [Elemental Plane of Water](<../elemental-plane-of-water.md>) and the [Plane of Magic](<../../plane-of-magic.md>); a border region of the [Elemental Plane of Water](<../elemental-plane-of-water.md>); or its own unique realm of life and growth.
