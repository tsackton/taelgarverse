# Miredepths
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A compound elemental realm in the [Energy Realms](<../energy-realms.md>)  
</div>


Miredepths is a poorly described [Energy Realm](<../energy-realms.md>), thought to be a place of slime, ooze, muck, and mire, associated in [Airion the Mistspeaker](<../../../people/other-humans/airion.md>)'s system with the mingling of Water and Earth.

It is variously considered a compound elemental realm, mixing the [Elemental Plane of Water](<../elemental-plane-of-water.md>) and the [Elemental Plane of Earth](<../elemental-plane-of-earth.md>); a border region between those two planes; or its own unique realm of mud and corruption.
