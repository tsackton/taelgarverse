# Smokeshroud
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A compound elemental realm in the [Energy Realms](<../energy-realms.md>)  
</div>


Smokeshroud is a poorly described [Energy Realm](<../energy-realms.md>), a place of endless smoke, ash and cinder falling from the sky in wave upon wave. In [Airion the Mistspeaker](<../../../people/other-humans/airion.md>)'s system, it is considered to represent the mingling of Fire and Air.

It is variously considered a compound elemental realm, mixing the [Elemental Plane of Fire](<../elemental-plane-of-fire.md>) and the [Elemental Plane of Air](<../elemental-plane-of-air.md>); a border region between those two planes; or its own unique realm of smoke and concealment.
