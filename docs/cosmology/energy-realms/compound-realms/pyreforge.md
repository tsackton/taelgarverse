# Pyreforge
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A compound elemental realm in the [Energy Realms](<../energy-realms.md>)  
</div>


Pyreforge is a poorly described [Energy Realm](<../energy-realms.md>), associated in [Airion the Mistspeaker](<../../../people/other-humans/airion.md>)'s system with forges, creative energy, and the mingling of Fire and Earth.

It is variously considered a compound elemental realm, mixing the [Elemental Plane of Fire](<../elemental-plane-of-fire.md>) and the [Elemental Plane of Earth](<../elemental-plane-of-earth.md>); a border region between those two planes; or its own unique realm of heat and transformation.
