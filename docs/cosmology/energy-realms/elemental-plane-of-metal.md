# The Elemental Plane of Metal
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A hypothesized energy realm in the [Energy Realms](<energy-realms.md>)  
</div>


The Elemental Plane of Metal is a hypothesized Energy Realm that is implied to exist by the traditional dwarven model of seven fundamental energies: Spirit, Magic, Earth, Air, Fire, Water, and Metal. In that model, the non-Spirit energies are each assumed to derive from a specific energy realm, requiring the existence of an energy realm associated with metal.

The plane's existence is disputed. [Lyrannis of Aquilona](<../../people/historical-figures/lyrannis-of-aquilona.md>) dismissed the idea, citing a lack of evidence for pure metal elementals. Other metaphysicists point to descriptions of massive twisted caverns of iron, usually assumed to be part of the [Elemental Plane of Earth](<elemental-plane-of-earth.md>), as possible evidence for the Elemental Plane of Metal. Alternatively, the dwarven Plane of Metal may actually be [Airion's](<../../people/other-humans/airion.md>) [Ironroot](<compound-realms/ironroot.md>) and [Pyreforge](<compound-realms/pyreforge.md>), two compound realms associated with the Elemental Plane of Earth.
