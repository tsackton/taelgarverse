# Standard Multiversal Model

The Standard Multiversal Model is the dominant scholarly classification of the [Multiverse](<../multiverse.md>) taught by the [Faculty of Metaphysics](<../../groups/chardonian-organizations/faculty-of-metaphysics.md>) at the [University of Chardon](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/university-of-chardon.md>). It grew out of earlier Drankorian work, especially the synthesis associated with [Gaius Devarro](<../../people/historical-figures/gaius-devarro.md>), and remains the most widely used framework among Chardonian planar scholars.

In its common form, the model divides the planes into four broad classes:

- Fundamental Planes: the [Plane of Creation](<../plane-of-creation.md>), [Astral Plane](<../astral-plane.md>), [Plane of Magic](<../plane-of-magic.md>), [Ethereal Plane](<../ethereal-plane.md>), [Material Plane](<../material-plane.md>), and [Plane of Souls](<../plane-of-souls.md>).
- [Energy Realms](<../energy-realms/energy-realms.md>): planes associated with the fundamental energies of the multiverse, especially the elemental planes.
- [Spiritual Realms](<../spiritual-realms/spiritual-realms.md>): the planes of gods, demons, and other spiritual beings, generally lying beyond the [Land of the Dead](<../land-of-the-dead.md>).
- [Echo Realms](<../demiplanes-and-echo-realms/echo-realms.md>): reflections, distortions, or reverberations shaped by the Riving, including the [Feywild](<../feywild.md>) and the [Dreamworld](<../dreamworld.md>). [Demiplanes](<../demiplanes-and-echo-realms/demiplanes.md>) and the [Shadowfolds](<../demiplanes-and-echo-realms/shadowfolds.md>) are usually also included in the Echo Realms.

The model is a classification scheme, not a universally accepted statement of metaphysical truth. [Yendalism](<yendalism.md>) rejects many of its distinctions in favor of the division between the [Inner Realms](<inner-realms.md>) and [Outer Realms](<outer-realms.md>), while [planar dualists](<planar-dualism.md>) collapse much of the model into the paired fundamentals of the Astral and Ethereal realms.
