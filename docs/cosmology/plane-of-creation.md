# The Plane of Creation
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } A plane in the [multiverse](<multiverse.md>)  
    :octicons-location-24:{ .lg .middle } Sometimes considered part of the [Astral Plane](<astral-plane.md>)  
</div>


The Plane of Creation is the name given by experimental theologists to the ultimate source of divine power, the place where [Soulstuff](<planar-concepts/soulstuff.md>) originates, and the home of the [Divine Presence](<../gods-and-religions/gods/high-gods/divine-presence.md>). Most scholars do not believe this is a place a traveler can visit, or at least not visit and survive; indeed, recent theoretical calculations by the theological cosmologist [Heloise of Tollen](<../people/tollenders/heloise-of-tollen.md>) suggest that the Plane of Creation itself may be an infinitely dense agglomeration of [Soulstuff](<planar-concepts/soulstuff.md>), impenetrable to any force except the [Divine Presence](<../gods-and-religions/gods/high-gods/divine-presence.md>).
