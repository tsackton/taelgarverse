# Hezguli
:speaker:{ .middle } *(hez-goo-LEE)*  
<div class="grid cards ext-narrow-margin ext-one-column" markdown>
-
   :octicons-info-24:{ .lg .middle } An [orc](<../creatures/species/orcs.md>) communal society  
</div>


The Hezguli are a community of [orcs](<../creatures/species/orcs.md>) who were born under the sway of [Thark](<../gods-and-religions/gods/embodied-gods/thark.md>)’s curse, but freed from it as children or young adults. They have learned—often with difficulty—to live free. They are most closely associated with General [Murook](<../people/orcs/murook.md>), widely regarded as their unofficial leader. 

The Hezguli also serve as a mutual-aid community for freed orcs learning to live outside Thark’s control, offering practical support and fellowship to people bearing the burden of the scars of Thark's chains.
