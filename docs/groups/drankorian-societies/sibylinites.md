# The Sibylinites

The Sibylinites were a magical society in [Drankor](<../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/drankor/drankor.md>) devoted to secret knowledge of [The Sibyl](<../../gods-and-religions/gods/incorporeal-gods/mos-numena-pantheon/the-sibyl.md>). In DR 688, they founded the Sibyline Archives, a small library on the south bank of the [Chasa](<../../gazetteer/major-rivers/chasa-nahadi-watershed/chasa.md>) in [Chardon](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/chardon.md>). It is now called the [Great Library](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/great-library.md>).
