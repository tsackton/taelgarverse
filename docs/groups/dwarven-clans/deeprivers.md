The Deepriver Clan was a [Dwarven](<../../creatures/species/dwarves.md>) clan that was prominent in the [Fiatara Mountains](<../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/fiamatara-mountains/fiatara-mountains.md>) before the [Great War](<../../events/1500s/great-war.md>). They were associated with the ancient mine workings under [Goldpeak Mountain](<../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/fiamatara-mountains/goldpeak-mountain.md>), in particular.
