The Havoc Host is mysterious mercenary company of unknown loyalties, based in the [Northwest Coast](<../gazetteer/northwest-coast/northwest-coast.md>) beyond the edges of the [Chardonian Empire](<../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardonian-empire.md>). They were involved in several kidnappings perpetrated by the aboleth [Ithu’rax](<../people/other-nonhumans/ithurax.md>) in DR 1748, including the kidnapping of [Dinia](<../people/other-humans/dinia.md>) from the [Fiatara Mountains](<../gazetteer/northwest-coast/fiamatara-mountains/fiatara-mountains.md>) and [Mikel](<../people/other-humans/mikel.md>) from [Suwi](<../gazetteer/northwest-coast/suwi.md>), though their subsequent fate is unknown.
