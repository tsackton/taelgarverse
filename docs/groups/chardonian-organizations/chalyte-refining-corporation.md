The Chalyte Refining Corporation is a chartered corporation, granted an imperial monopoly on the processing of raw [chalyte](<../../things/materials/chalyte.md>) ore within the Chardonian Empire. It is managed by a board of control, with equal seats for the [Windcallers](<windcallers.md>) and the [workers' guilds](<guild-of-chalyte-workers.md>), with the Magistros holding the tie-breaking vote. It currently controls and operates three chalyte refineries on the [North Bank](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/north-bank.md>) of [Chardon](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/chardon.md>). 

It was founded in DR 1749, when the [Magistros](<../../people/chardonians/mitus-verina-auratan.md>), in light of corruption within the [Curia of 42](<curia-of-42.md>) and the [Chamber of Stamps](<chamber-of-stamps.md>), claimed direct control of the chartered chalyte monopoly, with the aid of the new source of chalyte in the [Chataan Mountains](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chataan-mountains.md>) secured for [Chardon](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/chardon.md>) by the [Dunmar Fellowship](<../../people/pcs/dunmar-fellowship/dunmar-fellowship.md>).
