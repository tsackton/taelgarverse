The Praecanti Vigiles, often called "the Vigiles" or the mage guard, are the public-facing magical guard that protects [Chardon](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/chardon.md>), functioning as a mix between a city watch and a local defense force.
