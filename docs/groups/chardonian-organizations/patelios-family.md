The Patelios dynasty is one of the four major oligarch families that controls [Chardon](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/chardon.md>)'s [chalyte](<../../things/materials/chalyte.md>) trade. Historically the second most prominent, after the [Auratan Family](<auratan-family.md>), the current generation of leaders of the family is highly ambitious and eager to take advantage of fragmentation and bitterness among branches of the [Auratan Family](<auratan-family.md>) to expand their influence. 

They have invested heavily in refinery operations in [Chardon](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/chardon.md>), rapidly expanding the fraction of refined ore controlled by them, though some wonder about the source of the funds to support this seemingly endless expansion.
