The Guild of Chalyte Workers is a formal association representing workers in [Chardon](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chardon/chardon.md>)'s [chalyte](<../../things/materials/chalyte.md>) refining trade, emerging out of the [chalyte reforms](<../../events/1700s/1749/chardonian-chalyte-trade-reforms.md>) caused by the [defeat of Fausto](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/session-notes/session-116-dufr.md>), the [Eightfold Flame](<eightfold-flame.md>) riots, and the [opening of the chalyte trade](<../../campaigns/dunmari-frontier-campaign/session-notes/session-128-dufr.md>) in the [Chataan Mountains](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chataan-mountains.md>). 

The Guild regulates who is allowed to work in the chalyte refining trade, as well as the quality and standards for processed chalyte crystal and powder, based on a legal requirement that only Guild-approved workers can work in the refineries managed by the [Chalyte Refining Corporation](<chalyte-refining-corporation.md>). The [Chalyte Refining Corporation](<chalyte-refining-corporation.md>) both has a legal monopoly on the sale of refined chalyte, and is partially controlled by the Guild itself.
