# Mariners

The Mariners are an ancient seafaring culture, originally from [Hkar](<../../history/historical-realms/hkar.md>), who left in a broad migration in the years prior to The Downfall. They settled first around the [Eastern Green Sea](<../../gazetteer/eastern-green-sea/eastern-green-sea.md>) and spread westward along the shores of the [Green Sea](<../../gazetteer/green-sea.md>).
