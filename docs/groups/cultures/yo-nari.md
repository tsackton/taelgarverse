# The Yo'nari
:speaker:{ .middle } *(yo-NAH-ree)*  

The Yo'nari were an ancient proto-[Deno'qai](<deno-qai-tribes/deno-qai.md>) people and among the first humans to settle the northwest coast and interior mountains of [Apporia](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/apporia/apporia.md>). Before the [Downfall Wars](<../../events/ancient/downfall-wars.md>), most of these people vanished into [Cairn Dor](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/cairn-dor/cairn-dor.md>) with their queen, [Yeshara](<../../people/extraplanar-powers/yeshara.md>), leaving ruined settlements and standing stones behind on the [Material Plane](<../../cosmology/material-plane.md>).

## History

The Yo'nari belonged to the early Northros-speaking migrations, from which the [Deno'qai](<deno-qai-tribes/deno-qai.md>), [Vargaldi](<vargaldi.md>), [Zimka](<zimka.md>), and perhaps others descended. They frequently encountered fey as they traveled across [Apporia](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/apporia/apporia.md>), and called the [Feywild](<../../cosmology/feywild.md>) *Nazil-Dimu*. Their surviving sites on the [Material Plane](<../../cosmology/material-plane.md>) use monumental stonework and wolf imagery, including parallel rows of wolf-headed plinths and the trilithic [Cleaver-Stone](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/apporia/cleaver-stone.md>).

Yeshara's growing fear of dreams divided the Yo'nari. Some fled as she outlawed sleep rites and imposed prolonged vigils, while those who remained participated in a midwinter rite intended to sever their connection to sleep and the [Plane of Consciousness](<../../cosmology/plane-of-souls.md>). The rite instead created Cairn Dor and carried Yeshara and her followers into the new realm. 

## Cairn Dor

The native population of Cairn Dor is the continuation of the Yo'nari people, transformed by long residence in the [Shadowfolds](<../../cosmology/demiplanes-and-echo-realms/shadowfolds.md>) and by Yeshara's doctrine of eternal waking. The ordinary inhabitants, called the *Nurim-Dor*, or People of Eternal Waking, fear sleep yet must occasionally rest, while the [Shemra Azem](<../shemra-azem.md>), or Guardians of the Vigil, formed a distinct sleepless military order sustained by magic.
