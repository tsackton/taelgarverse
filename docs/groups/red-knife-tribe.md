The Red Knife tribe is a group of goblins that occupy the rough lands in the foothills of the [Fiatara Mountains](<../gazetteer/northwest-coast/fiamatara-mountains/fiatara-mountains.md>), northwest of [Voltara](<../gazetteer/northwest-coast/northern-provinces/voltara/voltara.md>). Though a nuisance to Voltara in the DR 1740s, since the rise and fall [Grumella](<../people/orcs/grumella.md>) they have not been regularly seen.
