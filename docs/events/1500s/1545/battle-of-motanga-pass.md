# The Battle of Motanga Pass

The Battle of Motanga Pass was one of the last battles of the [Great War](<../great-war.md>), fought on the [Aurbez Plateau](<../../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/aurbez-plateau.md>), where stoneborn forces defeated a hobgoblin rearguard and drove the remaining hobgoblin forces north of the [Aursen](<../../../gazetteer/major-rivers/istaros-watershed/aursen.md>). In the aftermath of this battle, lizardfolk began to return to the [Aursen Marshes](<../../../gazetteer/upper-istaros/refounded-alliance-of-aurbez/aursen-marshes.md>), though it would be years still until hobgoblin forces were decisively driven from the plateau.
