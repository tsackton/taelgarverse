# The The Downfall

The Downfall was the cataclysm at the end of the [Downfall Wars](<downfall-wars.md>), remembered for the sinking of [Hkar](<../../history/historical-realms/hkar.md>) and the reshaping of much of the world of Taelgar beyond. Refugees from Hkar founded [Drankor](<../../gazetteer/drankorian-hinterland/drankor/drankor.md>), from which the [Drankorian Empire](<../../history/historical-realms/drankorian-empire.md>) grew.
