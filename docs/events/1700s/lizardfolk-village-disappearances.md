# The Lizardfolk Village Disappearances

The Lizardfolk Village Disappearances describe a series of vanishings among lizardfolk villages along the coast from [Apporia](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/apporia/apporia.md>) to the [Fiatara Mountains](<../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/fiamatara-mountains/fiatara-mountains.md>), beginning in the DR 1730s, and continuing until the [Silver Tempests](<../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>) destroyed the connection between [Cairn Dor](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/cairn-dor/cairn-dor.md>) and the [Material Plane](<../../cosmology/material-plane.md>) in the fall of DR 1748. 

Over the course of more than a decade, the inhabitants of dozens of small lizardfolk villages seemed to vanish, leaving behind empty houses and a strange green sigil later known as the [Cairn Dor Sigil](<../../things/magic-items/cairn-dor-sigil.md>). Often, though not always, these disappearances were accompanied by disturbances to the local ecosystem or strange shadow-tainted creatures. 

## First Known Disappearance: Urkabi

The first well-known disappearance was the village of [Urkabi](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/apporia/urkabi.md>), famous as the birthplace of [Samso](<../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/samso.md>) of the [Silver Tempests](<../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>), one of the heroes who defeated [Yeshara](<../../people/extraplanar-powers/yeshara.md>) and quieted [Cairn Dor](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/cairn-dor/cairn-dor.md>), and the only survivor of the attack on [Urkabi](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/apporia/urkabi.md>). In DR 1737, Samso returned home from a journey to find his village empty. The trees showed black rot, nearby streams were polluted, and a green sigil was fading at the site. 

Urkabi established the pattern later seen elsewhere: the people vanished, the local environment was harmed, and the sigil remained as a magical trace. At the time, Samso had no explanation. 

## Later Disappearances

Similar disappearances continued across the broad region during the following decade. By DR 1747, abandoned villages had been found west of [Voltara](<../../gazetteer/northwest-coast/northern-provinces/voltara/voltara.md>) and near [Lake Valandros](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/lake-valandros.md>). Although accounts varied, many described the same green sigil, damage to the surrounding land, and, in some cases, shadow-tainted creatures resembling the missing inhabitants. These common features led the disappearances to be recognized as a single phenomenon rather than a series of isolated events.

In DR 1746 and DR 1747, reports of a strange sleeping sickness in the [Kedron](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/apporia/kedron.md>) valley began to spread, connected to disappearances of others in the same household where the sleeping sickness took hold. This was later established to reflect a new phase of kidnappings, expanding beyond the original [Lizardfolk](<../../creatures/species/lizardfolk.md>) targets. 

## Cause and Resolution

In DR 1748, the [green sigil](<../../things/magic-items/cairn-dor-sigil.md>) was identified as a magical mark associated with [Yeshara](<../../people/extraplanar-powers/yeshara.md>), the [Shemra Azem](<../../groups/shemra-azem.md>), and [Cairn Dor](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/cairn-dor/cairn-dor.md>). The vanished villagers had been taken through the [Cleaver-Stone](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/apporia/cleaver-stone.md>) and held in enchanted sleep in Cairn Dor as "dreamers."

On September 20th, 1748, the [Silver Tempests](<../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>) severed the connection between the Cleaver-Stone and Cairn Dor, bringing the disappearances to an end. The company reached Cairn Dor the following month, defeated Yeshara, and freed the surviving captives. A magical stasis within Cairn Dor delayed their return to the Material Plane until June 1752.
