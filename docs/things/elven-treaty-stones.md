# Elven Treaty Stones
:octicons-info-24:{ .lg .middle } **(monument)**  

A variety of carved pillars of stone can be found scattered across [Greater Chardon](<../gazetteer/greater-chardon/greater-chardon.md>), the [Northwest Coast](<../gazetteer/northwest-coast/northwest-coast.md>), and the [Central Highlands](<../gazetteer/central-highlands/central-highlands.md>). These are known as Elven Treaty Stones, and are ancient markers associated with old agreements between [elves](<../creatures/species/elves.md>) and the [early human settlers](<../groups/cultures/northerners.md>) of these regions. Usually carved with extensive writings in Elvish, along with images and occasionally [Northos](<../background/languages.md#northros-language-family>), they typically mark meeting points and understandings of friendship. While many have fallen into disrepair, those that remain often still carry lingering traces of the ancient magic of [Aldanor](<../gods-and-religions/gods/embodied-gods/aldanor.md>) and provide areas of sanctuary and peace.

One such stone, 60 feet high and marking friendship between elves and humans, is known from the travels of the [Silver Tempests](<../people/pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>) in the [Blackwater Fens](<../gazetteer/northern-sentinels/blackwater-fens.md>). Another, known as the [Harmony Stone](<../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chasa-river-valley/harmony-stone-chasa.md>), is known in the [Chasa River Valley](<../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/chasa-river-valley/chasa-river-valley.md>).
