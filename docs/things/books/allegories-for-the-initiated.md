# Allegories for the Initiated
:octicons-info-24:{ .lg .middle } **A [Drankorian](<../../history/historical-realms/drankorian-empire.md>) book, written during the [Drankorian Era](<../../history/history-of-the-drankorian-empire.md>), by the [Arithrimos Lamperum](<../../groups/drankorian-societies/arithrimos-lamperum.md>)**  

*Allegories for the Initiated* is a collection of short fairy tales and riddles intended to reveal the deep secrets of the [Arithrimos Lamperum](<../../groups/drankorian-societies/arithrimos-lamperum.md>) to members who already understand their meanings.
