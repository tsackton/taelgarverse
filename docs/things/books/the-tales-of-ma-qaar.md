# The Tales of Ma'qaar
:speaker:{ .middle } *(mah-KAHR)*  
:octicons-info-24:{ .lg .middle } **A [Deno'qai](<../../groups/cultures/deno-qai-tribes/deno-qai.md>) epic poem**  

*The Tales of Ma'qaar* is a [Deno'qai](<../../groups/cultures/deno-qai-tribes/deno-qai.md>) epic ballad, collecting a series of tales and poems of strange timeless realms, presumed to be the Feywild, attributed to a possibly-mythical Deno'qai storyteller and bard. Ulfgar Frostbeard's *Fey Discourses* in the [The Lore of the Feywild](<the-lore-of-the-feywild.md>) preserves one of the only recorded versions, pieced together from the unpublished notes of [Eukos the Wanderer](<../../people/chardonians/eukos-the-wanderer.md>).
