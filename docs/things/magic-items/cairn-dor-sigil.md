# The Cairn Dor Sigil
:octicons-info-24:{ .lg .middle } **([Shadowfolds](<../../cosmology/demiplanes-and-echo-realms/shadowfolds.md>) symbol)**  

The Cairn Dor Sigil is a shimmering green mark associated with the disappearance of [Urkabi](<../../gazetteer/greater-chardon/chardonian-empire/apporia/urkabi.md>) and other [lizardfolk villages](<../../events/1700s/lizardfolk-village-disappearances.md>) in the DR 1730s and DR 1740s. It was often seen, shimmering sickly green, in the aftermath of the disappearance of a village. 

The source and cause was not discovered until much later, when the lizardfolk [Samso](<../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/samso.md>) and the [Silver Tempests](<../../people/pcs/silver-tempests/silver-tempests.md>) traveled to [Cairn Dor](<../../gazetteer/extraplanar/shadowfolds/cairn-dor/cairn-dor.md>) and discovered that the sigil was connected to the [Shemra Azem](<../../groups/shemra-azem.md>) and the sleepless queen of Carin Dor, [Yeshara](<../../people/extraplanar-powers/yeshara.md>).
